# [February 14] Stranded Small Patch

Hello,  
  
We just released a small stranded patch, featuring those changes:  


  * Fixed several instances of mobs not giving proper combat experience
  * Added Water Bucket trade for 10k coins as a last resort for players who lose their water buckets
  * Added Lava Bucket trade for 25k coins as a last resort for players who lose their lava buckets
  * Added Smithmonger NPC for reforge stones
  * Zombie and Endermen minions now change their skin if they have a Sea Lantern Hat/Summoning Eye in them (unless they have a minion skin already)
  * Increased the mob cap
  * Swapped George and Zog NPC spawning requirements
  * Dwarven Mines Sea Creatures now spawn by default. This adds two missing collections - Hard Stone and Nether Quartz.
  * Broodmother now has a rare chance to spawn under certain conditions
  * Fixed setting island to Deep Ocean biome several times causing Sea Creature Chance to stack
  * Fixed setting your island biome not setting every block within the island.
  * The Weaponsmith NPC now sells anvils for 1000 coins.
  * Fixed Builder NPC requiring unobtainable collections being unlocked.
  * Fixed Grinch dying on one hit during the Season of Jerry.

  
We also want to give a quick update regarding the Nether Revamp Alpha release schedule:  
We're planning to release the nether update to alpha in**two stages** , we will first re-open alpha to players with the nether island closed but all the items and collections viewable. Players will be able to see the new island but will NOT be able to access it. Then at a later point we will open the island to all players so that they can test the new content. We're doing this to make sure the base game is stable before we start performance testing the island. No ETA on when this will be but we will keep you guys updated!
