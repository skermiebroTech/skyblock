### **► The Abandoned Quarry**​

  


**

![abandoned_quarry.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/abandoned_quarry-png.3385742/)

**​

  
A new area, named the Abandoned Quarry, has been added to the Dwarven Mines! Players can travel to this area by talking to the Station Master, and can choose to either spend their time mining veins of Dwarven Quartz, which have a higher base yield than regular Quartz, or to cast their rods in the hopes of catching some Mithril Grubbers.  
  
Mithril Grubbers drop varying amounts of Mithril Power when killed, the amount varying with the size of the Grubber:  


  * Small (55% of Grubbers): 250 Mithril Powder
  * Medium (30% of Grubbers): 500 Mithril Powder
  * Large (10% of Grubbers): 1000 Mithril Powder
  * Bloated (5% of Grubbers): 5000 Mithril Powder

Powder drops from Grubbers are affected by your standard powder-interacting perks, such as drill parts, HOTM perks, and the 2x powder event.  
  
Also added to the game is the Speckled Teacup, a **GREAT CATCH!** tier of treasure that can be returned to its rightful owner, Brynmor, in exchange for his Abiphone contact.
