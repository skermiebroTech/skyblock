# [November 5] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the fixes rolled after 0.23.6 to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Dungeon Chest Bingo tasks being uncompletable.
  * Removed a leftover build structure from the Dungeon Hub map.
  * Fixed Griffin Upgrade Stones being unstackable.
    * We are working on refunding people who lost Griffin Stones from this issue.
  * Fixed Kuudra Shard amounts not being consistent.
  * Fixed being able to damage Shellwise (and others) with non-fishing-net items.
  * Fixed Aurora Armor's ability allowing firing missiles after death.
  * Fixed Enchanted Cooked Salmon missing from Enchanted Fishing Sack.
  * Fixed Wood Singularity not applying to Wood Splitter Axe.
  * Fixed Crown of Avarice gaining coins from non-Mythos mobs.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
