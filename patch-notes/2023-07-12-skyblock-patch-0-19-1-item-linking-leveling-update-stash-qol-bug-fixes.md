# SkyBlock Patch 0.19.1 - Item Linking, Leveling Update, Stash QoL, & Bug Fixes

![sb-19-1.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-19-1-png.3223450/)

  
  
Hey Guys!  
SkyBlock has been updated to version 0.19.1. New features in this patch are Item Linking and several Leveling updates, as well as many other changes and bug fixes.  
  
  
  
**►** **Item Linking**  
You can now show off your items in the chat! All you need is the Cookie Buff, and you're good to go.  
  
To link in chat, you just run /show!  


![item chat hover preview.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/item-chat-hover-preview-png.3223328/)

  
You can also use the following keywords to link a specific item in chat: hand, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, necklace, cloak, belt, gloves, bracelet, pet. You can use one of these keywords by running /show <keyword>  
  
When clicking on an item link in chat, you will see the following menu:  
  


![item preview menu.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/item-preview-menu-png.3223380/)

Inside this menu, you can see the following:  


  * Profile Viewer
    * Allows you to view the player's information
  * Trade Request
    * Sends a trade request detailing the specific item you are interested in
  * Worth Breakdown
    * Will show the worth of the item + a breakdown of all modifiers and their individual worth
  * Wiki Link
    * Sends a link to the [wiki ](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Main_Page)to view more information about that item
  * Similar Auctions
    * Will show the cheapest auction available for the item + the closest auction to the item shown
  * Extra Data
    * Will show extra information about the item, such as the NPC sell price, dungeon info, etc.
  * Item Modifiers
    * Shows all available modifiers that can and have been applied to the item

**► Emblems**  
Emblems are a new way to show off certain accomplishments you have earned across SkyBlock. There are a grand total of 80 emblems you can unlock currently, and more will be added in the future!  


  * You select an emblem inside the SkyBlock Leveling menu.
  * Emblems are unlocked through **Skills, Dungeons, Slaying, your SkyBlock Level, and Misc. Achievements.**
  * Your Selected Emblem will display next to your name, in the tab list, and in chat.
  * When hovering over your emblem in chat, players will see extra information based on the emblem you've selected.
  * Players can also see your emblems in your profile viewer.

**►** **Book of Progression**  
The Book of Progression is a new accessory unlocked at **SkyBlock Level 25** that upgrades as you level up! When you first unlock the book, it will be Common, and as you level up your SkyBlock Level, it will eventually upgrade to Mythic!  
  
The book also has passive bonuses that you will unlock. You can select one to infuse to the book to help you along the way. The percentages on each of these passives scale as your SkyBlock Level increases:  


  * **Stacked Healing**
    * When you are being healed, heal +0-5% of the amount.
  * **Defensive Strongman -**_unlocked at Level 50_
    * When attacked, take 0-5% less damage.
  * **Extra Infliction -**_unlocked at Level 75_
    * When dealing damage, deal +0-5% more damage.



![book of progression.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/book-of-progression-png.3223175/)

  
  
**►** **Stash QoL**  
You can now view your item or material stash in a menu by using /viewstash!  


  * Stash menus allow you to visualize your stash and take individual items
  * Inside the material stash, you can insta-sell items to the Bazaar or send items directly to your sacks

**►** **Other Changes**  
  
**Trades**  


  * Added a new highest visual coin tier.
  * Added a coin trade limit of 50 million coins per day.
    * This daily limit increases to 1b coins if you are SkyBlock level 50 or higher.
    * This daily limit increases to 10b coins if you are SkyBlock level 100 or higher.

**UI and Commands**  


  * Added `/auctionsearch` command (`/ahsearch`, `/ahs`).
  * Can now use suffixes "d", "h", and "m" to specify time in auctions (i.e. _6d17h5m_)

**Composter**  


  * Added list of all crops and fuel in inventory and sacks when hovering over menu icons.
  * Added two separate buttons for taking items from inventory and from sacks for each crops and fuel.
  * Added support for taking fuel from sacks.

**_Other_ other Changes**  


  * Can now put Sacks in Ender Chests and Backpacks.
  * Buffed the Roll' em perks of Melon and Pumpkin Dicers. The upgrades of these dicers have a chance to drop more items with their Roll' Em ability.
