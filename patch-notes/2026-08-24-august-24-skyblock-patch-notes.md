# [August 24] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock, containing further improvements to the [0.27 Release](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-27-torrhus-canyon-critter-safari.6132090/), as well as some more general fixes.  


  * Added the "Marshroom" reforge stone to Vinesap VIII, which can apply the "Marshy" reforge to Equipment
  * Added the "Timber" stat and unified Petalfall, Timber HOTF Perk, and Woodpecker Shard systems under it
  * Added the Timid Mob Type, which is applied to mobs that typically flee/hide from the player:
    * Blue Jay
    * Blue Jerry
    * Chuckwalla
    * Coralot
    * Diamond Goblin
    * Duplico
    * Goldolot
    * Golden Goblin
    * Golden Jerry
    * Green Jerry
    * Grinch
    * Hideonfloor
    * Hideonwall
    * Joydive
    * Nozzlenose
    * Pangolin
    * Purple Jerry
    * Rockmite
    * Scared Skeleton
    * Scrappy
    * Sepialot
    * Shyworm
    * Trapper Animals
  * Added all Pots of Honeycomb to the Bazaar
  * Added the Forest's Favor as a 0.02% drop chance from Helix Tree Gifts. Upgrades Karma V -> VI
  * Added Torrhus Sea Creature Shards to the Huntrap Pool:
    * Spring Shallows:
      * Solar Shard: 75 Weight
      * Sepialot Shard: 100 Weight
      * Goldolot Shard: 25 Weight
      * Water Snake Shard: 50 Weight
      * Haggard Shard **(NEW)** : 100 Weight
      * Brineling Shard **(NEW)** : 50 Weight
      * Sprawl Shard **(NEW)** : 25 Weight
    * Spring Depths:
      * Ember Shard: 50 Weight
      * Sepialot Shard: 100 Weight
      * Stingray Shard: 50 Weight
      * Goldolot Snake Shard: 25 Weight
      * Water Snake Shard: 50 Weight
      * Torrid Shard **(NEW)** : 100 Weight
      * Silkbreeze Shard **(NEW)** : 25 Weight
      * Giant Isopod Shard **(NEW)** : 10 Weight
  * Added Sea Brine as a drop to the Brineling:
    * Used to craft the Brine Tonic
      * Effect: Grants +2.5 Charm Chance on Moonglade Sea Creatures and Torrhus Sea Creatures.
  * Improved the itemlore of the Reinforced Netting and Sublime Silk
  * Improved the Sweep formula - Swoop's menu now reflects the new, more intuitive formula
  * Increased the scaling of "Two-for-one" HOTF perk from 0.1-10% -> 0.25% -> 25%
  * Increased the amount of Foraging XP given as part of Miria's Contest Rewards:
    * COMMON: 20,000 -> 15,000
    * UNCOMMON: 30,000 -> 30,000 (Unchanged)
    * RARE: 40,000 -> 45,000
    * EPIC: 50,000 -> 60,000
    * LEGENDARY: 60,000 -> 75,000
    * MYTHIC: 60,000 -> 75,000
    * SPECIAL: 60,000 -> 75,000
    * DIVINE: 60,000 -> 75,000
  * Adjusted HOTF XP Gain from Tree Gifts:
    * Fig: 10 -> 10 (Unchanged)
    * Mangrove: 15 -> 20
    * Helix: 20 -> 30
  * Adjusted Shards from Honeyhives to no longer be affected by Hunting Fortune
  * Adjusted HONEYCOMB! messages to now mention the general location of the tree that attracted a Critter
  * Adjusted all Pots of Honeycomb to now only have 1 charge to prevent Auction House scams:
    * Increased crafting output of Family-Sized Pot of Honeycomb from 1x -> 8x
    * Increased Enchanted Helix Logs needed to craft Family-Sized Pot of Honeycomb from 16x -> 32x
    * Increased crafting output of Jumbo Pot of Honeycomb from 1x -> 8x
    * Increased amount of Behemoth Pots of Honeycomb dropped by the Beeheemoth from 1x -> 8x
  * Adjusted Jumbo Pot of Honeycomb recipe to output 1x -> 8x of the item
  * Adjusted Stealth enchantment:
    * **Designer Note:** Now scales from I-V and caps out at 60% instead of 100% with its Endcap as we didn't feel that entirely bypassing the behavior of Timid mobs with 100% reliability was healthy. In exchange for this numerical nerf, it now applies to all mobs with the Timid mob type (Grinches, Golden/Diamond Goblins, etc).
  * Adjusted Pangolins to now only spawn within Pangolin Hideaways
  * Adjusted Lassos to now use paper as their base item instead of leads
  * Adjusted some sources of point gain for Miria's Contests:
    * Increased COMMON Shards from 3 -> 9
    * Increased UNCOMMON Shards from 6 -> 25
    * Increased RARE Shards from 12 -> 50
    * Increased EPIC Shards from 24 -> 100
    * Increased LEGENDARY Shards from 48 -> 200
    * Increased Per Catch from 4 -> 10
    * Increased Per Good Catch from 25 -> 50
    * Increased Per Great Catch from 75 -> 250
    * Increased Per Outstanding Catch from 250 -> 750
    * Increased Honeycomb from N/A -> 1
    * Reduced Per Mob Killed from 8 -> 4
    * Reduced per Ruby Veilshroom from 12 -> 8
    * Removed point gain from Sea Lumies
  * Adjusted Distant Echo to increase the level of Stealth enchantment instead of Karma
  * Adjusted Lassos to use the modern item.lead.break sound when breaking
  * Adjusted Fusion Recipe for Queen Ant Shard from Ant + Elusive Family -> Ant + Queen Bee
  * Adjusted Fusion Recipe for Special Zealot Shard from Zealot + Elusive Family -> Obtainable via Generic Fusion
  * Adjusted all Torrhus Sea Creatures to be Charm only
    * **Designer Note: **Charm-only shards are an important part of the Hunting ecosystem as they are the cornerstone through which the Charm Chance stat, Salts, and Elusive shard systems operate. We specifically chose to make Sea Creatures charm-only two reasons: to avoid disrupting the fishing gameplay loop AND because you need to kill them more times than any other mobs on the island (they have more drops that you need and their higher bestiaries are reflective of this)
  * Renamed Woodpecker's Attribute from Forest Felling → Helix Timber - now only affects Helix Trees
  * Removed Tree the Fish from Helix Tree Gifts and Torrhus Canyon /pity
  * Removed Mana costs from Fishing Nets
  * Fixed an issue causing Grizzly Bears to despawn earlier than intended
  * Fixed an issue causing the Fusion Machine to prioritize lower-tier Shards
  * Reduced Shellfruit drops to only be Cocoa Beans
  * Removed the "Elusive" Shard Family - all mobs with the Elusive Mob Type that CAN drop a Shard will ALWAYS drop it on kill
    * Sanger's dialogue has been updated to better explain this mechanic
  * Fixed the Attribute Menu and Hunting Box repeatedly setting you back to Page 1 and resetting filters
  * Fixed the Queen Ant Shard not working against Queen Ants
  * Fixed Beeheemoths not granting points towards Miria's Contests
  * Fixed Bunbun Bestiary using the incorrect icon
  * Fixed an issue causing players to be unable to Lasso mobs if standing too close
  * Fixed several Critter Bestiaries awarding Magic Find instead of Hunting Fortune
  * Fixed several Lotus Atoll Critters using the incorrect Bestiary Brackets
  * Fixed Melon armor mentioned still in descriptions.
  * Fixed Hocus Pocus cipher not being rift-transferrable.
  * Fixed reclaim only returning 1 Farming For Dummies.
  * Fixed Feast Milestones menu showing 0 progress for locked milestones.
  * Fixed Hoppity egg in Dwarven Mines saying "beside Aristocrat Passage".
  * Fixed Hoppity call shop not taking from bank.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
