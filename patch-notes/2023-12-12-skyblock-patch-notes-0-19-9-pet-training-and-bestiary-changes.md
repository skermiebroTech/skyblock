# SkyBlock Patch Notes 0.19.9 - Pet Training and Bestiary Changes

![sb-19-9-fin.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-19-9-fin-png.3273530/)

  
Hello! Welcome to **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.9.** Today's update includes Pet Care, some Bestiary improvements, new items, and more!  
  


### **►** The Pet Trainer​

![Hootie and Fann](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fann_and_hootie-png.3273515/)

  
_Hootie and Fann are the new duo in town!_ ​

  
While Kat allows you to upgrade your Pets to a higher rarity, Fann can train your Pets in her playground, giving them EXP!  
  


### How does it work?​

Start by choosing a training slot. Initially, you have access to one slot, but by treating Hootie and Fann’s Owl to candies, you unlock two additional slots.  
  
After selecting a slot, you can donate any pet you own, and then you have to select a few options:  


  * The Training session
    * Pick from six unique training modes, each offering increasing amounts of Pet EXP daily.
    * Higher-level training costs more.
    * The first training mode is free, and training sessions are unlocked every 10 Taming Levels.
    * The free training session gets better and better the higher your taming level, from 1000 exp per day to 5000!
  * Set the duration
    * Opt for a fixed period (5/10/20 days) or a target level (level 100, for example).
  * You can also use an Owl Pet to help in the training session!
    * Your Owl Pet also levels up while helping out in the training session.
  * You can use the Caducous Feeder on your pet while it is in a training session!



### The Owl Pet​

Fann’s passion for Owls shines through in her work. She also offers the Owl Pet for sale!  
  


![owl.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/owl-png.3273560/)

  
The more money you invest on Fann’s training sessions, the cheaper they will get.  
  
Also, you can always donate your Owl in any training sessions, and your cute Owl will jump in the playground with your Pet to help train it.  


### Taming 60​

George is a Pet collector facing a unique challenge: certain elusive pets elude his collection, as no one is willing to sell them to him.  
  
Once you reach Taming Level 50, George proposes a trade. By donating 10 unique Pets, you unlock additional Taming Levels, all the way up to 60.

### **►** Bestiary Improvements​

### New Features and Changes​

  * There is now a reminder if you have Bestiary Milestones that you have not claimed yet. This reminder shows up whenever you swap islands
    * You can toggle this reminder off in the Bestiary menu.



![Claim Reminders.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/claim-reminders-png.3273517/)

  * Kada Knight and Kada Pigman now have distinct Bestiary categories.
  * The amount of kills needed to max Bestiary has been adjusted for the following mobs:
    * Wither Skeleton dropped from 10,000 to 3,000.
    * Dungeon Minibosses except King Midas (Lost Adventurer, Angry Archaeologist, Shadow Assassin) dropped from 10,000 to 3,000 each.
    * King Midas dropped from 1,000 to 750.
    * Zealot dropped from 100,000 to 25,000.
    * Watcher and Obsidian Defender dropped from 25,000 to 10,000.
    * Berserker Imp dropped from 10,000 to 3,000.
    * Silverfish dropped from 40,000 to 10,000.
    * Fels and Zombie Knight dropped from 25,000 to 10,000.
    * Voracious Spider dropped from 40,000 to 10,000.
    * Spider Jockey dropped from 10,000 to 4,000.
  * Reindrake now rewards Bestiary progress to anyone who has damaged the drake at least once.
  * The amount of kills required to max each mob’s bestiary is hidden by default. You can toggle it on in the bestiary menu.



![Bestiary_Family_Toggle.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/bestiary_family_toggle-png.3273518/)

  * You can now click the center icon in the Bestiary menu to access the Hypixel Wiki Bestiary page.
    * A prompt will appear in chat. You will need to click that prompt to be redirected to the Hypixel Wiki.
  * Bestiary won't be available until you speak to Bramass Beastslayer.
    * Some players may have to interact again with Bramass Beastslayer to re-unlock their Bestiary.



### Stranded Bestiary​

We’ve officially added support to the Bestiary in Stranded! Only mobs that can spawn on Stranded will appear in Stranded’s bestiary. There are also some slight discrepancies in the amount of kills needed to max each mob’s bestiary between Stranded and non-Stranded.  
  
To celebrate this momentous occasion, we’ve also added a bunch of new spawn conditions while also revamping some of the existing spawn conditions. Check them out on the official Hypixel Wiki [here](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Stranded#Mobs)!

Malik now features the Secret Tracker 3000 in the shop. Right click the Secret Tracker 3000 to display a particle trail towards the nearest secret.
