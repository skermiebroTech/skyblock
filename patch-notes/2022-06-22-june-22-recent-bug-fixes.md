# [June 22] Recent Bug Fixes

Hey all!  
  
Over the last few weeks we have fixed an absolute boat load of bugs, however we've been incredibly busy so we have a big backlog of patch notes. That said, here's a big cumulative list of all of them!  
  
Bug Fixes  


  * Fixed Dungeon Master Mode 7 mage beams not dealing the correct damage to dragons
  * Fixed Vanquished Glowstone Gauntlet lore missing bonus information
  * Fixed certain scaling stat items from spamming chat when used
  * Fixed Sinseeker still working if the player has died during the process
  * Redstone fixes:
    * Fixed Redstone now always giving extra drops in Crystal Hollows
    * Fixed Redstone sometimes not working with the Compact Enchantment
    * Fixed Redstone sometimes not firing for the Refined reforge
  * Fixed Slayer Bosses regenerating health after they have already died
  * Fixed Pickonimbus durability not degrading
  * Fixed Drill rarity not updating in chat messages after being recombobulated
  * Fixed applying drill upgrades taking the entire stack
  * Fixed Smelting Touch disabling Farming Fortune
  * Fixed Juju Shortbow hitting 3 mobs on impact sometimes not working
  * Fixed Horseman's Candle being placeable in Stranded mode
  * Fixed Mercenary Armor set bonus not working in Stranded
  * Fixed Zealots not aggro-ing on Stranded
  * Refined reforge fixes:
    * Fixed Refined reforge giving Enchanted Ink Sacs when mining Lapis
    * Fixed Refined not dropping Enchanted Mithril when mining Mithril
    * Refined reforge now gives a chat message on activation like Compact and Pristine
  * Fixed emptying an empty gemstone sack sending an inaccurate message
  * Fixed the Suus NPC quest becoming impossible to complete after the first attempt
  * Fixed issues with translations in several Crystal Hollows messages
  * Fixed Maxwell reporting incorrect stats
  * Fixed a large variety of stat items reporting incorrect stats after the Crimson Isle update
  * Fixed Drill Engine Parts not showing their 2nd stat boost
  * Fixed several Mythic Flying Fish perks not working
  * Fixed Explosive Bow causing lag spikes and added a mob cap (like Gauntlet of Contagion)
  * Fixed taking more damage with Fervor armor instead of less damage
  * Fixed Crimson Armor doing damage to mobs on other players' islands
  * Terror Armor Fixes:
    * Fixed Terror armor giving stacks when attacking players/other creatures that players can't attack
    * Fixed Terror armor giving stacks on melee damage (should only be bows)
    * Improve the Terror armor lore to be more readable
  * Fixed Fire Aspect damage being affected by Rekindled Ember armor when it's not equipped
  * Fixed necromancy souls blocking their owner's arrows
  * Fixed drill fuel notifications always using the default amount when there's a fuel tank or something that gives it more fuel capacity
  * Fixed a method of getting negative drill fuel
  * Fixed "Ring Ring" Abiphone achievement, again...
  * Allow players to claim the "Scam?" achievement by clicking on Bruuh again, if they've paid
  * Extend the Crimson Isle Cathedral zone a bit to make one of the rescue entrances possible to reach
  * Improved Thunder in a Bottle logic and sources
  * Revamped some of the Crimson Isle Matriarch mechanics
  * Lava Fishing Fixes:
    * Fixed the Vanille Trophy Fish fillet reporting the wrong reward amounts
    * Fixed being able to obtain vanilla fishing drops when on the Crimson Isle
    * Fixed certain Lava Sea Creatures being able to attack you when wearing special armor sets
  * Fixed an issue where some users were not able to pick up Pheonix Pet drops from Magma Slugs
  * Fixed an issue where Goblins in the Dwarven Mines raid event would sometimes spawn in walls
  * Fixed Projectiles not correctly counting towards the entity limiter
  * Optimised animated hats
  * Players whose islands are marked as under investigation can now be sent to hubs upon subsequent joins
  * Nerfed the Livid Clone entity combat XP and fixed them teleporting into blocks
  * Fixed some entities not registering themselves correctly
    * This was namely in dungeons and fishing which should result in less invisible mobs or random zombies
  * Hungry Hiker:
    * Fixed the hiker disappearing when he shouldn't and getting people soft-locked out of the quest
    * Swapped out the Rabbit Stew item for 30x Cooked Chicken
    * Improved the internal handling of his data and code structure making him overall a more stable experience
  * Fixed second inferno apex not dropping from the minion
  * Fixed friendly faction tier giving 6 quests instead of 5
  * Fixed absorption hearts not scaling properly and sometimes covering the screen
  * Players are now warned when conflicting enchantments will override each other in anvils
  * Fixed rare cases where pets could get stuck in Minecarts
  * Fixed getting glitched Blindness potions when using an Enchanted Redstone Block
  * Fixed minion collection page showing some minions that don't exist and missing some minions that do exist
  * Fixed minions mining but not putting items in chest storage if their personal inventory is full and they have the "This location isn't perfect" text above them
  * Fixed a method of obtaining infinite Mining XP
  * Fixed Witherborn Armor Set attacking barbarian guards resulting in reputation being lost
  * Fixed many items not having a catacombs requirement
  * Fixed Wither Cloak using a lot of mana due to fire changes

  * Fixed Tier 12 Flower Minions missing their own texture
  * Fixed the top portal blocks of island portals not functioning
  * Fixed Melody's Hair working with the Thaumaturgist buffs even if you don't have it unlocked in the harp
  * Fixed parts of The Crypt in the SkyBlock Hub being called Coal Mine

  * Fixed Romero not accepting Tier 12 Flower Minions

  * Fixed Flaming Fists not doing correct damage

  * Fixed a bug where this thread displayed as April instead of June

QOL Additions  


  * The Teleport Pad cap has been increased to 28 after copious internal fixes and optimisations
  * The Rock Paper Shears social item now displays extra info after each round and always go to a final round with 2 players
  * Improved Private Island name filtering
  * Gift items will now give directly to the player's inventory/stash instead of dropping on the ground, although a cosmetic drop still shows on the ground when opened.
  * Updated the Dungeon Note system to use an Anvil UI to allow longer messages and improved its chat filter settings
  * Improved the Quick Craft recipe order for Thunder and Magma Lord equipment
  * Made the Personal Deletor work with minions
  * Improved Parrot Pet chat filtering
  * Merged the Skeletor and Skeletor Prime enemies in the Bestiary
  * Applying a new drill part now returns old ones if you have enough coins
  * You can now sell to Jax in the Hub

  
Many thanks for reporting all the bugs, we're doing our best to keep up to date and fix them as they are coming in.
