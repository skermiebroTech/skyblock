# [August 15] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed sometimes not being able to gain Combat EXP and Bestiary progress from killing mobs with abilities from weapons such as Hyperion.
  * Fixed "Entering Dungeons floor" Autopet rule not working.
  * Fixed being able to use Sinrecall Transmission in Jungle Temple (Crystal Hollows).
  * Fixed Midas Staff ability not showing correctly when used above blocks like carpet.
  * Fixed Midas Staff ability activating and taking Mana when no blocks are spawned and no damage can be dealt. This could happen when standing close to a wall. Now the ability doesn't activate and doesn't take any Mana. Keep in mind: An activated ability can still be stopped by blocks mid-way.
  * Sea Creatures now despawn when their "owner" disconnects or leaves the server. This fixes a bug where you could change profiles and earn Fishing EXP on a different profile.
  * The Potato and Carrot areas in the Barn now use all three Crystals to regenerate the Crops instead of just one. This will speed up the Crop regeneration.
  * The Nick Level setting now only appears in the Settings Menu when you're nicked.
  * Reworded Toxic Arrow Poison description to be more accurate.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
