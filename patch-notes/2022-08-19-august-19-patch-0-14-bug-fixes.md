# [August 19] Patch 0.14 Bug Fixes

We've been around the clock fixing a whole bunch of different things that popped up over the course of our maintenance!  
Here's a good list of what we've got:  
  


### **► Bug Fixes**​

  * Fixed Crimson Isle boundary issues
  * Fixed a potential wipe on Stranded
  * Fixed an invincibility bug caused by Mastiff armor
  * Fixed Crimson Isle armor appearing incorrectly in the Hex
  * Fixed non-dungeonized items requiring dungeon levels to use outside of the Catacombs
  * Fixed dungeon chest loot giving more stats than they should
  * Changed Jerry's Lost and Found to return Essence if lost in the Bazaar
  * Fixed a possible duplication bug with eggs
  * Fixed a possible duplication bug on Stranded islands
  * Fixed the Lifelong Contract achievement not rewarding
  * Fixed the name tag of Scared Skeletons appearing during the spawn animation
  * Fixed Fels' name tags being missing
  * Fixed displaying stats that a player doesn't have
  * Fixed Overload enchant not working
  * Fixed a Catacombs level bypass for buying and selling Essence
  * Fixed various missing enchants from the Bazaar
  * Fixed various unobtainable enchants being in the Bazaar
  * Fixed broken Dungeon items giving one of every stat
  * Fixed some small typos in the Hex, Stats, and Carpentry menus

  


### **► Other Changes**​

  * Added commas to Damage Holograms and Stats for greater readability.
    * _This feature may break some client-side modifications! Please check in vanilla before reporting bugs!_

  


### ******► Closing Notes******​

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](http://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs in this release!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
