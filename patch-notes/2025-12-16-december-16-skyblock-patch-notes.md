# [December 16] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Allowed triggering the Greenhouse Tutorial quest by giving Sam the Greenhouse Blueprint if it wasn't triggered when receiving the Blueprint from the Carpenter
  * Fixed grammar in Godseed, Thunderling and the tutorial garden chip
  * Doubled the effect of Farming Fortune on the new pests base drops
  * Added Greenhouse Skins to the Gem lore
  * Added proper requirement information on the Hydrocans in the SkyMart

  
We are still working on a fix for past Jacob's Contest rewards being inaccurate (either missing entirely, or extra rewards), which is why the rewards menu is currently disabled, and hope to have it out soon.  
  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
