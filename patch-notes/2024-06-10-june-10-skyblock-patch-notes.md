# [June 10] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest fixes that were just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Fixed titanium instantly respawning into mithril after being mined instead of turning to bedrock.
  * Fixed polished diorite in the Crystal Hollows respawning.
  * Fixed heat in the Magma Fields going no higher than 50.
  * As of 0.20.2, special ores, such as mithril and gemstones, now respawn even if the chunk they're in gets unloaded.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
