# [February 17] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Added the Hub Revamp City Project.
  * Delicate Enchantment no longer prevents breaking crops inside the Greenhouse.
  * Dying to the Void in the Garden now respawns you at the default spawn location.
  * Fixed Garden Chip redemption only showing 1 as quantity even when redeeming multiple at a time.
  * Fixed Mutations with Fermento as a requirement not showing their spreading conditions.
  * Halved Soggybud water siphon thresholds.
  * Changed the way the offline mutation grace period is calculated. It no longer stops mutations from growing even if they had enough water to survive. Instead, freshly grown mutations while offline will now only stop growing if they were about to dry out, effectively increasing how long they can grow while offline.
  * Fixed giving the Bachelor's Rose to Jerry. This should work on the Jerry on private islands, in the Hub during the winter event, and on the Winter Island.
  * Fixed Emissary Sisko and the Wizard not accepting the Bachelor's Rose.
  * Fixed crops in The Garden growing slower than expected after harvesting them.
  * Prevented cocooning for mobs that come from Greenhouse mutations.
  * Fixed Melon Dicers with Silk Touch not gaining any tool exp.
  * Fixed the tops of trees in the Barn plot (but outside of the Barn build) being unbreakable due to build height limit.

Update February 18th:  


  * Dying in any way in the Garden now respawns you at the default spawn location.
  * Fixed TNT being able to break the Barn plot.
  * Fixed Greenhouse blocks being left behind when changing to a different plot type sometimes.

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
