# [January 22] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  
Changelog:  


  * Updated the Dark Auction mode to have more worlds per server- this should mean roughly 3x the amount of Dark Auction instances open per auction cycle.
  * Increased the minimum price for most Dark Auction products to ensure players weren't _colluding_ to get them cheap.
  * Changed how Beth generates rewards when she visits The Garden to balance with how often she spawns for her quest.
