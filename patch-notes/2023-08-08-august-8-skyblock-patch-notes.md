# [August 8] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Fixed very high Fishing Speed causing long fishing time under certain conditions.
  * Fixed Experimentation Table _still_ having duplicate Experience Bottle pairs.
  * Sinseeker Scythe's ability has been enabled. There was a small change to prevent multiple exploits with it: The ability now cancels when the player teleports due to some other reason.
  * Fixed blocks from Midas Staff blocking arrows.
  * Fixed Dungeon party finder not changing floor setting again when trying to queue.
  * Improved some wording in Melody's menu.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
