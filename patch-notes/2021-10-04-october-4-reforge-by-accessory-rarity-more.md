# [October 4] Reforge by accessory rarity + more

Hello!  
  
**Features**  


  * You may now filter by rarity when you reforge your accessory bag. This feature also works for the advanced reforge using reforge stones:



![1633370748816.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1633370748816-png.2763877/)

  * Reforge stones will now show a preview of the legendary stats of the associated reforge:



![1633370889088.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1633370889088-png.2763882/)

  * When opening a sack from the Sack of Sacks using right-click, there will now be a Go Back button:



![1633370953538.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1633370953538-png.2763883/)

  * Sirius heard rumors from the SkyBlock underworld and decided to offer the new Nether Artifact in the Dark Auction:



![1633371120156.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1633371120156-png.2763894/)

  
**Balance-related Bugfixes**  


  * Fixed power orbs priority. The plasmaflux buff will now correctly be applied before radiant.
  * Fixed Last Stand enchant to grant % defense instead of flat defense.
  * Fixed Gloomlock Grimoire Extreme Measures debuff lasting longer than it was supposed to.

  
**Other Bugfixes**  


  * "It Never Ends" achievement now procs on Ornate & Florid Zombie Swords.
  * Fixed sponge/shark armor stacking.
  * Fixed some damage reduction talismans stacking. (zombie, spider, ender, wither, wolf)
  * Fixed a typo in the lore of Spirit Assistance
  * Actually fixed deleting pending co-op invites using /coopadd:



![1633371291224.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1633371291224-png.2763897/)

  
That's it!
