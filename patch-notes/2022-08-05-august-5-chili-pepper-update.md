# [August 5] Chili Pepper Update

Hey!  
  
Gabagool price landed a lot higher than we predicted, excluding a lot of players from this economic niche.  
As a solution, we've reduced the RNG element of running Hypergolic fuel by introducing a new Combat collection: **Chili Pepper**!  
There are also some general changes to Blaze Slayer to make it a slightly less infernal experience.  
  
**General**  


  * Kelvin Inverter now has a 100k NPC price
  * Blazetekk Radio range increased to 55 blocks
  * Sorrow Armor changed:



![1659555955013.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1659555955013-png.3055695/)

  
Now 50/80/70/40 True Def, but with a 2-Piece Set Bonus​

  
**Wisp Pet**  


  * Increased Blaze Combat XP by 10% to 30/40/45/50% per rarity
  * Increased True Defense by 5
  * Renamed Coldfusion Perk to Ephemeral Stability

  
**Inferno Minion**  


  * Now gets (the same) max Inferno buff at 10 minions, meaning you need less of them!
  * Drop rate for Hypergolic unique drops increased by 10%
  * Hypergolic Fuels now commonly drop Chili Pepper.
  * Expired Hypergolic fuels now always drop a Hypergolic Ionized Ceramics. Combine 8 into an Inferno Vertex.
    * ****Make sure to use an sc3k on your minion, as it drops on expiry and your minion might be full!****



![1659556382869.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1659556382869-png.3055696/)

  
Guaranteed Vertexes using Science​

  
**Chili Pepper**  
  
Chili Peppers are a new item which drop somewhat frequently from Inferno Minions running Hypergolic Fuel.  
You should get between 5 and 30 per minion per day, depending on how many minions you have on your island.  
It's not required to be able to kill the Inferno Demonlord in order to run Inferno Minions.
