# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.26 - SkyBlock Resource Pack, Loadouts, Security Quest, and more!

![banner.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/banner-png.3589366/)

  
​

Hello! Today we released update 0.26 to SkyBlock. This update includes the long-awaited SkyBlock Resource Pack, a Loadouts system for easier storage of your gear, and a new quest that teaches you how to keep your account secure. _With this release we have also dropped support for Minecraft 1.21.9 and 1.21.10_. We recommend using Minecraft 26.2 for the best experience.

►** **SkyBlock Resource Pack**  
  


![gallery.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/gallery-png.3589242/)

  
With today's update we are releasing the first version of the SkyBlock Resource Pack. This initial version of the pack contains 800+ textures, with sprites for most of the weapons and tools in the game along with plenty of other miscellaneous items.  
  
The pack is force-enabled, you will not be able to join SkyBlock without loading it.  
  


### **➥ License & Community Support**​

As mentioned in our [FAQ on the announcement post](https://hypixel.net/threads/skyblocks-7th-anniversary-skyblock-resource-pack-news.6108272/), we intend to continue providing support for community-made packs, including any pack that reverts textures back to their old states. In our eyes, the SkyBlock Resource Pack is how we want the base SkyBlock experience to feel. However, we know that many players prefer to customize their experience, so we want to make it easy for community resource packs to thrive.  
  
As our way of giving back to the community, we are choosing to make it as easy as possible for anyone to download and extract the SkyBlock Resource Pack. We have not obfuscated the names of textures or corrupted the files in any way, and the assets will be easily browsable by everyone. You can download the latest version of the resource pack at any point from the [Hypixel API](https://api.hypixel.net/v2/resources/packs) without needing to browse through your game files.  
  
Within the pack, you can find a file named LICENSE, which details what you can and cannot do with our assets. We have chosen to be very permissive as a way of saying thank you, and we're excited to see what you all create with it!  
  


### **➥ SkyBlock Resource Pack FAQ** ​

#### **< item> has an underwhelming texture. Is the team open to feedback?**​

Our textures are always subject to change based on feedback. If you don't like a particular texture, please tell us which ones you don't like and more importantly, why you dislike them! The art team is always looking for feedback to help steer them in the right direction, but the best feedback tells us which specific items you have problems with.  
  
Since the initial version of item textures were released on Alpha, we have changed over 100 individual textures (and sometimes we changed a texture multiple times) based on this feedback. We will continue to make changes in the coming days, weeks, and months. What you see in-game right now is not necessarily the final version for any given item.  
  


#### **When will <item> get a custom texture made for it?**​

This is just the first version of this resource pack; the goal is for most items in SkyBlock to eventually get a texture. We expect to update the SkyBlock Resource Pack quite frequently.  
  
Things like armor and pets are all things we would like to tackle in the future, but will require more time and care to get right. We are starting with more basic items and weapons for texturing first, as they are what you see in your hotbar and menus the most often.  
  


#### **Why are you not supporting a way to disable the pack?** ​

While we realize some players may not like the official textures, we would rather players apply any community-made packs on top of our pack as a base, than have them walking around with null textures and/or broken models after each update. Unfortunately, vanilla Minecraft does not provide a way for us to load server resource packs beneath community packs. So, until this is changed, we believe the vanilla customization experience falls short of what existing community mods can provide.  
  


#### **Why can’t you provide a Lite version of the pack that omits all of the “optional” features?** ​

If we were to provide a “lite” version of the SkyBlock Resource Pack, we would greatly complicate our production processes in many ways. A lite resource pack is essentially a second version of the resource pack that we have to maintain. By supporting multiple variants of the pack, we’d have to continue to create skull textures for the “vanilla” version of the game, or create alternative solutions to new content that ensures the game is still up to our standards for lite resource pack users. By having a single pack, our designers and artists know they only need to provide 1 item sprite/texture per item. Not only this, but our entire production team knows that SkyBlock now uses a resource pack, and can design and implement content with that reality in mind.  
  
While we hope that many players use and enjoy the base SkyBlock Resource Pack, we recognize that some players might hope to maintain the “OG” look of the game. For those players, we feel that community-made resource packs are the best solution.  
  
  
**►** **Loadouts**  
  
Over the course of a normal SkyBlock day, you probably switch armor more than once. One moment you're in full combat gear, the next you're mining or fishing with a completely different pet, armor set, equipment set and accessory setup. Until now, putting that together meant hopping between various menus every single time.  
  
With Loadouts, that whole process becomes one click. Edit your setup, save it, and you're pretty much good to go.  


  


![Loadouts.gif](https://hypixel.net/attachments/loadouts-gif.3589204/)

### **➥ What a Loadout saves** ​

Each Loadout allows you to save the following things:  


  * Armor, all 4 pieces
  * Equipment, your Necklace, Cloak, Belt and Gloves
  * Pet, whatever pet you select
  * Accessory Power, your selected Power Stone for the Accessory Bag
  * Tuning Points, your Magical Power tuning distribution
  * Heart of the Mountain
  * Heart of the Forest

  


### **➥ How it works** ​

  * Open the new Loadouts menu (replacing the Wardrobe menu in your SkyBlock Menu, or /loadout)
  * Right-click one of the Loadout slots to edit it
  * Here you can begin customizing your loadout. For example, click the Armor slots which opens the Wardrobe
  * Select one of your Wardrobe slots to save to your Loadout
  * You now have connected that Wardrobe slot your Loadout which means that when you select the Loadout the next time, it will always load that specific Wardrobe slot
  * You can add more things to your Loadout, for example your Equipment which opens the new Equipment Wardrobe (/eq)
  * Then rename the loadout to whatever you want so you can remember it better

### **➥ Equipment Wardrobe** ​

As part of this update, we have also added a Equipment Wardrobe. The new Equipment Wardrobe works the same as your current Wardrobe, but it saves your Equipment seperately from it. This way allows you to use your Equipment sets with any of your Armor sets without locking yourself to one specific composition.  
  


### **➥ HotM/HotF Loadouts** ​

We also added Loadouts for your Heart of the Mountain and Heart of the Forest, allowing you to swap between different setups really easily.  
  


### **➥ Additional Loadout Changes** ​

  * Increased the default amount of wardrobe slots from 2 -> 4
  * Made the first tuning template free, refunding everyone that spent bits on it previously
  * Added Community Upgrades for Loadouts, Hotf, Hotm and Equipment Slots
  * Removed "Full Set First Worn" line from the wardrobe

  
We've also made the following changes since Alpha:  


  * Added an additional free HotM/HotF Slot
  * Increased the cooldown of HotM/HotF swaps from 30 seconds to 10 minutes



> _Designer's Note_ : We have previously set this low at first, but we've noticed some issues a shorter swap cooldown could cause. We want to avoid the concept of swapping your tree often for minor reasons, for example in and out of mineshafts, which is why we are scaling back to a more realistic value.

A message from our Community Manager, Judge:  
  


![pfp.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/pfp-png.3589244/)

Hello! Today we're introducing a Security Quest alongside the 0.26 update.  
  
This quest is part of our ongoing efforts towards preventing players from losing their accounts. With this quest, we want to provide information and raise awareness regarding account security and scams to our players.

**►** **Miscellaneous**  


### **➥ Changes** ​

  * All enchantments are now removable via the enchanting menu, even if they cannot be acquired from an Enchanting Table
  * Updated Summoning Eye sound effects
  * Reworked Golden Dragon Gold's Power perk to buff Midas items' Greed ability
    * Because of this change, we have buffed Ragnarock by +30 Strength



> **_Designer's Note_ : **With the introduction of the resource pack, we had to make an adjustment to Golden Dragon's "Gold's Power" perk, as it's no longer apparent what a "gold" item is. The original perk had to change for two reasons:  
> 
> 
>   * The art for items affected by the perk should not be restricted to the color gold just for the sake of this perk.
>   * Conversely, it doesn't make sense to mark an item as "golden" if it doesn't _look_ golden. The perk's design is inherently not healthy for the design of the game, many times we add an item and forget it's affected by the perk due to it being gold.
> 
With the perk change, we addressed two relevant use cases for the perk, and added a new one:  
> 
> 
>   * Midas' Sword
>     * The change to Midas' Sword is largely inconsequential compared to the previous +100 buff. The sword now deals _slightly_ (~0.5%) less damage outside of Dungeons.
>     * Directly comparing with Dark Claymore: the latter has +100 CD outright, it's clear that Dark Claymore is still miles ahead.
>   * Midas' Staff
>     * The Midas' Staff buff is just an added bonus.
>   * Ragnarock
>     * Ragnarock's base Strength was buffed by only +30 as the item is inherently very strong, so we're reluctant to retain the item's previous strength. It's an awkward time for a balancing change, but it shouldn't be consequential in the long run.
