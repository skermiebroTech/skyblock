# [November 6] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Fixed Crimson Armor Swipe ability causing duplicate kill rewards to be given.
    * We have identified an issue with this fix: Swipe does not damage the entity that was hit by the player. A fix for this is rolling, but will take a while until it's deployed on every server.
  * Fixed Reflection enchantment not dealing damage when taking damage from an arrow that only deals absorption heart damage.
  * Fixed Looting enchantment not working on rods.
  * Fixed Luck enchantment not working on Phantom Rod.
  * Fixed Chance enchantment not working on Enderman kills.
  * Fixed Soul Eater enchantment not working on Slayer mini bosses.
  * Fixed Overload enchantment not working on Bonemerangs.
  * Fixed Bat pet Nightmare perk not being active under specific circumstances.
  * Fixed Rats never spawning on specific servers "randomly".
    * Due to the already long respawn time for Rats, we have decided to remove the time of day restriction.
  * Fixed Slayer boss spawning problems when the last mob to kill is dying to the void.
  * Fixed Slayer boss spawning problems when the last mob to kill is killed while applying a lot of knockback to it.
  * Fixed Slimes sometimes being able to deal damage for a short time after they have died.
  * Fixed Slimes never changing direction when they have no target.
  * Fixed some visual issues with blocks from the Yeti Sword's ability.
  * Fixed Mithril Gourmand 1 minute timer message appearing unintentionally.
  * Fixed Parrot pet repeating not respecting /myfilter.
  * Fixed Superboom ineffective message being shown outside Dungeons.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
