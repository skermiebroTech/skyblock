### **➥ Resource Pack FAQ** ​

**What Minecraft version do I need to be on?**  
You must be on at least Minecraft version 1.21.11 or higher in order to join the Alpha Network.  
  
With the release of Minecraft 26.2 recently, we are dropping support for versions 1.21.9 and 1.21.10. Please update soon if you haven't already. In a few weeks' time, we'll also drop support for 1.21.11, so we recommend players migrate to 26.1 or 26.2 instead.  
  
  
**Am I required to use the resource pack?**  
Yes, once the pack is added to the Main Network, it will be force-enabled there. Many pieces of future content coming soon will use custom models, textures, and menus. It would not be feasible for us to support an entirely different version of the game that didn't use the resource pack.  
  
If there are any textures for specific items that you aren't a fan of, please leave your feedback below as always.  
  
  
**Am I allowed to override the SkyBlock Resource Pack with mods?**  
Yes, although please keep in mind that all mods are considered use-at-your-own-risk. Additionally, bear in mind that a significant portion of items in SkyBlock have been turned into vanilla Paper items on the backend to help prevent placing animations/exploits, so doing this will make items indistinguishable from each other.  
  
  
**When will** **< item> get a custom texture made for it?**  
This is just the first version of this resource pack; the goal is for most items in SkyBlock to eventually get a texture. We expect to update the SkyBlock Resource Pack quite frequently.  
  
Things like armor and pets are all things we would like to tackle in the future, but will require more time and care to get right. We are starting with more basic items and weapons for texturing first, as they are what you see in your hotbar and menus the most often.

**Will future updates come out with textured items in the SkyBlock Resource Pack?**  
Going forward, yes, all new items except for Pets and Cosmetics should get textures on release. A few new items might slip through the cracks for the first month, but by and large, everything should be getting custom textures.  
  
  
**Where are the custom menus that were shown off a couple of years ago?**  
Over the past several months, we've been rolling code to the game to allow us to properly implement custom menus, but the menus themselves will not be included in the first release. This first release will help us iron out style and stomp out any bugs. These textures are just the first steps on the road!

**► Loadouts**  
  
Switching builds is one of the clunkiest things you do in SkyBlock. Going from your combat setup to a fishing or mining one means bouncing between the Wardrobe for armor, the Pets menu for your pet, and the Accessory Bag to swap your Power and even your Magical Power tuning. Everytime you want to swap from one activity to another, there are bunch of menus that you have to go through before you can just start playing.  
  
Loadouts lets you save a full build once and bring it back whenever you want with a single click.

### **➥ What a Loadout saves** ​

Each Loadout allows you to save the following things:  


  * Armor, all 4 pieces
  * Equipment, your Necklace, Cloak, Belt and Gloves
  * Pet, whatever pet you select
  * Accessory Power, your selected Power Stone for the Accessory Bag
  * Tuning Points, your Magical Power tuning distribution
  * Heart of the Mountain
  * Heart of the Forest

  
The way it works is that your Loadout talks to the other systems in the game, like your Wardrobe, Tuning Templates, Pets, Equipment Wardrobe (new) and Hotf/Hotf Slots (new) and loads the stored data that you have already configured there. You select multiple of these setups and connect them to one loadout. This means that most things are not stored within the loadout itself, but in their seperate instances instead. This also allows you to use your Wardrobe, Equipment Wardrobe or Hotm/Hotf Loadouts seperately from the Loadout system aswell.  
  


### **➥ How it works** ​

  * Open the new Loadouts menu and edit a loadout (replacing the Wardrobe menu in your Skyblock Menu, or /loadout)
  * Here you can select for example your Armor which opens the Wardrobe
  * Select one of your Wardrobe slots to save to your Loadout (which remain the same as they were before the update)
  * You now have connected that Wardrobe slot your Loadout which means that when you load the Loadout the next time, it will always load that specific Wardrobe slot
  * You can add more things to your Loadout, for example your Equipment which opens the new Equipment Wardrobe (/eq)
  * Then rename the loadout to whatever you want so you can remember it better

  
And that's basically it. From now on everytime you load that loadout, it will load your Armor from Wardrobe and load your Equipment from the Equipment Wardrobe, or whatever else you might have added to it.  
P.S: You can also choose to select nothing, which keeps whatever you had before the swap!  
  


### **➥ Equipment Wardrobe** ​

As part of this update, we have also added a Equipment Wardrobe. The new Equipment Wardrobe works the same as your current Wardrobe, but it saves your Equipment seperately from it. This way allows you to use your Equipment sets with any of your Armor sets without locking yourself to one specific composition.  
  


### **➥ HotM/HotF Loadouts** ​

We also added Loadouts for your Heart of the Mountain and Heart of the Forest, allowing you to swap between different setups really easily.

### **➥ Planned Adjustments** ​

  * Dungeon class changes, including:
    * Tank
      * Tank gets 10 + 0.5 Vitality per Class Level as part of its base stats.
    * Healer
      * Healer gets 10 + 0.2 Vitality per Class Level as part of its base stats.
      * Healer gets 10 + 0.5 Mending per Class Level as part of its base stats.
      * Mending also boosts the self-healing of Healers while in The Catacombs.
    * Berserk
      * Increased the melee damage boost from +0.4% per level to +0.75% per level.
      * Melee Hits increase the damage bonus from "Lust for Blood" 5x faster.
    * Mage
      * Mage now gets 5 Ability Damage + 1 Ability Damage every 5 levels as part of its base stats.
  * Some HP% changes:
    * Fire Traps: 50% HP Damage → 15% HP Damage
    * Arrow Traps: 50% HP Damage → 15% HP Damage
    * Crusher: 100% HP Damage → 30% HP Damage
    * Lava Damage (applicable to F7, M7):
      * F7: 40% HP → 8,000 Regular Damage
      * M7: 40% HP → 60,000 Regular Damage
  * Crimson Isle Miniboss Adjustments
    * Lowered kill-credit requirement from 1,000,000 to 250,000 damage.
    * Ashfang's Gravity Orb now deals 7,500 damage per second instead of 1-shotting the player.
    * Ashfang now heals 500,000 per absorbed Blaze instead of 2,000,000.
    * Ashfang's Blaze HP will be reduced to 2,000,000 HP.
