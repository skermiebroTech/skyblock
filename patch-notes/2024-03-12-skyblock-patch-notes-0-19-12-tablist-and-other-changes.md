# SkyBlock Patch Notes 0.19.12 - Tablist and Other Changes

![sb-19-12v2.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-19-12v2-png.3298313/)

  
  
Hello!  
  
Welcome to **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.12**. Today's update includes the long-awaited Tablist update, a new dungeon accessory in the General’s Medallion, various pet changes and buffs, a bunch of Garden and Pests QoL, and more!  
  
Continue reading for more details.  
  
  
  
****►** Status Update**  
The Garden was disabled for ~6 hours upon the release of the update as we encountered an unforeseen issue that affected Garden Milestones and some other features. We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.  
  


### **►** Tablist Widgets​

Ever felt the need to tweak what shows up on your tablist? Well, now you can! We’ve added a new setting that allows you to rearrange your tablist based on your own preferences depending on which island you are currently on using Widgets.  
  


![end-widgets.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/end-widgets-png.3298102/)

  
The tablist is now made of Widgets, each Widget revolving around one particular piece of content with ways to customize the content and appearance. Want quick access to your bank info? There's a Widget for that. How about keeping tabs on your Bestiary progress? You got it. You have the option to select which Widget you want to display and further customize how you want this information to be portrayed.  
  


![essence-widget.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/essence-widget-png.3298103/)

  
On top of that, we’ve added a bunch of new Widgets that you can display on the tablist from what you’ve been normally used to. For example, you can now track your Bestiary kills, view timers for Dark Auction or Experimentations, show additional Trophy Fish info, and much, much more.

### **►** General’s Medallion​

Some keen-eyed players may have noticed an unobtainable accessory was added to the game. With this update, General’s Medallion is now up for grabs!  
  
General’s Medallion is an accessory that gives you an extra stat boost while in dungeons. You will gain a 1% stat boost for each digit on your secret count. This stat boost is additive with your dungeon stat boost from increasing your catacombs level.  
  
General’s Medallion can be obtained by purchasing it from Zodd, who resides in a room that’s not been seen before.  
  


![medallion-shop.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/medallion-shop-png.3298109/)

  


### ➥ Secret Count Changes​

We have been tracking the amount of secrets you’ve obtained on the player itself instead of per profile for the past four years. As this wouldn’t be ideal for the accessory, we’ve utilized some dark magic to split up your secrets into portions and assigned them to all eligible profiles. The _ratio_ of how your secrets are split between your profiles is based on the amount of secret bat kills you’ve had. Secrets will be tracked on the player’s profile instead of on the player itself going forward.  
  
With this change, you can now check your secret count through the /secretcount command!  
  


![secret-count.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/secret-count-png.3298111/)

### ➥ Architect’s First Draft​

Architect’s First Draft is a new secret item you can obtain in Dungeons. You no longer have to fear your teammates failing Tic Tac Toe, as you can now reset failed dungeon puzzles with the Architect’s First Draft!  
  


![first-draft.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/first-draft-png.3298112/)

  
The drop rate of the Architect’s First Draft is the same as a Treasure Talisman.  
  
  


### **►** Garden QoL​

  


![portable-washer.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/portable-washer-png.3298113/)

  
Pests have been a solid addition to the Garden and helped make Farming more active while still maintaining player agency through features such as Bonus Pest Chance and Pest Repellent. This small update to the Garden focuses on further improving the pests gameplay loop while also cleaning up certain areas of the Garden, such as the SkyMart menu.  
  


### ➥ General Garden Changes​

  * Visitors will now spawn at 15-minute intervals while the player is not in the Garden, up to a maximum of 5 visitors.
  * Added a ‘Bulk Claim’ button to Jacob’s contest rewards menu.
  * Added the ‘Swift Slicer’ ability to the Cactus Knife, which increases the player’s speed cap by 100 while in the Garden.
  * Cacti can no longer destroy items.
  * Some dialogue has been added to contests affected by Finnegan’s GOATed perk to make players aware of the more lenient rankings.
  * Finishing in the Platinum bracket in a Farming Contest will now award 70 Bits.
  * Finishing in the Diamond bracket in a Farming Contest will now award 80 Bits.
  * Separated the SkyMart Menu into 3 distinct sections (Farming Essentials, Barn Skins, and Pests).
  * The Garden Desk is now accessible through the SkyBlock Menu.
  * The player’s Strength is once again shown in TAB when Mushroom Cow is equipped.

  


### ➥ Pest Changes​

#### **Pest Mechanics** ​

  * Pests now spawn a lot more frequently than they did before.
    * The cooldown of 5 minutes before spawning another pest (2.5 minutes with a spray active) has not changed.
    * This change affects the chance of getting a pest after the aforementioned cooldown is over.
  * Pests can no longer leave the plot they spawn in.
  * Pests will now always spawn on the highest Y level.
  * Pests will now spawn at an hourly rate while the player is not in the Garden, up to a maximum of 8.
  * Pests will no longer spawn in plots that haven’t been unlocked.
  * Earthworms may no longer go down on the Y axis when burrowing.
  * Moths are now attracted to lamps (glowstone blocks and beacons)!
  * Players may now vacuum pests through blocks, and the range of all vacuums has been increased to 15 blocks.
  * Killing a Pest now awards 5 Bits if the player has a Booster Cookie active.

### **►** Pet Buffs and Reworks​

It’s time for some changes to pets that have typically been underutilized! Not all of the pets involved in this update are underutilized, but all of the changes should make the pets better. Many of these changes were submitted and/or adapted based off of feedback from SkyBlock Nerds.  
  
Quick note that the all damage buffs listed below are additive damage modifiers unless specified to be multiplicative with a “x” indicator. (e.g 1.2x the amount of damage).  
  


### ➥ Ghoul Pet​

#### **Base Stats** ​

  * The Vitality from “Amplified Healing” should become a pet stat instead. The “Undead Slayer” perk (see below) should replace “Amplified Healing”.
  * Add Mending to the pet’s base stats equal to the amount of vitality it has.



#### **Undead Slayer (Replacement of “Amplified Healing”)** ​

  * Gain 1.005x ➜ 1.5x Combat XP against Zombies.



#### **Reaper Soul (Rework)** ​

  * Reaper Soul reduces the summoning cost of mobs by 0.3% ➜ 30% and increases their damage output by 0.2% ➜ 20%.
  * Reword the part about health of summoned mobs to “Increases the health of all summoned mobs by 1 ➜ 100%”.



#### **Army of the Dead (Replacement of “Zombie Arm”)** ​

  * Increase the amount of souls you can store and summon by 2. Also increases the chance of getting a mob’s soul by 0 ➜ 20%.



### ➥ Giraffe Pet​

#### **Base Stats** ​

  * Add +0.01 ➜ +1 Swing Range to the pet’s Base Stats.



#### **Good Heart (Rework)** ​

  * Gain +x Health Regen.
    * Common: 0.015% ➜ 1.5%
    * Uncommon, Rare: 0.02% ➜ 2%
    * Epic, Legendary: 2 ➜ 200



#### **Long Neck (Rework)** ​

  * Increases your melee damage by 50% ➜ 100% if you are more than 3 blocks away from the target.



#### **Higher Ground (Rework)** ​

  * Increase your Crit Damage and Strength by 0.0015% ➜ 0.15% for every 0.1 Swing Range over 3. Capped at 6 Swing Range.
    * There are many ways you can gain swing range and figure out how to best utilize the Giraffe Pet. You can easily reach the 6 Swing Range cap while in dungeons as Berserker, or cobble together an interesting combination of items in the overworld to reach the 6 Swing Range cap. Hopefully this opens up more options to players and enjoy theorycrafting what works best!



### **➥ Golem Pet**​

#### **Base Stats** ​

  * Add +0.01 ➜ 1 Swing Range to the pet’s base stats.



#### **Last Stand (Rework)** ​

  * While at less than 20% HP, reduce incoming damage by 20%. Additionally, gain a temporary shield equal to 40% of your maximum health and deal 40% more damage. (Lasts 12 seconds, 60s cooldown).



#### **Toss (Buff)** ​

  * Toss deals 5x damage per hit. Cooldown reduced to 5s.



### **➥ Hound Pet**​

#### **Base Stats** ​

  * Change the Attack Speed stat to +0 ➜ +25 Attack Speed.



#### **Pack Slayer (Replacement of “Fury Claws”)** ​

  * Gain 1.005x ➜ 1.5x Combat XP against Wolves.



#### **Finder (Rework)** ​

  * Change the scaling of the perk to 0.3% ➜ 30% chance increase to find Armor.

### **➥ Megalodon Pet**​

  * Add +5 ➜ +10 Ferocity to the pet’s base stats.
  * Add +0 ➜ 40 Fishing Speed to the pet’s base stats.
  * Add 0 ➜ 10 Sea Creature Chance to the pet’s base stats.



#### **Blood Scent (Rework)** ​

### **►** **Other Changes** ​

  * The quiver arrow system has been reworked to improve visibility and fix some annoying issues:
    * The quiver arrow item in the inventory is now a client-side only item and therefore should no longer randomly wipe items that were in that slot (e.g. during the Thorn boss-fight).
    * The quiver arrow item in your inventory has been updated to show which arrow is being used and how many are left in your quiver.
    * The quiver will now prioritise the strongest arrow in your quiver instead of the first available in the slot if you haven't selected a specific type using the arrow swapper.
    * Several situations in which the quiver arrow didn't update or randomly disappeared after a shot have now been fixed.
  * Arachne Fangs and Arachne Fragments have been added to the Bazaar.
  * Adjusted the drawbacks from using regular Ender Pearls in The End:
    * There is now a warning message after using your first Ender Pearl.
    * Subsequent Ender Pearls thrown within 30 seconds will spawn 1 aggressive Enderman and deal 10% true damage.
    * Nearby Endermen will no longer aggro when using Ender Pearls.
    * Silent Pearls can now be crafted after unlocking the Ender Pearl I Collection.
  * Added Pests to Stranded Bestiary.
  * Damage dealt from punching is now affected by your Strength Stat.
