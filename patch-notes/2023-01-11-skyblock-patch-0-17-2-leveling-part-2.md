# SkyBlock Patch 0.17.2 - Leveling Part 2

![sb-17-2.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-17-2-png.3154955/)

  
Hello,  
  
Today we are releasing the second part of the Leveling update!  


  * 23 New Ways to Level Up
  * Redesigned and Cleaner Levels Menus
  * Museum Value Updates
  * Levels XP Balancing
  * Kuudra Hunt Fixes

  
**► New Ways to Level Up**  
There are 23 new ways to earn SkyBlock XP!  


  * Mining Ores during the Mining Fiesta
  * Killing Sharks during the Fishing Festival
  * Placing in a bracket during the Spooky Festival
  * Purchasing perks from the 6 Essence Shops:
    * Dragon, Undead, Wither, Ice, Spider, and Crimson
  * The Kuudra Boss Collection
  * Killing Mythological Creatures
  * Defeating Kuudra Infernal Tier
  * Completing the Crystal Nucleus
  * Leveling your Peak of the Mountain
  * Special Rock Pet Milestones
  * Special Dolphin Pet Milestones
  * Purchasing Upgrades from Anita's Shop
  * Upgrading your Accessory Bag
  * Eating Reaper Peppers
  * Unlocking new Power Stones
  * Getting all your Abiphone contacts
  * Purchasing free upgrades from the Community Shop
  * Upgrading your Personal Bank

Overall, there is now 3,887 new XP in the game amounting to another 38 levels you can now achieve!

**► Levels Balancing**  
Through it's time on Alpha, we have balanced the amount of XP that certain tasks give, along with the ways to complete them.  


  * **The Dojo**
    * You now earn XP based on the highest belt you've achieved
    * XP earned: 350->425
  * **Harp Songs**
    * You will now earn XP for partially completing songs as well!
    * XP earned: 210->236
  * **Bank Upgrades**
    * XP earned: 165->200

  
**► Museum Changes**  


  * Item Values in the Museum are now determined based on the items value **with all of its modifiers!**
    * When determining a value, the server will use values from the bazaar to determine your total item value.
  * There are more rewards in the Museum: 230->288
    * The rewards will scale as new items are added to the Museum.



![Museum Value.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/museum-value-png.3154912/)

  
_Each item in the Museum now has a Clean Value and a Total Value!_ ​

  
**► Kuudra Hunt Changes**  
Now that the Kuudra follower hunt has been solved, we have made a few changes to the steps to make it more accessible to players!  


  * All players no longer have to be in the air for Drakuu to recognize their efforts.
  * You no longer need as much collection to speak to the Kuudra Loremaster!
  * You can now use any drill you please to obtain the Kuudra Relic!

  
**► Other Balancing**  


  * **Slugfish Bobber**
    * Reduced Catch Time: 30->20 seconds
