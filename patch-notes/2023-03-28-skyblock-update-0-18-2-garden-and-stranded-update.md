# SkyBlock Update 0.18.2 - Garden and Stranded Update

![sb-18-2.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-18-2-png.3186092/)

  
  
Hey everyone! SkyBlock has been updated to version 0.18.2! This version comes with major updates to the Garden and Stranded, including composter upgrades, NPC sell price changes, Garden Visitor adjustments, and Stranded Lava Fishing.  
  
► **Composter Upgrades**  
You can now upgrade your Composter using crops from your Garden! This new feature includes five upgrades, with each having 25 levels. As you progress through each level, you'll significantly increase the amount of compost gained as well as gain additional SkyBlock XP.

► **Additional Garden Changes**  


  * Significantly increased the NPC sell price of all crops, including rare crops.
  * Visitors can take items held in sacks when their offer is accepted. Items in the player's inventory are prioritized over sacks.
  * Visitors have been chastised for sometimes asking for too many items. Some of these requests should have been shrunk.
  * Increased Copper rewards by an additional 30%
  * Added Seymour's Armor set to the Museum.
  * Increased the private island and Garden teleport pad limit to 56.
  * Added a chat message when a visitor joins your Garden.
  * Seeds are now affected by Farming Fortune.
  * The wheat crop upgrade now gives Farming Fortune to seeds.
  * Custom plot presets can now have special icons added to them.
  * Nether wart now gives Farming XP instead of Alchemy XP.
  * The next Farming Contest will always display in tab.
  * Crop Milestones will now appear in the tab list for your current crop.
  * Strength will now appear in tab when you have a Mooshroom Cow pet equipped.
  * All farming tools can now be sold at the Auction House.
  * Added Skill Numeral support for all Garden Milestones.
  * Farming tools can now only break crops and stems (farming axes can also break logs)
  * Each unlocked Garden plot gives +3 Farming Fortune while on your Garden and +5 SkyBlock XP.
  * Seeds, Cropies, Squash, Fermentos, and Condensed Fermentos can now be added to the Composter.
  * Color codes now work on signs in the Garden.
  * Using /warp garden while in your Garden will bring you back to your garden spawn point
  * Jacob and Anita now have their own space instead of invading Sam's.
    * Note: You may not see their new location until a different barn skin is applied to your garden plot.
  * Added 3 new Garden accessories.
  * Added 2 new Garden enchants.
  * Added 5 new Garden reforge stones.
  * Added 2 new pet items.

**Lava Fishing Changes**  


  * Enabled Lava Fishing on Stranded
  * Odger can now be placed on Stranded Islands.
  * Lava Sea Creatures now have an extra chance to become corrupted (0.1% chance per fishing level)
  * Plhlegblast can now spawn on Stranded Islands.
  * Some special lava fishes will have different methods of obtaining than normal game modes. Experiment to figure out how to obtain them!
    * Volcanic Stonefish, Sulphur Skitter, Skeleton Fish, Moldfin, Soul Fish, Karate Fish, Gusher

**Other Changes**  


  * Ender Leggings drop rate nerfed from 3% to 0.3% to match the rest of the armor set
  * Zealots now have a chance to drop the Enderman pet
  * Golden Ghouls now have a chance to drop the Ghoul pet
  * Removed Chili Pepper, Sulphur (ore), and Mycelium from the Builder NPC spawn requirements
  * Runic Mobs now spawn on stranded islands
  * Dungeon Completion Requirements are no longer required on Stranded Island items
  * Marina's Fishing Wisdom perk now also works on Stranded Profiles
  * Oasis Sheep and Rabbits can now be fished. Experiment to figure out how to obtain them!
  * Fixed Zealot Spawning
  * Added the following trades to Villagers as level 1 trades:
    * 1 to 1 Emerald > Mycelium
    * 1 to 1 Emerald > Sulphur

► **Other Changes**  


  * Can now use _/warp home_ and _/warp garden_ to warp to your island spawn from within the island.
  * Improved Gwendolyn's dialog with new players to teach them about the Fast Travel menu's Crystal Hollow option
  * All quest items in Romero and Juliet's quest are now co-op soulbound
  * All Tyashoi Alchemist items are now co-op soulbound and can be sold to vendors for a minimal amount of coins.
  * The Seal of the Family, Crooked Artifact, and the Shady Ring are now soulbound.
    * These items can now also be re-purchased from Rusty for the following price:
    * Seal of the Family: 10 Million Coins
    * Crooked Artifact: 2 Million Coins
    * Shady Ring: 500,000 Coins
  * Reworded the Zombie Pet's perk Chomp to say Heal rather than Gain. Functionality has not changed.
  * Updated some discrepancies in the Sea Creature Guide (/scg) for Crystal Hollow sea creatures to ensure the correct information is shown in the Requirements section.
  * Odger should turn magmafish into silver magmafish as needed when fileting Trophy Fish.
  * In Dungeons: The Tank ability "Seismic Wave" should now also be able to explode crypts.
  * Re-enabled fishing rod damage on Private Islands (and Stranded Islands)
  * Added two more tiers to the Sack of Sacks upgrade to the Clownfish collection and three tiers of it to the Community Shop.
