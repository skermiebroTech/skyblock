# [April 10] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Redstone minions saying their location was imperfect in the presence of Glowing Redstone Ore.
  * Fixed Fishing minions complaining sometimes about imperfect location when surrounded by water.
  * Updated the Cactus minion's layout menu to show that it needs to be surrounded by air for a perfect layout.
  * Fixed an issue where the Chuckleton NPC was sticking around longer than he should. He's an April fool.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
