# [December 5] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Fairy Souls and gifts progress sometimes not saving correctly.
  * Fixed Cleaver Swords cleave often barely doing damage.
  * Marco's quest no longer requires a rank.
  * Changed Matriarch Perfume cooldown now resetting every day, instead of having a 24 hour cooldown.

  
**Pet Care Expansion City Project**  
There is a new City Project for you to contribute to which will expand Pet Care for everyone!   
It introduces a new NPC that will allow you to train multiple pets at once, and give them EXP!  
  
  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
