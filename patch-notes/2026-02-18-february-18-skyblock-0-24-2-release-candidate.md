### **► The Year of the Witch**​

![year_of_the_witch.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/year_of_the_witch-png.3544314/)

  
The Year of the Witch is the third event of its type, following on from the Year of the Pig and Year of the Seal. During this year, talk to Alcina in The Hub to view the Witch's Stew. During this year, mobs throughout SkyBlock will drop various ingredients, the type of which depends upon their Mob Type.  
  
Mobs of multiple types are eligible to drop multiple ingredients, with the tasty list including, but not being limited to...  


  * Waterborne Lice
  * Dirty Fingernails
  * Void Ooze
  * Festering Flies
  * Funny Bones
  * Frogs Legs

You can use these delightful ingredients to craft various stews that will reward you with permanent Magic Find buffs when consumed, or to purchase useful items - and even a pet - from the Year of the Witch Shop.  
  
As you may notice, two items that can be purchased from the Year of the Witch Shop are mixins to help you out during the Year of the Pig and Year of the Seal. The long-term goal for this year is for it to be something that helps improve your performance in other "Year of the" events in a manner not all too dissimilar from how Alchemy as a whole is used to improve your stats/efficiency in other areas of the game, so expect more mixins to be added to the YOTW Shop as more "Year of the" events are added.

#### **➥ Frozen Corpse Milestones**​

Grandpa Wolf can be found at -56, 123, 332 in the Glacite Tunnels. If you can loot enough Frozen Corpses, he will reward you for your time in the form of several useful perks and a new talisman line. All Frozen Corpses Milestones are retroactive, meaning you won't have to get frostbite once again if you've already put the work in.

### **► Various QOL Changes**​

Today we have a number of high-impact quality-of-life changes to several areas of the game.  
  


#### **➥ Combat**​

  * Adjusted the unlock order of the Kuudra Pet's perks. The new order can be found below:
    * COMMON -> Wither Bait
    * UNCOMMON -> Trophy Bait
    * RARE -> Crimson
    * EPIC -> Kuudra Fortune
    * LEGENDARY -> Kuudra Specialist
      * **[!] Designer Note:** A key goal here is for the "Trophy Bait" perk to be more accessible, since +20 Trophy Chance is a massive amount to be locked behind the Epic tier of the pet.
  * Adjusted the rarity of the Wolf Talisman from COMMON -> UNCOMMON.
  * Adjusted the purchase requirements for the Blood God Crest:
    * Can now be purchased infinitely instead of once per profile.
    * Now capped at +7 Strength.



#### **➥ Dungeoneering**​

  * Added the Spirit Mask to the Master Mode Floor 4 drop table and removed it from the Dark Auction.
    * Can only drop from the Obsidian Chest, costs 15m to open.
    * Has 4x less weight than a Spirit Sword.
  * Added a chat message that displays whenever you level up a Boss Collection.
  * Adjusted Golden Heads and Diamond Heads to now be purchased from Ophelia upon being unlocked.
    * **[!] Designer Note:** With this change, Diamond Heads now carry over upgrades from Golden Heads.
  * Added 250 Gold Essence to Boss Collection tiers where Golden Heads were previously awarded.
  * Added 1,000 Diamond Essence to Boss Collection tiers where Diamond Heads were previously awarded
  * Adjusted the default dungeons class from Archer -> Berserk.
  * Removed the ability to activate Berserk's Throwing Axe by sneaking while in The Catacombs.
  * Adjusted the Shadow Assassin Cloak's speed stat:
    * Base: 3 -> 10
    * Fragged: 5 -> 15



#### **➥ Fishing**​

  * Added a Golden Fish Tablist Widget to the Crimson Isle.
  * Added a Seal Pet to Lukas the Aquarist's shop during the Year of the Seal!
  * Adjusted the speed of earlier-tier Fishing Minions to ensure the speed increase was more gradual between tiers.
  * Adjusted Fishing Baits to only be consumed upon successful catch.
  * Adjusted Sea Creatures to no longer attack players aside from the spawner unless provoked.
  * Removed the mechanic that would cause you to catch nothing if you didn't move for a while when fishing.



#### **➥ Miscellaneous**​

  * Added the ability for players to retrieve perishable minion fuels (e.g Hyper Catalysts) from minions.
  * Added reminder text to all Accessories to inform the player that they still function while in the Accessory Bag.
  * Added the ability for Hoppity to take coins from Bank if you have insufficient funds in your Purse.
  * Adjusted the Pig Pet, giving it a new set of perks.
  * Adjusted items within the 20s can't-buy period on the Auction House to be hidden until the 20s has elapsed.
  * Removed the ability to Recombobulate several types of items:
    * Wisp Upgrade Stones
    * Enchanted Bookshelves
    * Bejewelled Handles
    * Soul String
    * Griffin Feathers
  * Removed NPC sell prices from several special items:
    * Creative Mind
    * Quality Map
    * Dead Bush of Love

    * French Bread
    * The Cake
