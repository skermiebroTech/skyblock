# [October 31] Great Spook Event Post-Patch Fixes!

Good morning video gamers,  
Following the past day of review from the Great Spook Event, we've made a few changes and bugfixes that should make things be a bit better! Have a look below:  
  


  * **Made Primal Fears more common** :
    * Primal Fears will spawn after **6 minutes, minus 3 seconds for every 1 Fear that you have**.
    * For awareness, once they are able to be summoned, they will have a 10% chance to spawn for every mob killed. This is the same behavior as pre-patch.
  * Added extra information about the Great Spook, your current Fear Stat, and the cooldown until you can spawn your next Primal Fear in tablist.
    * Edit (Nov. 1): Fixed an issue where sometimes this information would overlap with Crimson Faction Quests or Firesale info.
  * Added some extra information about the Great Spook when you join SkyBlock.
  * Added buttons to the Vargul milestone menu for giving him candy from your inventory or sacks. _(Yes, Stranded players can do this too!)_
  * Added a hologram above Fear mobs so that you can see who they belong to.
  * Added information in the Tyashoi Alchemist's menu for what your current Primal Fear cooldown is.
  * Added information to the Eerie pet's 'Fearcreasing' perk to display how many Primal Fears you've killed.
  * Prevented Primal Fears from spawning in the Rift. Too much spooky in one place.
  * Messages that make the Public Speaking Demon happy will no longer be sent to chat.
  * Muted players can now complete the Public Speaking Demon and Math Teacher gimmicks.
  * Answering two Math Teachers' questions in a row will no longer get blocked by the spam filters.
  * Fixed Primal Fears being able to be runic or corrupted.
  * Nerfed the Deadline timer from 8s -> 5s.
  * Fixed receiving double rewards for killing Commitment Phobias.
  * Fixed an issue regarding Diana burrows.
  * ****Added a special emblem for players that give Vargul 600 Dark Candy!****
