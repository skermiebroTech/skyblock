# [December 4] Enchanting Update Day-One Patch

Hello!  
  
As a response to the large amount of feedback from players, we implemented some changes to the [Enchanting Update](https://hypixel.net/threads/skyblock-enchanting-mega-overhaul.3604521/).  


  * Dramatically reduced the level requirements for top enchants.
  * Daily XP cap from non-experiments raised from 50k -> 500k.
  * Added a button to replay the experiments up to 3 times a day (4 total) for some extra XP and a few bits.



![1607100884498.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1607100884498-png.2162940/)

  
Reset your experiments cooldown!​

  
We've read a lot of feedback about the Exp limit from non-experiments and some of the panic is due to a lack of information.  
Keep in mind, you can earn **millions** of experience from experiments, way more than regular enchanting in a short timespan!  
  
Anyway, we hope that now that the panic points are dealt with, you will all enjoy the new enchanting features!
