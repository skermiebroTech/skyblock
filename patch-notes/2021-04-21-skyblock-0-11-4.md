# SkyBlock 0.11.4

![skyblock-with-art-2021.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/skyblock-with-art-2021-png.2503403/)

Hey Guys!  
  
SkyBlock has now been updated to version 0.11.4. This update brings a variety of new and revamped content. By the way, [did you know backpacks were back?](https://hypixel.net/threads/april-20-backpacks-revamp.4142765/)  
  
In this update:  


  * Revamped Farming Islands
  * Catacombs - Master Mode Floor 6
  * Bug Fixes and QoL Changes 

  
**►****Farming Islands: Revamped**  
Our first order of business is the Farming Islands. The first thing you'll notice is that both the Barn and the Mushroom Desert are on the same server.  
  
**The Barn**  
  


**

![1619017238715.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1619017238715-png.2502905/)

**​

  
The barn hasn't changed much. We have added a lot more crops and increased the respawn rate of them, and there is a new quest in the windmill with the goal of adding a vendor on the island.  
  
**The Mushroom Desert**  
  


![1619018444906.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1619018444906-png.2502939/)

  
  
Sometimes desert, sometimes caves, sometimes there are mushrooms, sometimes there's an oasis, the new Mushroom Desert island is a complete revamp of the old one - complete with all old collections, a new way to get sand, and a lot of new quests.  
  
The Mushroom Desert is Farming peaceful content, you will not find an overpowered boss or any sort of danger here (unless you can't swim or fly).  
  


  * Hunt various animals with Trevor the Tracker.
  * Look for treasures with the Treasure Hunter. Some people's parents aren't very original when naming their children.
  * Help Jake gather animals into his house (he really needs to move somewhere safer).
  * Or just relax at the new oasis and do some fishing.

Take a breather from all this stressful combat and come check out the revamped farming islands!  
  
►**Catacombs - Master Mode Floor 6**  
It is finally time to challenge MASTER Sadan.  
  


![1619012586065.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1619012586065-png.2502759/)

  
​

Floor 6 has been balanced to be extremely difficult - it is a pretty hefty bump in difficulty from Floor 5.  
  
Along with Floor 6, we're adding a variety of fixes and balance changes:  
  


  * Reduced the number of mobs in Master Mode Thorn fight, but made them stronger. This should help with the lag.​

  * Added a more obvious visual clue during the Livid Boss fights, so players can tell which is the real one.​

  * Master Mode Livid now deals less damage at the start but will gain more damage than before when the wrong ones are killed​

  * Monster loot in Master Mode will now be stronger than in regular mode.​

  * Berserker's passive "Lust for Blood" is now half as effective when there is more than one berserker. Berserkers are notorious for being selfish in their lust for blood.​

  * All master mode floors will now have the same stacking completion EXP bonus as Regular Floor 7.​

  * All 3 mage goggles have been nerfed to account for the stats they gain from stars. This makes them just as good as before, but not for free.​

  * The Wither Shield ability has been changed to only reduce 10% damage (down from 30%), and the critical damage scaling has been reduced to 150% (from 200%). We still think it might be too good, but will be monitoring.​

  * Changes to Sadan Boss Fight - Killing Terracotta now reduces the survival phase by 1 second.​

  * Mana Potions now increase mana regeneration by 1 mana per second, per level of the potion​

    * _This change came out in the last patch, we just thought we should mention it now since we forgot last time._ ​
