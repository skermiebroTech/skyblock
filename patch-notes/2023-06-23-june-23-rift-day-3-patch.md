# [June 23] Rift Day 3 Patch

Hello, here are the changes to the Rift island in the last few days since its release!  
  
**Bugfixes**  


  * Implemented better reset of leech supreme abilities (cleaning up holes in the ground that appeared sometimes)
  * Fixed getting teleported inside the plaza by the Déja Vu Alley despite not having unlocked it yet
  * Fixed being able to get multiple agaricus caps from 1 mushroom by spam clicking
  * Stopped criminals from accessing Shen's Auctions without paying the Barrier Street entrance
  * Give back detective scanner if the player lost it at the stage where you need to click that one painting
  * Fixed the yellow breadcrumb particles being displayed in every island
  * Fixed a state where players would forever bounce on the colosseum floor, even after /killbacte
  * Now letting Rift Ferret level up as a regular combat pet
  * Fixed Weird Tuba / Weirder Tuba walk speed buff not working inside the rift
  * Fixed the wrongly named "Acquire Healing Melon" objective
  * Fixed multiple typographic anomalies

  
**Slayer-Related Fixes / Tuning**  


  * Fixed Blood Ichor spawning way too high
  * Fixed Killer Spring not spawning in the underground area
  * Fixed assisters still being in the attack list of the slayer boss once they die, making them fill up the guards of the Oubliette by repeatingly dying to mania
  * Adjusted RNG meter for vampire stuff to be easier to grind
  * Fixed players limit on Leggings of the Coven
  * Fixed Bluetooth Ring and Silkrider Safety Belt reducing hearts damage (including Coagulation)
  * Fixed Anti-Bite Scarf NOT reducing damage
  * Increased Splash Yo Face mana cost from 15->20
  * Reduced Melons mana cost from 60->50
  * Made Blood Donor Artifact recipe 4x cheaper

All in all, the bugfixes mean the boss is harder, especially at t5.  
If you are now having trouble in t4, getting better gear should do the trick.  
  
**Protip!** When the riftstalker flies way up in the air, aim the red particles at the dead clotgoyles to destroy them!  
  
**Note!** We are still investigating the mirrorverse dance room locking out, + we might change how motes can be transferred from coins using slayer drops / hemovibe next week.
