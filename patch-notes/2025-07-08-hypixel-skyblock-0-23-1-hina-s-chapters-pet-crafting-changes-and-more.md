### **► Pet Crafting Changes**​

We've revamped how Pet Crafting works to remove any and all randomness. Any Pet you craft now starts at Common rarity, and you'll need to upgrade it at Kat now to get it up to Legendary. We've rebalanced all of the material, coin, and time costs. Generally speaking, most Pets should be easier to obtain and upgrade for early game players, which is what our ultimate goal is. Since this update hit the Alpha Network we've done another balance pass over all of the resource costs for Pet upgrades. You can find all of the updated values [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Jw5Y53n5ajcaKFndjgfeWZddZgGcEkM8kKVbr7SDjBc/edit?usp=sharing).

  * Added Common, Uncommon, and Rare Pigman Pets
  * Added Common, Uncommon, and Rare Wither Skeleton Pets



### **➥ Revamped Chicken Pet**​

We've revamped the Chicken Pet to make it more useful for early game players as an early game Farming Pet.

### **➥ Husbandry Drop Changes**​

We've taken this opportunity to make a couple of consistency changes to certain Husbandry items.  


  * Added Enchanted Cooked Rabbit _(crafted with x160 Enchanted Raw Rabbit)_
  * Enchanted Rabbit Hide Recipe: x576 → x160 Rabbit Hide
  * Enchanted Leather Recipe: x576 → x160 Leather
  * Changed a variety of recipes that utilize Enchanted Leather to compensate for this change
  * Rabbit Minion recipes now only use Raw Rabbit
  * Fixed an issue where Rabbit Minions were only dropping half the Rabbit Hide/Rabbit's Feet they should've been

We want to make more improvements to SkyBlock's new player experience in the future, stay tuned!
