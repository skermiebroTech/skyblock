# [November 9th] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about some further adjustments rolled after 0.23.6 to SkyBlock!  


  * Added Mythos Fragments to the Bazaar under Oddities -> Events -> Mythological
  * Increased Mythos Fragment weight from Griffin Burrows dug with the Archaic Spade from 10 -> 40 
    * (1.78% -> 6.76%)
  * Increased Mythos Fragment weight from Griffin Burrows dug up with the Deific Spade from 100 -> 300 
    * (3.31% -> 9.31%)

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
