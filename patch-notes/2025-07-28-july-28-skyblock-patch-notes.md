# [July 28] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hi,  
  
Following our [previous wave of fixes](https://hypixel.net/threads/regarding-event-rewards-and-habanero-tactics.5957272/), we have rolled some further changes that are related to Spider Slayer.  


  * The Shriveled Cornea is now obtainable.
    * The recipe unlocks at Spider Slayer 8, and requires 4 Shriveled Wasps and 64 Enchanted Eye of Ender.
    * The Bloodshot passive works on minibosses.
      * However, Barbarian Duke X, Ashfang, Bladesoul, Mage Outlaw, and Magma Boss are now classified as bosses and therefore won’t be affected by the ability.



  


![shriveled_cornea.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/shriveled_cornea-png.3467826/)

  
  
​

  * The Mosquito Pet's upgrade costs have been modified, as follows:
    * Crafting Recipe now requires 4 Digested Mosquito, 4 Tarantula Silk, and 1 Super Enchanted Egg.
  * The Mosquito Pet's maximum Base Pest Chance has been increased from 40% to 50%.
  * Kat upgrades for the Mosquito Pet are now as follows:



| COMMON → UNCOMMON| UNCOMMON → RARE| RARE → EPIC| EPIC → LEGENDARY  
---|---|---|---|---  
Time in h| 1 Day| 2 Days| 3 Days| 5 Days  
Coin Cost| 250k coins| 500k coins| 1m coins| 2.5m coins  
Additional Cost| 8x Tarantula Silk| 16x Tarantula Silk| 32x Tarantula Silk| 64x Tarantula Silk  
  
  * The Shellwise attribute now works exactly the same as a Dwarf Turtle Shelmet when at level 10.
    * At lower tiers, it now grants a chance to negate knockback instead of slightly reducing knockback.
  * Egg sac spawn heights have been raised by a couple blocks.
  * Revamped the Gravel Mines to be easier to navigate, and Gravel now respawns much faster.

  


### **► Shriveled Cornea Hotfix**​

Due to an issue that allowed players to get the Bloodshot reforge without using the reforge stone **we've removed Bloodshot from all items**. Players who previously used a Shriveled Cornea to apply Bloodshot will have one refunded to them within a few days. Keep an eye on Jerry if you're expecting a refund!  
**Update 7/31/25** Refunds have been sent out!   
  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
