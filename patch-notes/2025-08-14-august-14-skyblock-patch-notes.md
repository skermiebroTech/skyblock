# [August 14] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hello!  
  
Today we have a quick patch with small changes to several areas of the game following the release of [Hypixel SkyBlock 0.23.3](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-23-3-foraging-changes-mob-types-and-more.5963817/).  


  * Added "Daily Bonus" to the Forest Whisper replacement reward when HOTF is maxed.
  * Removed the conflictions between the "Sharpness" enchantment and mob-specific enchantments, such as "Smite".
    * **Designer Note:** Please note that this change may require additional balancing in the future, however we feel it's the most intuitive change to make following the introduction of mob types.
  * Removed the requirement to occasionally move while fishing to prevent yourself from catching nothing.
  * Fixed an issue where the "Woodsplitter" enchantment was an unknown Bazaar product.
  * Fixed an issue causing damage to not be dealt correctly to some ☽ Spooky mobs during the Spooky Festival.
  * Fixed an issue causing Scaffolding not to work while in shallow water.
  * Adjusted some HOTF/HOTM messages to say "Exp" instead of "EXP".
  * Adjusted the "Smite" enchantment to now also grant its damage bonus on Skeletal and Wither mobs.
  * Adjusted the Mob Types of several mobs to now match the below:
    * Abyssal Miner → Aquatic/Undead/Subterranean
    * Automaton → Subterranean/Construct/Arcane
    * Boss Corleone → Humanoid/Subterranean
    * Butterfly → Arcane/Airborne/Subterranean
    * Cellar Spider → Arthropod/Subterranean
    * Fire Eel → Magmatic/Animal
    * Fireproof Witch → Magmatic/Humanoid/Arcane
    * Ghosts → Arcane/Subterranean
    * Glacite Mutt → Glacial/Animal
    * Kada Knight → Animal/Undead
    * Key Guardian → Undead/Subterranean
    * Lava Flame → Magmatic/Infernal
    * Mimic → Arcane/Construct
    * Night Squid → Aquatic/Animal
    * Reindrake → Frozen/Shielded/Elusive
      * **Designer Note: **This is a weird case, since it's also airborne by nature. We may make it the first mob to be four types in the future - time will tell.
    * Rider of the Deep → Aquatic/Undead/Animal
    * Skeletor → Skeletal/Construct
    * Squid → Aquatic/Animal
    * The Loch Emperor → Aquatic/Skeletal/Arcane
    * Trash Gobbler → Aquatic/Arthropod
    * Wither Miner → Wither/Skeletal
  * Adjusted the bracket points for Agatha Contests in accordance with community feedback:
    * Epic: 2,500 -> 2,000
    * Legendary: 5,000 -> 3,000
    * Mythic: 7,500 -> 5,000
    * Divine: 10,000 -> 8,000
    * Special: 12,500 -> 12,000
  * Adjusted point gain for Starlyn Contests,
    * **Designer Note:** The goal here is for activities outside of Fig Trees to be capable of reaching top brackets.
    * Per 100 Mangrove Logs: 4 → 6
    * Hunting Verdant/Coral/Azure: 1 → 3
    * Hunting Mossybit/Cod: 2 → 6
    * Hunting Coralot/Lumisquid/Spike/Salmon: 3 → 15
    * Hunting Shellwise/Mudworm: 5 → 45
    * Hunting “Other”: 10 → 30
    * Fishing Treasure: 5 → 25
    * Per Catch: 1 → 4
    * Per Sea Creature Catch: 3 → 10
    * Per Sea Creature Kill: 2 → 8
    * Per Lushlilac: 3 → 9
    * Per Sea Lumies: 2 → 6

If you run into any issues following this release, please report them [HERE](https://hypixel.net/forums/skyblock-bug-reports.159/).  
  
Thanks!
