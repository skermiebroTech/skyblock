# [October 20] Crystal Hollows Achievements & Dungeon Finder Settings

Hey All!  
  
We've just rolled a patch that includes some crystals hollows achievements, more dungeon search settings, and a couple of other small changes.  
  
**Crystal Hollows Achievements**  


  * I Call That... Mercy | Place all 5 Gemstones into the Gemstone Gauntlet
  * Lapidarist | Forge a perfect Gemstone
  * Should’ve stayed cool | Melt to death in the Magma Fields
  * Smells Better | Wash off the King’s Scent using water
  * Didn't mean to! | Kill a Kalhuiki Youngling
  * Magical Place | Find the Fairy Grotto
  * How to train your dragon? | Find the dragon room
  * Prepare for trouble! | Kill Corleone
  * Crystal Nucleus | Complete the Crystal Nucleus 5/10/15/20/25 times,
  * Divan's Treasures | Find treasures in the Mines of Divan 30/60/90/120/150 times,

  
**Dungeon Search Settings**  
  
Additional search settings have been added that allow you to filter groups between a given class level and/or dungeon level range.  
  


![Search settings.PNG](https://hypixel.net/attachments/search-settings-png.2778789/)

  
  
On top of that there's now a sort groups section that allows you to organise groups by most recently queued, most players or highest dungeon level requirements!  
  


![Sort Groups.PNG](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sort-groups-png.2778791/)

  
**Additional changes**  


  * Fixed a bug where boss fights say you have a new damage record when that's not the case
  * Fixed recombobulators working on arrows, they should now only work on armor/weapons/tools and talismans
  * Skill/collection rankings now show a more accurate percentage of your position
  * The /warp command is now case insensitive
  * Fixed an issue where guests were able to break the block at 0,0,0
