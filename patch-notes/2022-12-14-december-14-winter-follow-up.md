# [December 14] Winter Follow Up

Hi all,  
Today we have a quick follow up patch for the recent Jerry's Workshop update with some QOL changes and bug fixes.  


  * Glacial Ice treasure mining is now per player
    * Makes Ice Mining treasure rewards count towards collections
  * Fixes Frost Walker messing with Hot Spring water/ice generation
  * Fixes Block Zapper working on Frost Walker Ice
  * Makes gifts drop North Stars outside of December
  * North star shop is still open all year but accessories are locked to December
  * Ironman profiles can now trade gifts with co-op members
  * Added 1 extra second to a few checkpoints on the Chicken Race
  * The Nutcracker armr recipe is now 3x more expensive
  * Nutcracker armor & Sea Creature now requires Fishing 28 instead of 30
  * Reindeer Pet Changes
    * Increased the cost of the pet from 40 to 60 North Stars
    * Added a 0.25 base to 25 max Fishing Speed base stat
    * Infused Perk only scales to 75 now instead of 100
    * Added 10 Sea Creature Chance to the Infused perk (still only while on the Jerry island)
    * Snow Power Perk now scales 0.05% chance per level, still caps 5% at max level
    * Icy Wind Perk now scales 0.2% per level, still maxes out at 20%

  
Many thanks for all the feedback and happy holidays!
