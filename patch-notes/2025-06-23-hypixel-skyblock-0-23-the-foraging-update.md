# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.23 - The Foraging Update

![foraging-forums.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/foraging-forums-png.3449271/)

  
  
Hello,  
  
The **Foraging Update** is here, and it’s the most technically ambitious update in the history of Hypixel SkyBlock.  
Today, we’re proud to finally reveal it to you. Thank you for your patience!  
  
  


### **► Latest Version ![:hypixel_smile:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)**​

This is the first SkyBlock update to fully utilize mechanics from the latest versions of Minecraft.  
  
**To access The Park and Galatea, you’ll now need to play on Minecraft 1.21.5 or higher**.  
  
Don’t worry; the rest of SkyBlock remains fully accessible on older versions.  
  


![2024-04-09_09.07.18.jpeg](https://hypixel.net/attachments/2024-04-09_09-07-18-jpeg.3449191/)

  
This is just the beginning, but here’s what you can already find on the new islands:  


  * New blocks
  * New mobs
  * New particle effects
  * New sounds
  * New swimming mechanics
  * And more!

This update lays the foundation for something much bigger. We’re now able to ramp up the complexity and quality of our content using the latest Minecraft tech.  
  
If you’re interested in how far we’re taking it, stay tuned! We’ll be releasing a separate dev blog soon covering resource packs, custom menus, models, and more.  
  


### **► The Park Revamp ![:hypixel_sloth:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)**​

![park-overview.jpg](https://hypixel.net/attachments/park-overview-jpg.3449205/)

  
We’re planning to gradually upgrade all islands to the latest version — and, with each one, improve its gameplay and design. The Park is the first island to receive this treatment.  
  
Here’s what’s new:  


  * The **main Foraging tutorial quest** has been completely revamped and streamlined.
  * The **Foraging Collections** have been improved.
  * There are now early game **Foraging Armors** and new items tied to the new progression system.
  * Meet **Molebert** , a new NPC with a short quest for curious players.

  


### **► Galatea ![:hypixel_cool:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)**​

![2024-04-09_09.13.23.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/2024-04-09_09-13-23-png.3449192/)

  
The **Moonglade Marsh** is the first of three planned Foraging Islands of Galatea, each with its own trees and mysteries.  
  


### ➥ Heart of the Forest ![:react_useful:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

When you first arrive, you’ll meet **Hina** , who grants you access to the **Heart of the Forest,** the core of Galatea’s progression system.  


![1750689791605.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1750689791605-png.3449293/)

Just like the Heart of the Mountain, you’ll level up this system, unlocking perks and upgrades as you go.  
Its main currency is **Forest Whispers** , earned by cutting down trees across the island.  
  


### ➥ Swoop ![:react_helper_lapis:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

At the entrance of the village, you'll meet **Swoop** , your first Foraging guide.  
He teaches you the basics of tree cutting on Galatea and tracks your **Foraging Milestones,** rewarding you as you chop your way through the island.  
  


### ➥ Trees Toughness, Styles and Gifts ![:react_superstar_gold:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

  * **Sweep** is a new stat that determines how many logs you can cut in a row, similar to the Treecapitator effect.
  * **Toughness** is a property of each tree that reduces the impact of your Sweep stat, requiring more Sweep to take down tougher trees.
  * **Style** defines the _correct order_ in which each tree should be chopped. Cutting them the wrong way is inefficient.

There are two types of trees on the first island of Galatea:  
  


#### **Fig Trees** ​

  * **Toughness:** 7
  * **Style:** Start with the **Trunk** , then finish with the **Branches**
  * You can throw your axe into the air to cut branches! On Foraging islands, all axes can be thrown, though thrown strikes only cut half the usual logs.



#### **Mangrove Trees** ​

  * **Toughness:** 50 (We recommend at least 50 Sweep before attempting these!)
  * **Style:** Start with the **Branches** , then the **Trunk** , and finally the **Roots**

  
Once a tree is fully cut down, any player who contributed at least 10% to its removal will receive a **Tree Gift**.  
These contain:  


  * Foraging EXP
  * Heart of the Forest EXP
  * Forest Essence
  * Unique Collection Items
  * And sometimes... surprises.

Working together with other players to fell trees is not just helpful; it’s rewarding.

### ➥ Agatha, the first Starlyn Sister ![:react_admin_diamond:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

Throughout each in-game day, Agatha will track your activity across the island, awarding you points for:  


  * Cutting Trees
  * Collecting Berries and Sea Lumies
  * Hunting
  * Fishing
  * Killing monsters

At the end of each day, she’ll reward you based on your total score, including a special currency called Agatha’s Coupons, which you can spend in her personal shop.

The release of attributes brought a wave of new possibilities, but also several design challenges, as outlined in previous design threads.  
  
With the **Hunting** update, we introduce a brand new skill that adds layered gameplay, fresh progression, and most importantly, transforms attributes into permanent buffs tied to your profile.

In the village of Galatea, you’ll quickly meet **David Hunterborough,** a brilliant hunter who will teach you the fundamentals of this new skill.  
  
Progress far enough under his guidance, and he’ll reward you with his prized **David’s Cloak** , the most powerful cloak in the game.

### ➥ Hunting Tools ![:react_useful:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

Hunting revolves around tracking and capturing a wide variety of unique creatures, each with their own Shard to collect. There are multiple ways to hunt and acquire shards, depending on the species:  


  * Some creatures feature unique, custom hunting mechanics
  * Others can be caught using Lassos or Fishing Nets
  * Certain shards are obtained via shops, crafting recipes, or the Fusion Machine
  * Some creatures are hunted through combat, using specialized Hunting Weapons

It may sound like a lot but don’t worry; it quickly becomes intuitive as you progress.  
  


### ➥ Attributes and Menus ![:react_youtuber_redstone:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

When you obtain a creature's Shard, it will be stored in your Hunting Box, found within the Hunting Skill Menu. From there, you can Syphon the shard to unlock or level up the corresponding Attribute, permanently.  


  * These new Attributes work similarly to the old system, but they are no longer tied to items
  * All active Attributes can be seen inside the Attribute Menu, also within the Hunting Skill interface
  * If you already own attributes from before, you can use the Transfer System to obtain the new shards
  * Players with “Godrolls” will receive Ananke Feathers, based on the value of that Godroll.



### ➥ Fusions ![:hypixel_thinking:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

Finished leveling an Attribute? Your extra shards don’t go to waste.  
Head over to the Fusion Machine and start experimenting, simply select a shard, pair it with another, and see what combinations appear.  


  * Each fusion will present 1 to 3 possible outcomes, including exclusive Fusion-only shards
  * Most fusions will require 5 copies of each shard, so plan accordingly
  * There’s an element of discovery here, and some rare combinations may lead to unexpected rewards

  
Fusions are still ongoing large balancing changes, and we plan to finalize the details within a week.  
  


### ➥ Kuudra ![:hypixel_evil:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)​

The Kuudra loot table has been overhauled to fit this new ecosystem.  
Key changes:  


  * Significantly increased **Crimson Essence** gains
  * Existing **Crimson Isle shards** remain obtainable, alongside **new exclusive shards**
  * **1 to 3 Kraken Shards** will now drop from **every Paid Chest** , based on the fight’s tier
  * **Ananke Feathers** have been added, powerful items that can progress **any RNG meter**
  * **Enrager** is no longer obtainable, however there is a new item: the **Tormentor** , rare, but reasonably attainable
  * The **Hellstorm Wand** has received a full **revamp**
  * and more!

### **► What's Next ![:hypixel_wink:](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)**​

As usual, the list below is not exhaustive, it's just what we're comfortable sharing at this point.

**Foraging Follow-up patch and future islands**  
We have already a few improvements planned and being developed for the Moonglade Marsh, and will release patch notes in the following week alongside eventual balancing changes.  
  
The Hunting Update changed the whole Attribute system and we will be monitoring the effects closely during the following weeks. In particular, Crimson Isle attribute-centric content will be revisited with necessary updates to them.  
  
While we have already planned out the second and third islands of Galatea, production is on hold until we have gathered enough feedback from this update.  
  
**Mythological Ritual v2**  
We are looking to release an update in the near future that will modernize Diana's Mythological Ritual event, adding some new mobs, drops and, obviously, the mythic griffin pet.  
  
**Slayer Content**  
Back at the start of the year, we promised all of you some slayer content. While we ended up reallocating some of our resources to the Foraging project, we took that time to design a brand-new slayer that we hope to release later on this year. All of our slayer content is now either in the later stages of development or awaiting QA.  
  
**Year of the Witch**  
The Year of the Witch looks to be the third event of its type, this time centered around Alchemy.  
  
As part of the Year of the Witch event, players will need to seek out particular mobs, killing them to gather ingredients. Ingredients can be used to gain permanent buffs or as currency in the Year of the Witch Shop. Also...we will be adding a Seal Pet with this update!  
  
**Lotus Atoll**  
The Lotus Atoll is our next fishing island and will be accessed via the Ship Navigator. It's going to contain some Trophy...Frogs?  
  
**Pet Crafting Changes**  
One of our upcoming goals is to improve the new player experience of SkyBlock. One of the ways we are planning doing this in the near future is through moving some Pets earlier in collections and making them cheaper to craft. For awhile we've wanted to remove randomized Pet crafting in the game, and we decided to take this opportunity to do that.  
  
All crafted Pets will now always start at Common rarity, but their early Kat upgrades are being changed to be incredibly cheap and quick to compensate. No more waiting for Diana!
