# [October 26] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Allowed Ironman players to claim Tyashoi's items once per profile instead of once per account.
  * Made the Head of Betrayal obtainable again. Make sure to talk to the Alchemist before doing it!
    * Now, any nearby players meeting the conditions will receive the Head of Betrayal instead of just the clicker.
  * Fixed the Nightmare Nullifier being smeltable in a furnace.
  * Fixed an exploit that allowed players to brew potions without meeting the recipe requirements.
  * Allowed this year's editioned Tyashoi items to be added to the Special category of the Museum.
  * Updated Diamond Goblin Bestiary kill bracket from 5 to 7.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
