# [October 21] Museum Patch Notes

Hello,  
  
We released a few changes to the Museum.

    * SkyBlock Level 100 to 199: 25 Billion Coins
    * SkyBlock Level 200 to infinity: 50 Billion Coins
  * Shen's Auction unlock have been lowered to Milestone 10, instead of 12.
