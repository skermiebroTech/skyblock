# [February 13] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
  
We are here to inform you about the latest changes fixes that were rolled to SkyBlock in the past day for 0.21.3.  


  * Added Fishy Treats to the Events Sack.
  * Bouncy Beach Balls will now pop when they hit cobwebs.
  * Reworked the Tidal Enchantment to give 2/4/6% Defense against Sea Creatures instead of Damage Reduction.
    * Adjusting this to 5/10/15% Defense***.
  * Fixed Bouncy Beach Balls awarding double the loot when coming into contact with a wall at the ground simultaneously.
  * Fixed Bouncy Beach Balls not having a reward message when it lands in the void.
  * Fixed Fishy Treats not going into sacks.
  * Fixed some Treasure Fishing loot pools being incorrect.

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
