# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.21.3 - Year of the Seal

![sb-21-3.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-21-3-png.3390092/)

  
  
Hello!  
  
Welcome to **Hypixel SkyBlock Patch 0.21.3**. Today's update introduces the yearly events system headlined by Year of the Seal and bringing back the Shiny Pig event as Year of the Pig, along with some other changes.  
  
Read on for the details!  
  
  
  


### **► Year of the Seal**​

The Year of the Seal is a new event that lasts an entire SkyBlock Year and follows a 12-year rotation. We are aiming to have a yearly event for each year within the 12 year rotation, but for now, only the Year of the Seal and the Year of the Pig will be available. These two events will alternate every six years until more yearly events are added.  


### ( •ᴥ• ) | **How the Year of the Seal works** ​

  * During the Year of the Seal, you have a chance to fish up Beach Balls or Giant Beach Balls from Treasure.
  * Use the Beach Balls to play a minigame by yourself that involves bouncing it on your head.
  * Use the Giant Beach Ball to play the same minigame but everyone around you can participate as well.
  * Based on your performance, you receive Fishy Treats which you can spend at Lukas near the Hub Blacksmith for various items.

  


![Beach Ball.gif](https://hypixel.net/attachments/beach-ball-gif.3390093/)

  
Spend your hard-earned Fishy Treats at Lukas near the Hub Well to unlock a variety of new rewards, including Fishing Tier 12 and Clay Tier 12 Minions, two new Accessories, a brand-new enchantment, and more!

### (՞•Ꙫ•՞)** | Return of the Shiny Pigs Event**​

The Shiny Pig Event is back, now in the form of the Year of the Pig as part of the new yearly rotation system! While the event remains familiar, we’ve made a few adjustments to make it easier to access and understand:  
  


  * The Pig Shop has been moved to near the Hub Blacksmith.
  * Talking to Piggy gives a brief introduction to the event
  * Added an Event Info within the Pig Shop menu

  


### **► Other Changes and Features**​

#### **➥** Crystal Hollows Changes​

**Jungle Temple**  


  * Significantly shortened parkour and removed some lava and traps.
  * Moved respawn location closer to temple.
  * Added more gemstones.
  * Improved navigation through the room significantly by raising the river canyon.
  * Prevented people breaking into the temple by mining through it.
  * Added ways to escape the lava.

**Lost Precursor City**  


  * Significantly improved navigation throughout the city.
  * Added more Automaton spawns.

**Mines of Divan**  


  * Improved navigation to aid travel between treasure points.
  * Removed difficult to access treasure locations.
  * Widened pathing around the Dwarven statues and crystal.



#### ****➥** General Changes**​

  * Added a new Rift objective for obtaining Detective Amog's wallet.
  * Added better error message when trying to collect a Relic after collecting all of them.
  * Adjusted the Time Chamber modifier descriptions to be more accurate and consistent.
  * Changed the Spiky achievement to unlock when applying Thorns 3 to an armor piece.
  * Rabbits now give Combat Exp like other animals.
  * Limited the Squeaky Mousemat's pitch and yaw settings to their actual minimum and maximums.
  * Updated Carrolyns initial dialogue to say mysterious instead of spooky
