### 2023 Advent Calendar​

![advent-calendar.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/advent-calendar-png.3269293/)

  
Similarly to last year's Advent Calendar, players will be able to claim a gift reward every day of December until the 25th.  
  
This year, you can also claim a second and third daily reward by playing SkyBlock each day and gifting other players!  
  


### ****Meet Generow, the Gift-Master Penguin ****​

![image \(2\).png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/image-2-png.3269294/)

  
  
Generow, our charming new NPC on Jerry's Workshop, is here to spread the joy of giving.  
  
The more unique players you donate gifts to during the month of December, the more milestones you’ll reach in Generow. The new rewards include:  


  * Frozen Bait (an improved version of Ice Bait)
  * Gift of Learning (Soulbound item letting you choose a skill to gain EXP in)
  * A way to obtain Einary’s Abiphone contact
  * A unique Memento item
  * A new emblem
  * Many, many gifts!

Generow will vanish from the face of SkyBlock in early 2024, so don’t forget to claim your rewards!  
  


### New Bottles of Jyrre​

Everybody’s favorite item(?) is back!  
  
But this time it’s actually good.  
  


![refined-bottle-of-jyyre.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/refined-bottle-of-jyyre-png.3269296/)

  * Old Bottles of Jyrre you may own are now legacy items.
  * Once maxed, the new bottle of Jyrre evolves and can grant you a permanent 1 Intelligence, and 2 SkyBlock XP.
  * You can only obtain that permanent buff 5 times.
  * It takes a total of 300 hours to max it out, and all you have to do is keep it in your inventory.
  * The Bottle does not reset anymore if you place it in a chest.
  * Oruo increments the timer of the Bottle by 1 full hour each time you complete his puzzle.
  * The Old Bottles of Jyrre are now removed from Sherry’s Shop, and you can obtain the new ones from gifts.



### New Boss Fight Reward​

The final gift pile of every Battle of Jerry Mountain event will now grant a Volcanic Rock if the pile was intact.  
You can now use the Volcanic Rock in Einary’s Shop to purchase new items:  


  * A new Ultimate Enchant: Refrigerate!
  * A Magic Top Hat (used to upgrade the Snowman Pet to Mythic)
  * Winter Fragments (used to upgrade the Yeti Sword, Glacial Scythe, and Ice Spray Wand.



### Other Winter Changes​

  * There are now more Gifts being rewarded in each Gift Pile during the main Event.
  * The Ice Essence Shop has a new perk, allowing you to increase the Odds to find a Reindrake
  * The Reindrake is 50% more likely to be found by default
  * The Glacial Cave loot table now includes new items, such as the Frozen Bait and Einary’s Red Hoodie.
  * The smallest Gift and EXP rewards from gifts have been removed, to increase their value.

  
Enjoy these wintery enhancements and have a splendid time in Jerry's Workshop!  
  


### **► Secret Bingo** ​

To celebrate the 2nd anniversary of SkyBlock Bingo, a special Secret Bingo is being held in December! To complete this bingo, you must figure out the goals and complete them. Do you have the smarts to find the solutions? Talk to Bingo in the SkyBlock Hub to get started!  
  
Here’s some top-secret information that could be useful:  


  * Secret Bingo is considered a special Bingo card! This means:
    * Secret Bingo will last for 14 days.
    * The points awarded for each goal and row/column completion is double the regular amount.
  * The names of Personal Goals will be revealed once the Bingo event starts.
    * The names of Community Goals have been revealed in advance for you to strategize. Good luck!!
  * Three hints will be released for each Bingo goal over the course of the event.
    * Hint reveals are staggered per hour, they are not all revealed at once.
    * Hovering over a goal will tell you which hints are already revealed and when the next hint reveal will occur for that goal.

### **►** **Other Changes** ​

### General **Changes and Enhancements** ​

  * Added an autopet kill entity rule for Mythological Creatures.
  * Added an autopet kill entity rule for Sea Creatures.
  * Swappable skins (such as Gemstone Divan) now open a menu, instead of having to spend a century cycling through each skin.
  * Swappable skins can now have each individual skin previewed while it's in the Community Shop.
  * Added the Alchemist as an Abiphone Contact.
  * Added /warp trap as an alias for the Trapper's Den.
  * Added a warning when picking up a minion with consumable fuel.
  * Added a message when unlocking a new Guide Tier in the SkyBlock Guide.
  * You can now hover over the header of the salvage text in chat to view a list of the items you salvaged.



![hi heatran.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/hi-heatran-png.3269298/)

  * Added Flaming Sword as a 1% drop chance from Redstone Pigman.
  * Buffed the amount of Ice Walker spawns.
  * Replaced the “Coming Soon” items in collections with Skill XP.
    * They will no longer be coming soon.
  * Gold Forger now asks for 1024 Golden Carrots instead of 1028.
  * Some accessories have been made soulbound instead of co-op soulbound,
    * This is introduced prevent co-op members from being able to bypass content because their co-op members trade them a talisman from some questline that they have not done.
  * The museum zone has been adjusted to be slightly lower to not interfere with Diana burrow digging.
  * Renamed the Ink Sack category in the Bazaar to Ink Sac to match the items within it.
