# [March 4] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Femento/Helianthus disappearing if you have a full inventory when they get super compacted.
  * Fixed items that get sent to stash not counting towards Commission progress.
  * Fixed /managebazaarorders allowing ironmen to view the regular Bazaar.
  * Fixed /wardrobe 3 opening page 1 even when page 3 is unlocked.
  * Fixed Efficient and Sweet axes not applying their effects for Logs broken by Sweep.
  * Fixed Training Dummys not having Mob Types applied.
  * Fixed Enchanted Golden Carrots and Enchanted Cookies not granting Farming XP when taken from a Minion.
  * Fixed Hideonring Accessory Bag upgrade not applying until switching servers.
  * Fixed the Bazaar not giving Shards as stackable items.
  * Fixed being unable to get the Sea Emperor's Soul.
  * Fixed the Fashionable Bingo Goal unique Armor counter resetting when switching servers.
  * Fixed Pandora's Box "Damage Exacter" Challenge not completing when the correct amount of damage is dealt.
  * Fixed the "Obtain a Babuleaf, Mochibear, and Bambloom" Hina Task not counting shards that have been syphoned and shards used to fuse Bambloom.
  * Disabled combining multiple books with different enchantments.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
