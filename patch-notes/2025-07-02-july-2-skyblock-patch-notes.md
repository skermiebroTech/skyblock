# [July 2] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Re-enabled Golden and Diamond Goblins
  * Fixed Keep It Cool HOTM perk using Mithril Powder instead of Gemstone Powder
  * Fixed not being able to unlock certain HOTM perks
  * Tree Gifts will now award Foraging XP even if the skill is maxed
  * Fixed Core of the Mountain and Blue Cheese Goblin Omelette not boosting Pickaxe Abilities
  * Fixed server-swapping resetting Attribute toggle
  * Fixed Tokens not being given back after Glacite Tunnels HOTM Reset
  * Fixed Murkwater Loch island portal not working when placed in certain directions
  * Fixed Soul Campfire not respecting Defense if your total Health is lower than 1 tick of its damage
  * Fixed Flaming Flay and Soul Whip using the incorrect sound effect
  * Fixed the 'Titanium Ore Multiplier' SkyMall perk being permanently enabled
  * Fixed HOTM and HOTF Abilities not costing Tokens to unlock
    * Players who were detected to have abilities unlocked from this bug have had their HOTM/HOTF tree reset and their tokens and powder/whispers refunded
  * Fixed some items desyncing, which made them stick to the cursor in an inventory:
    * Dead Bush
    * Enchanted Books
    * Golden Apple
    * Exp Bottle
    * Nether Star
    * Spawn Eggs

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
