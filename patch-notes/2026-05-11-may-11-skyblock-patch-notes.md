# [May 11] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Sugar Cane getting less Seasoning and **RARE CROP** drops than others.
  * Fixed Ender Pearls being able to phase through blocks, as they were being used to glitch into inaccessible areas.
  * Fixed feeding Nessie wheat items (e.g. Staff of the Volcano).
  * Fixed Chloronite not using proper fortune calculations.
  * Fixed Heat not decreasing when equipping heat armor.
  * Fixed Garden Commission dye drops not counting in the dye menu.
  * Fixed Seal of the Family not working on stacks.
  * Fixed cancelling Obfuscated Bait giving the wrong bait back.
  * Fixed a visual bug in which the All Crops menu would incorrectly display crops as "Out of season till next event" during Grand Feast in the later months of the year.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
