# [August 13] Item Drops + Tiny Patch

Quick update!  
  
**Item Drops**  
  
Today we're making a small update to the dropping mechanics in order to prevent abusive trading that we've been seeing. You will no longer be allowed to drop items to non-coop members. These changes are based on feedback from the community regarding items being dropped to them that they did not want, as well as combating IRL trading and boosting in general. We've had multiple instances of players being concerned about receiving an item they did not agree to receive, thinking that they may be banned for boosting or IRL trading themselves. Through your feedback, we believe this is the best route going forward in order to put those concerned about receiving duped or otherwise illegitimately obtained items that they did not ask for.  
  
You will receive the below alert when you first try to drop items to other players.  
  


![1628867570328.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1628867570328-png.2700484/)

  
  
That's about it for item dropping, pretty self explanatory. We do understand that this may impact a few legitimate trades, and we'll be monitoring this change as we go to make sure we're not impacting legitimate gameplay.  
  
**Tradeable items**  
Jacob's items are now tradeable (through /trade).  
  
**Rend**  
Updated Rend to avoid damage exploits with other items:  
  


![1628865475685.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1628865475685-png.2700425/)

  
  
This means:  


  * Rend now only takes into account crits from the Rend bow itself
  * Rend only counts the shots on the mob which is being Rended

**Other**  
Made these items sellable to NPCs:  


  * All reforge stones
  * Slayer tokens
  * Runes
  * Wishing Compass
  * Ascension Rope
  * Charcoal
  * Superboom TNT

Thank you!
