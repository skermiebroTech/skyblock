# [March 26] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Fixed a way to spawn more pests than intended.
  * Fixed Magma Lord armor bonus being active without meeting its requirements.
  * Fixed another case of being able to heal from taking damage with a high defense value.
  * Fixed some issues with Archer's Healing Bow in Dungeons.
  * Fixed Empowered Agility perk from the Spider Essence shop not applying to the Leaping Sword and Silk-Edge Sword.
  * Fixed the SkyBlock levels menu in some cases reporting a higher than 100% completion.
  * Fixed not being able to add Dean as a contact back after removing.
  * Fixed pests spawning in plots that are not cleaned.
  * Fixed recombobulating stacked items at once resulting in the deletion of some of the items.
  * Implemented some small tweaks to some widget layouts.
  * Resolved a few performance issues with the new tablist.
  * Fixed more typos!

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
