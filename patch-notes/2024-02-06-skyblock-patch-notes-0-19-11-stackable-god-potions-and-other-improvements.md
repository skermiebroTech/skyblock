# SkyBlock Patch Notes 0.19.11 - Stackable God Potions and Other Improvements

![skyblock-19-11.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/skyblock-19-11-png.3289177/)

  
  
Hello!  
  
Welcome to **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.11**. Today's update includes a bunch of miniscule yet fun changes as we continue to work towards the next content update. Read on to find out more!  
  
  


### **►** God Potion/Mixin Rework​

We have done a bunch of major improvements towards God Potions and Mixins. To support these improvements, how we store the duration of God Potions has been reworked to significantly reduce the chance of God Potions being wiped in the future.  
  
You can now stack the duration of your God Potions as long as your active God Potion duration is under 7 days. You no longer have to worry about wasting the remaining duration of your God Potion when you drink another one.  
  
The God Potion description has been updated to show that you can stack your God Potion duration as long as your active God Potion duration is under 7 days. It also features a better breakdown of the God Potion’s duration modifiers.  
  


![god-potion.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/god-potion-png.3289167/)

  
The timer on your God Potion is now automatically paused while you’re on your private island. Don’t worry, you can still enjoy the effects of a God Potion while on your private island, it’s just that you’d no longer lose any time on your God Potion while being on your private island. Nifty, eh?  
  


![private-island-paused-timer.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/private-island-paused-timer-png.3289169/)

  
Mixins can now be consumed by right-clicking them as long as you currently have an active God Potion and Cookie Buff. Consuming a mixin has the same effectiveness of brewing a mixin into three God Potions - it will give you effects for 3x the duration of a God Potion. If you don't have an active cookie buff, you can still brew mixins into your God Potions the same way as before.   
  


![deepterror-mixin.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/deepterror-mixin-png.3289170/)

  
If you have any Mixins that haven’t expired when your God Potion expires, they will be paused.  
  


![mixin-pause.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/mixin-pause-png.3289172/)

  
Last but not least, a new filter setting has been added to your /effects menu to easily figure out what effects you currently have.  
  


![effects-filter.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/effects-filter-png.3289173/)

  


### **►** Phasing out Enchantment Book Recipes​

All enchantments that can be obtained through the Enchantment Table have their book recipes replaced by a 25% EXP discount for that enchantment. This was done as enchantment books are no longer required to obtain those enchantments. For enchantment book exclusive enchants such as Replenish, those crafting recipes still remain and the collection requirements are the primary way you unlock that enchantment.

### **►** **General Changes** ​

### **Items and Equipment Changes** ​

#### **Combat Items** ​

  * Added a new recipe for an upgrade to the Bone Necklace, requiring Thorn Fragments.
  * Ender Bow’s ability cooldown has been reduced from 45 seconds to 5 seconds.
  * Explosive Bow can now be used in dungeons and acts the same as Superboom TNT.
  * Souls Rebound, Venom's Touch, and Spider Queen's Stinger can now be converted to dungeon items using Spider Essence.
  * Void Sword can now be converted to a dungeon item using Dragon Essence.



#### **Mining Items** ​

  * Added 2 new tutorial commissions to the Dwarven Mines.
  * The despawn timer of Powder Ghasts will now reset every time it gets hit.



#### **Misc. Items** ​

  * Bubblegum now buffs all Deployables instead of just Power Orbs.



### Other Changes​

#### Garden Changes​

  * Beth will now always give an offer when visiting your garden instead of disappearing after her initial dialogue.
  * Flowering Bouquet can now be put inside a composter providing 6k Organic Matter.
  * Enchanted crops can now be used to paste plots in the Garden.
    * Excess crops that have not been placed are returned to the player.
    * If the player happens to be offline when the paste finishes, the items are stored and returned the next time the player warps to their Garden.



### Misc. Changes​

  * **Added a little Bee pet next to Bea!**
  * Added an audible indicator when the Wither Skeleton jumps in the 'Test Of Control' Dojo challenge.
  * Added Enchanted Flint to the lore of the Large Enchanted Mining Sack.
  * Added Poison Sample to the Bazaar.
  * Added Polarvoid Books to the Bazaar.
  * Angry Archeologist has been renamed to Angry Arch**a** eologist for the sake of consistency with other Archaeologists in SkyBlock.
  * The following items are now salvageable:
    * Netherrack Looking Sunshade for 15 Crimson Essence
    * Pickonimbus 2000 for 5 Diamond Essence
    * Handy Blood Chalice for 15 Dragon Essence
    * Pocket Espresso Machine for 15 Dragon Essence
    * Treasure Talisman for 15 Gold Essence
    * Glacial Talisman for 15 Ice Essence
    * Tarantula Talisman for 50 Spider Essence
    * Scarf Studies for 15 Wither Essence
  * The reward message from the Generow NPC has been improved for clarity.
  * The Royal Resident now unlocks on the Garden after receiving the cake from him.
  * The Witherborn ability (from all variants of Wither Armor) will no longer attempt to attack Garden pests.
  * Updated most of the mythic pet coin values at George.



### **User Interface and Messaging Changes** ​

  * Added a "Claim Your Listings Only" button to the Manage Auctions screen for co-op profiles
  * Clicking the pets icon in your SkyBlock Menu now takes you to the pets menu.
  * Edited Tia's garden visitor dialogue.
  * George’s dialogue line when you hand in an exotic pet will now only show once per pet.
    * The taming level in the message 'You can now start working towards level 52' is now green.
  * The Personal Bank upgrade in the SkyBlock Menu will now show information about the next interest.
  * The Wizard will now show how long is left until your next free infusion.

### **►** **Bug Fixes** ​

#### Item Fixes​

  * Fixed Lava Rods sending missing requirement error messages twice.



#### Mining Fixes​

  * Fixed a few more custom ores in the Dwarven Mines not being breakable.
  * Fixed the Instant Transmission ability being usable in the Jungle Temple.



#### Achievement Fixes​

  * Fixed an inaccuracy in the description of the United in Blood achievement where it doesn’t explain that the achievement can also be completed with a Gilded Midas Staff.
  * Fixed an inaccuracy in the description of the Accessories Galore achievement incorrectly referencing the Accessory Bag as the “Talisman Bag”.



#### Misc. Fixes​

  * Fixed the auction house referring to coins as gold.
  * Fixed clicking anywhere inside an Item Frame giving an error saying 'You cannot take this item'.
  * Fixed Mana Vampire enchant showing irrelevant decimals in the enchantment’s description.
  * Fixed Void Sword referencing Ender Armor as “End Armor”.
