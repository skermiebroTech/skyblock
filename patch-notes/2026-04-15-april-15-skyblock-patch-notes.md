# [April 15] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Squash, Cropie, Helianthus, and Fermento showing double "Right-click to view recipes".
  * Fixed Spirit Mask second wind invulnerability from activating when in the Matriarch's Lair.
  * Fixed some Mythological Ritual carnival perks showing 0 when the event is not active.
  * Fixed Melon Armor set giving 2% at tier 2/4 instead of 12%.
  * Made shiny pigs much less likely to go through walls or over the sides of bridges.
  * Fixed Hub Rat's nametag not appearing.
  * Fixed Hub Rat's hitbox being partially obstructed.
  * Delayed when bait conservation chance activates from hitting the water to reeling in.
  * Improved the Pickobulus Ability's projectile collision detection.
  * Updated Pickobulus Ability sound effect.
  * Updated Jaeger's free fishing nets to be soulbound.
  * Fixed Jaeger and Auryon giving extra hunting tools after completing their quest.
  * Fixed Year of the Pig Potato and Pork rewards not counting toward collections.
  * Fixed Jotraeline Greatforge not knowing that the Abiphone Basic is the cheapest Abiphone.
  * Fixed Enderman Slayer Yang Glyphs sometimes being launched through walls.
  * Fixed Null Ovoid and other spawn egg items closing the crafting table.
  * Changed some Spawn Egg items to fit better with the updated textures.
  * Fixed Rusty selling the wrong Bioanalysis Talisman tier if you didn't claim the reward.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
