Hello!  
  
Welcome to **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.6**. This update contains permanent Spooky content in the form of The Great Spook, several additions and changes to Essence Shops, and much more!  
  
  
  
► **Great Spook 2023**  
This year, the arcane master known as the Tyashoi Alchemist re-emerges from the veil of mystery to aid players in banishing The Great Spook!  
  
Will you succeed or completely fail? Time will tell!  
  
Like the previous year, you have access to the 2021 and 2022 hunts and their items.  
However the 6 new hunts will be revealed day by day, starting from today.  
Click on the items in his shop to reveal the clues to find them, good luck!

Spoiler: SPOILER AHEAD

In a shocking twist, The Great Spook will unleash its terror on the 6th day, once all new hunts are completed. Turns out, the Tyashoi Alchemist is actually terrible at his job; he may have actually accelerated the arrival of The Great Spook!  
  
**New Mechanics: Primal Fears and Dark Candies**  
During the Great Spook Event, players will occasionally encounter Primal Fears while defeating mobs. These entities are simple to defeat but beware—they can manifest as YOUR primal fear. From Math Teachers to Commitment Phobia, each Primal Fear has a unique trick for defeating it.  
  


![vargul.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/vargul-png.3258309/)

  
  
**Milestones and Rewards**  
Defeating Primal Fears will drop Dark Candies, and you'll also find the Tombstone of Vargul the Unearthed at the back of Hub Island. Donating enough Dark Candies to Vargul will unlock various new items.  
  
Good luck, and don't get spooked!

  
**Extra Spooky Events Scheduled**  
Like the previous years, we added 6 extra **Spooky Events** in the **SkyBlock Calendar,** between the 28th and the 31st of October.  
Look inside the SkyBlock Calendar in your SkyBlock Menu if you don’t want to miss them!  
  


### ► **Essence Shops** ​

![diamond essence shop image.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/diamond-essence-shop-image-png.3258327/)

  
As part of this update, the final two essence shops will be making their long-awaited appearance. In addition, several changes have been made that will make other essences easier to get and also add a few new perks to existing shops. Read on to learn more!  


### ​

### Gold Essence Shop​

![gold essence shop.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/gold-essence-shop-png.3258310/)

> A direct descendant of King Midas himself, Marigold has finally arrived at the Royal Mines. Players who help her catch a pesky thief will be rewarded with access to the Gold Essence shop. Here, players may trade gold essence for a variety of perks that will help them in a number of ways, such as by granting bonus mining speed or increasing the chances of worms and scatha spawning when mining. 
> 
> Click to expand...

### ​

### Diamond Essence Shop​

![diamond essence shop.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/diamond-essence-shop-png.3258311/)

> Additionally, Gemma has set up shop in the Crystal Nucleus! Players who prove their worth by completing a Crystal Nucleus run will be rewarded with access to the Diamond Essence Shop. Here, players may trade in diamond essence to gain useful perks, such as bonus breaking power on diamond pickaxes, increased chances of fishing up diamond trophy fish, and a higher chance of spawning diamond goblins. Players may also purchase an item that can be applied in an anvil to upgrade any piece of Tier XII Perfect Armor to Tier XIII. 
> 
> Click to expand...

### ​

### Diamond Goblins​

![diamond goblin.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/diamond-goblin-png.3258312/)

> Diamond Goblins are an exceedingly rare type of Goblin that have a chance to spawn in place of Golden Goblins. When killed, they will always drop diamond essence, mithril powder, and gemstone powder. Some lucky players may find themselves dropping an item that can be used to upgrade a particular pet to mythic.

### **Other Changes to Essence Shops** ​

> We have also made several changes related to essence shops that give players new ways to gain essence and new perks to spend essence on. 
> 
> Click to expand...

  * **New Perk: Unbridled Rage** added to Dragon Essence shop. Increases Berserk class XP gain.
  * **New Perk: Toxophilite** added to Spider Essence shop. Increases Archer class XP gain.
    * When taking into account the perks of the two new essence shops, every dungeons class now has access to a perk that allows them to gain class XP faster.
  * Gold & Diamond Essence can now drop from Treasure Chests in the Crystal Hollows.
  * Golden Goblins may now spawn in the Crystal Hollows.
  * Goblin Eggs of all kinds can now be thrown and have varying chances of spawning a Golden Goblin:
    * 2% chance from Goblin Egg
    * 2.5% chance from Green Goblin Egg
    * 3.33% chance from Yellow Goblin Egg
    * 5% chance from Red Goblin Egg
    * 10% chance from Blue Goblin Egg
  * Golden Goblins now have a 0.1% chance of spawning when mining Gemstones, Mithril, or Titanium in the Crystal Hollows.
  * Golden Goblins now have a 0.01% chance of spawning when mining Hard Stone.
  * Golden Goblins no longer can spawn from cobblestone and vanilla ores.
  * Gold & Diamond Dungeon Heads are no longer salvageable.
  * A salvage function has now been added to the Spider Tamer NPC.
  * Dungeon secret loot now includes Ice Essence, Spider Essence, Diamond Essence, and Gold Essence.
  * A guide menu has been added to the Crystal Nucleus that tells players where to find each Gemstone Crystal.

**Dungeon** **Fixes**  


  * **Fixed Ragnarok's damage boost (Berserker class ability) working on all damage instead of melee only.**

    * It's a common strategy to abuse this bug to get Spirit Bows faster. While it is not intended for players to get bows in such quick succession, we have taken this opportunity to tweak the boss fight to make it playable without the bug. M4 now has the same spawning mechanics of 1 bear per spawn as F4, requiring slightly more mob kills to spawn each bear. This change is open to further adjustments to spawn the bears faster overall.
