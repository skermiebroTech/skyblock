# Regarding Event Rewards and Habanero Tactics

Hey Guys!  
  
We've decided to postpone the deletion of unclaimed event rewards for another 2 weeks.  
  
Weeks ago, we added a warning inside of the claim event rewards menu letting you know that from now on, after 10 SkyBlock years, your rewards would expire and would no longer be obtainable. We warned players through patch note threads and this menu, and today we rolled the change that officially made it a reality.  
  
We realized after receiving player feedback that the communication for this change may not have reached everyone. For this reason, we've decided to restore all event rewards, and delay this change for another 2 weeks.  
  
Upon logging in, from now on, if you have unclaimed event rewards, you will be notified in chat. Please make sure you claim your event rewards, and avoid leaving event rewards unclaimed. **On the 5th of August** , we will re-enable deletion of event rewards.  
  
We apologize for any confusion or panic this might have caused.  
  
This patch also includes the fix for **Habanero Tactics** not applying Magic Find buffs on kills; the buff is now inherent to the Magic Find calculation similar to Bestiary or Magma Lord Set bonuses.
