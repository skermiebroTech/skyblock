# [August 1] Thunder Revolution Update, QoL, and Bug Fixes

Hey all!  
  
Recently, we've worked closely with our community SkyBlock team, The Nerds, to fix a few bugs and make some improvements to existing content.  
  


### **► Bug Fixes**​

  * Fixed the removed enchantment, Telekinesis, still appearing in the enchantment guide.
  * Fixed the Wisp Pet's Extinguish ability not working in Kuudra fights.
  * Fixed the Compact enchantment not working on netherrack.
  * Fixed Odger's Tooth accessories stacking their Magic Power.
  * Fixed an exploit that caused the Matriarch to softlock indefinitely.
  * Fixed some miniboss loot displaying weirdly tilted.
  * Fixed a visual bug with Ashfang where the gravity bomb particles would move when a blaze is caught in it.
  * Fixed Transmission Tuner applying twice when used in an anvil. The max amount of Transmission Tuners that can be applied to an item has been increased to 4 to account for this.
  * Fixed sacks that are not owned being able to be opened from Collection menus.



### **► Quality of Life**​

  * Added options to disable Sea Creature and Ability Cooldown chat messages, as well as an option to disable Ability Cooldown sounds.
    * To access these options, open the _SkyBlock Menu_ , click on _Settings_ , then click on _Personal_. There are buttons to open the _Sounds_ menu and the _Chat Feedback_ menu.
  * Added auto-pickup to miniboss loot.
    * Miniboss loot will now be picked up automatically a few seconds before the boss podium disappears or if the players leave the server.
  * Blessings and loot are now _much easier_ to pick up.
    * You should no longer have to briefly pause on top of drops to ensure you pick them up. Enjoy zooming by drops with 400 speed!
    * This affects Dungeons and Crimson Isle minibosses.  


![bosslootpickup.gif](https://hypixel.net/attachments/bosslootpickup-gif.3050758/)

  * Added the current health bonus to the New Year Cake Bag.
    * This was something small, but a lot of people were asking for it.

  * Added some missing items as well as the Slayer minion items to the Slayer Sack.
    * Added Undead Catalyst, Revenant Catalyst, Spider Catalyst, Null Atom, Raw Soulflow, Soulflow, and Crude Gabagool.  


![1659041185986.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1659041185986-png.3050773/)

  * Added an _Insert Inventory_ button to the Sack of Sacks.
    * This will take all available items from your inventory and insert them into your Sacks.

### **► Balancing**​

  * After tons of feedback from the Nerds, we have completely revamped the methods for charging the Empty Thunder Bottle.
    * Our goal was to make the bottle charge passively while doing normal tasks, but also have several methods to specifically farm for Thunder Charge. You can view all of the new methods on our Official Wiki Page, [**here**](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Empty_Thunder_Bottle).
    * Thunder boss will now spawn several _Sparks_ when killed, which can be collected with an Empty Thunder Bottle.
      * Previously, one of the best ways to charge the Thunder Bottle was from soaking damage from the Thunder boss until it despawns. We wanted to keep this method alive while also allowing players to farm Thunder kills.
  * Thunder Armor is no longer Soulbound to be consistent with Magma Lord Armor.
  * The Pulse Ring accessory has been given a few new perks.
    * This includes a small Magic Find boost as well as a Thunder Charge multiplier for the Empty Thunder Bottle.  


![1659045037803.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1659045037803-png.3050813/)

  * A few issues with Ashfang have been fixed.
    * We've fixed an issue where Ashfang Followers would get stuck on ledges when being pulled in by Gravity Bombs.
    * In addition, we've flattened the Ashfang arena to make it easier to line up shots for the Blazing Soul projectiles.
