# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.21.2 - Year 400 Raffle Event

![skyblock-400.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/skyblock-400-png.3387699/)

  
  
Hello all, and welcome to Hypixel SkyBlock Year 400! What a party we have in store for you, featuring new century cakes, dozens of prizes ranging from our new and esteemed Paint Drying Simulator to the elusive Shattered Locket, gift bags, and more!  
  
Read on for all the details!  
  
  


### **► Year 400 Raffle Changes**​

In celebration of 400 years of Hypixel SkyBlock, we will be rerunning [Year 300's Raffle event](https://hypixel.net/threads/skyblock-year-300.5481420/) alongside a number of changes that aim to make the event more enjoyable for all.  


  * More Medium Rewards and Major Rewards are being handed out than in Year 300.
  * All Raffle Rewards are now entirely randomized. When you win a minor reward, it can be anything from that pool.
  * Raffle Ticket gain from simply being online now has a daily cap of 24 tickets (12 hours).
  * Rezzus offers 63 daily quests. Completing a daily task rewards you with 1 raffle ticket and 1 slice of cake.
  * Slices of cake come in a number of different flavors:
    * Slices of Blueberry Cake must be fed to members of **BLUE** team.
    * Slices of Lemon Cheesecake must be fed to members of **YELLOW** team.
    * Slices of Strawberry Shortcake must be fed to members of **PINK** team.
    * Slices of Matcha Cake must be fed to members of **GREEN** team.
    * Slices of Red Velvet Cake must be fed to members of **RED** team.
  * Feeding someone a slice of cake rewards you both with a raffle ticket and some random loot!
  * This loot can be coins, more raffle tickets, unique items, and even century cakes!

### **► Raffle Rewards**​

  


![Raffle Rewards.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/raffle-rewards-png.3387655/)

  
Unlike Year 300, where each raffle randomly selected several rewards from each tier, leading to some players not wanting to participate in certain raffles because their rewards weren't as valuable. This time around, each raffle will simply drop a guaranteed number of **MINOR** , **MEDIUM** , and **MAJOR** rewards. The reward is randomly selected from the corresponding loot pool only when a player goes to claim their reward from this tier.

### **► New Raffle Milestones**​

  


![Raffle Milestones.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/raffle-milestones-png.3387656/)

  
The Raffle Milestones system has been adjusted this time around! As you acquire Raffle Tickets, you'll unlock new Raffle Milestones, which award Slices of Century Cake, Emblems, a Pet Skin, Century the Fish, and even a Giftbag that will allow you to pick out a specific minor reward from a selection!  
  
  


### **► Participation Rewards**​

Compensation Rewards have been adjusted this time around, and are now known as Participation Awards. In addition to all players once again receiving a memento at the end of the event commemorating the team that gathers the most tickets, raffle compensation has been adjusted:  


  * Failure to win a Speed/Daily Raffle grants +1 Cake Slice for every 5 tickets you entered without winning.
  * **All** players who contribute 50+ tickets to The Big One will receive 1 Century Cake as a participation award once it rolls.

  
  
And that's all for today! Thanks for sticking around to see Year 400, and we hope to see you at the Year 500 celebrations when they inevitably roll around!  


### **► Hotfixes**​

We are actively monitoring the release and rolling out some minor bug fixes, which may not be seen immediately. We'll keep this list updated as we add more fixes:  


  * Disabled Recombobulators on Runebooks.
  * Fixed Gifts of Learning from raffles being corrupted and unable to be opened.
  * Increased the eaten Cake Slice limit from 20 to 50.
  * Fixed Daily Tasks and Cake Slices eaten per day not resetting at the correct time.
    * They will now reset at 12am ~~EST~~ UTC for the rest of the event.
  * Fixed the Daily Task time until reset counter showing the wrong time.
  * Fixed being unable to gain any playtime tickets on a new day after reaching the daily cap.
  * Fixed occasionally not marking a Daily Task as complete.
