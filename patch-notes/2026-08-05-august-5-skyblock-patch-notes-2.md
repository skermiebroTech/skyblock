# [August 5] SkyBlock Patch Notes #2

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed potion effects sometimes not running out.
  * Fixed the Daily Wishes HOTF perk not triggering when breaking logs.
  * Fixed being able to spawn infinite Gimmiegolds in the Critter Safari.
  * Fixed a few spots where you could get stuck in the Critter Safari.
  * Blacklisted some glitched blocks from newer Minecraft versions such as Polished Blackstone Button, Concrete Powder, Pale Moss Carpet and such.  
**NOTE** : We understand that this will upset some players who collected these items, but due to these items being involved in various exploits and crashes we've decided to remove them to prevent future issues from popping up.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
