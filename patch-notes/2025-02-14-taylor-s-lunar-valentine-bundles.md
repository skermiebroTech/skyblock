### **► Other Info**​

Taylor's Valentine & Lunar Bundles will be available alongside the skins in Taylor's Winter Bundle for the rest of its runtime during the year.   


  * Taylor's Valentine, Lunar, and Winter Bundles will be available until **Saturday, March 22nd.**
  * As mentioned previously, the items **currently present** in these bundles**will return** in next year's respective bundles! 

In addition, we made a few bug fixes:  


  * Added a reminder to collect unclaimed Year 400 rewards.
  * Made Necron's Ladders and Diana Bookshelves one-time consumable items. If the item reaches its max potential, you can consume it to gain its permanent bonus. The item will be destroyed in the process.
    * We have added both Necron's Ladder and Diana's Bookshelf to Shen auctions, at a cost of 75M.
  * Made Necron Ladders require 3 Necron Handles instead of 4.
  * Only 1 Necron's Ladder or Diana's Bookshelf can be increased at once, and the item with the highest count on it (that is not at its maximum) will be increased. If they are all the same progress, the first one found will be increased.
  * Fixed some typos in the Fruit Bowl and Century Party Invitation items.
  * Placed Cake Counters no longer show '300' on the side.
