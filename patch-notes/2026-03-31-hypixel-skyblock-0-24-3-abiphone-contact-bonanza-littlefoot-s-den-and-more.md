### **► Littlefoot's Den**​

Littlefoot's Den is a new Glacite Mineshaft variant. If you are ever so lucky to happen upon one, you'll notice Littlefoot lingering around; it is his home, after all. This little guy packs quite a punch once he gets over his fear of outsiders, so come ready for a fight.  
  
  


![Littlefoot.jpg](https://hypixel.net/attachments/littlefoot-jpg.3557898/)

  
  
Littlefoot can be hunted to drop the Littlefoot Shard, an Attribute which, at max, grants +0.5 Gemstone Spread per 20 Cold Resistance while in the Glacite Mineshafts. And, if you're lucky, he might just drop the Littlefoot Fluff, an item which can be used to upgrade the Baby Yeti pet to MYTHIC.  
  


![MythicBabyYeti.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/mythicbabyyeti-png.3557871/)

  
It is also worth noting that Littlefoot sometimes ventures beyond the confines of his den - all Glacite Maniacs have a base 1% (increased by Tracking) chance to be replaced by a Littlefoot.

### **► Reforge Anvil Discontinuation**​

**

![reforge.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/reforge-png.3557879/)

**​

  
  
Starting today, Reforge Anvils have been removed from Hypixel SkyBlock, for the purposes of simplicity. Everything you previously needed to use a reforge anvil for (applying reforges, recombobulators, etc) can now be done in a regular anvil.  


  * Replaced the Reforge Anvil recipe at Obsidian Collection IX with +1 Mining Fortune & +50,000 Mining XP
  * Removed Reforge Anvil purchase from Smithmonger
  * Removed the Reforge Anvil from Malik's menu
  * Removed all Reforge Anvil NPCs from Private Islands
  * Added a 2m coin NPC sell price to Reforge Anvils
  * Added +1 Mining Fortune to Obsidian Collection V, VII, and VIII
  * Adjusted Reforge Anvil itemlore to show its sole purpose is now to be NPC sold
  * Adjusted itemlore of all "Combinable in Reforge Anvil" items to now say "Combinable in Anvil".
  * Converted all Reforge Anvils on Public Islands, such as the SkyBlock Hub, into regular Anvils
