# [December 15] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about today's fixes following the 0.24 Garden Greenhouse update!  
  


  * Fixed an issue where new profiles joining the garden were getting wrongly refunded for Garden Upgrades they don't have
  * Increased the Rarefinder Chip's drop chance
  * Fixed Sunflower and Wild Rose being placeable as full crops in the Barn plot
  * Fixed Sunflower, Moonflower and Wild Rose not being placeable in the Greenhouse
  * Removed Legacy Item warning for items that have been removed from a sack but aren't legacy, also changed the warning message on the item element in the sack
  * Corrected NPC sell prices for Red Mushroom Blocks, Enchanted Golden Carrots and Enchanted Cookies
  * Removed Wheat from Enchanted Cookie recipe
  * Increased Farming XP from Sunflower, Moonflower and Wild Rose outside the Greenhouse
  * Made the Beth visitor not request Sunflower, Moonflower or Wild Rose
  * Fixed being able to paste more than the maximum amount of Greenhouse plots allowed
  * Fixed being able to save plots as they are pasting in some cases

We are also aware of and working on a couple high priority bugs:  


  * Past Jacob's Contest rewards being inaccurate (either missing entirely, or extra rewards)
  * Carpenter sometimes gives the Blueprint without triggering the quest
    * This can be fixed by accepting more visitors after him. He comes back more frequently than most visitors to give another Blueprint if you don't use it

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
