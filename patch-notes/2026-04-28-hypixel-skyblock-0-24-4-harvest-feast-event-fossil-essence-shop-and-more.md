# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.24.4 - Harvest Feast Event, Fossil Essence Shop, and more!

![banner.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/banner-png.3564833/)

  
  
Hello! Today we are releasing a new Hypixel SkyBlock update which introduces a new yearly farming event: the Harvest Feast! Along with this we've added Fossil Essence and a corresponding shop to spend it in. Keep reading for all the details!

![feast_chef_ted.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/feast_chef_ted-png.3566686/)

### **➥ Overview**​

![harvest_feast_menu.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/harvest_feast_menu-png.3566697/)

  
  
Feast Chef Ted is cooking up a massive Communal Stew for everyone in SkyBlock, and he needs your help to season it! Harvest Feasts take place every SkyBlock Year for 3 months: Early Autumn, Autumn, and Late Autumn.  
  
During each month, 3 crops will enter their "harvesting season". Harvesting these crops during this period has a small chance to drop Seasoning for the stew and a new crafting material unique to each crop.  
  


### **➥ Seasonings**​

Seasoning is a **RARE CROP** that has a 1/2,250 chance of dropping from in-season crops when harvesting them. It's not a physical item—it'll automatically be donated to the Communal Stew in The Hub.  
  
Each Seasoning you donate to the stew will contribute towards your Donation Tiers.  
  


### **➥ Donation Tiers + Celestial Mason Jars**​

There are five Donation Tiers each Harvest Feast at 5, 25, 75, 150, and 250 Seasonings cumulatively donated. Each Donation Tier reached will earn you Feast Flasks, a critical ingredient for crafting the new Celestial Mason Jar, the most powerful Mixin in Hypixel SkyBlock!  
  


![celestial_mason_jar.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/celestial_mason_jar-png.3566688/)

  
Hitting the max Donation Tier will also earn you a Commemorative Mason Jar for that event.  
  


### **➥ New Stat: Overbloom**​

![overbloom.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/overbloom-png.3566698/)

  
Many of the items in this new event reward a new stat: Overbloom! You can think of Overbloom as Farming's equivalent to Magic Find: it increases your chances of dropping **RARE CROPS**! Every 1 Overbloom you have adds +1% to the drop rate of all **RARE CROPS** , so 100 Overbloom doubles your chances!

### **➥ Crop Drops**​

Outside of just dropping Seasoning, in-season crops will also have a 1/18,000 chance of dropping a new crafting material unique to that crop. Each crafting material is also considered a **RARE CROP** , much like Seasoning.  
  
Each of these materials is used to craft or obtain 1-3 new items.

**► Grand Feasts**  


### **➥ Overview**​

![feast_baker_scott.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/feast_baker_scott-png.3566710/)

  


Alchemist photo bomb​

Mayor Finnegan now has a new perk: "Grand Feast"! This replaces the Harvest Feast with a version that lasts an entire SkyBlock Year, in addition to opening the Grand Bakery for its duration. Feast Baker Scott runs the Grand Bakery and will appear at the market stall next to Ted during Grand Feasts.  
  


### **➥ Grand Bakery**​

![grand_bakery.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/grand_bakery-png.3566711/)

  
During a Grand Feast, whenever you drop a Seasoning from an in-season crop, it'll also give you a Kernel—a new currency you can spend at the Grand Bakery in The Hub to "bake" new rewards!  


  * Freshly Baked Accessories
    * Grants +1-5 Overbloom

    * Artifact: 250 Kernels + Ring

    * Heirloom: 1,000 Kernels + Relic
  * Feast I Books
    * Applied to Farming Tools
    * Grants +0.5-2.5 Overbloom
    * Costs 25 Kernels
  * Finn's Focaccia
    * Consumable
    * Grants +5 Overbloom for the rest of the Grand Feast
    * Costs 50 Kernels
  * Ted's Contact Baked In Bread

  * Scott's Contact Baked In Bread

    * Is a super cool thing to give to somebody

  


### ➥ Removal of Pelt-pocalypse​

With the introduction of Finnegan's new Grand Feast perk we're retiring his old "Pelt-pocalypse" perk. Talbot's Shop and the Horse Trapper Animal are now permanently available. In addition to this, we've slightly reworked the Trapper's Crest accessory. It now upgrades an additional time and buffs the amount of Pelts you drop to compensate for the removal of the Mayor perk.  
  
  
****► Fossil Essence Shop****  


![fossil_essence_shop.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fossil_essence_shop-png.3566647/)

  
Fossil Dust has been turned into Fossil Essence, which can be spent in a brand-new shop! The Fossil Essence Shop is accessed through Researcher Beryl, a new NPC that can be found in the top floor of the Fossil Research Center.  
  
Perks:  


  * _Sleight Of Hand I_ : Start the Fossil Excavator with an additional charge
  * _Chilled to the Bone I-X_ : Increases your chances of fishing up Skeleton Fish in the Crimson Isle by 2-20%
  * _Dwarven Expertise I-X_ : Grants +2-20 Dwarven Metal Fortune
  * _Cut Loose I-V_ : Killing a ❆ Glacial mob while inside a Glacite Mineshaft grants +0.5-2.5 Cold Resistance (up to x10)
  * _Prehistorian I-X_ : Increases the chance of a Fossil Dye spawning in the Fossil Excavator by 1-10%
  * _Resourceful I-V_ : Grants a 1-5% chance to not consume keys when looting a Frozen Corpse

  
In total, you will need 66,250 Fossil Essence to max out the shop.  
  
Additionally, you are now able to purchase a Chisel through this menu for 5 Fossil Essence. Suspicious Scrap is now purchased here for 100 Fossil Essence as well.  
  


### **➥ Fossil Muncher** ​

![fossil_muncher.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fossil_muncher-png.3566648/)

  
Meet the Fossil Muncher, a new NPC found in the Glacite Tunnels. Every 24 hours he'll ask for a random Fossil. Giving him this Fossil will reward you with 25,000 Glacite Powder and 100 Fossil Essence.

******► Farming Toolkit******  


![farming_toolkit.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/farming_toolkit-png.3566650/)

  
One of the most popular suggestions we've gotten recently is for a farming equivalent to the Hunting Toolkit. It's finally here! Consuming the item now grants access to the /farmingtoolkit menu, where you can store your Farming Tools. You can also access this menu by shift right-clicking the Farming Tool you are currently holding, just like the Hunting Toolkit.

********► Update FAQ******  
  
What's coming next month?**  
Early next month we want to release a fairly large QOL patch and an early game quest to teach players about account security. Later in the month we plan on releasing the Lotus Atoll, a brand new fishing island. You can read the sneak peek for that island [HERE](https://hypixel.net/threads/sneak-peek-lotus-atoll.6000454/).  
  
  
**Where are the Minister perks from the Aura event?**  
The team is still actively working on implementing all Minister perks from Mayor Aura's event. One or two perks have already been released, as you might have seen with April's Secret Bingo event, and most of the remaining perks will be released bundled together in one update.  
  
  
**When can we expect the second Foraging Island?**  
We are very deep into development of the second island of Galatea - Torrhus Canyon - and are hoping to release it later this year. You can expect a Sneak Peek for it over the next month or two.

**What happened to the Guardian Slayer update?**  
The Guardian Slayer update always required Lotus Atoll to release before it, but now that we have access to latest-version tech we would like to take some time to revisit the Slayer, which was originally built using only 1.8 mechanics. We still plan on releasing it later this year.  
  
  
**What happened to the Great Glacite Lake update?**  
With the Lotus Atoll and Desecrated Shrine (Guardian Slayer) updates currently planned for fishing, we thought that yet another fishing update so soon would've grown tiresome. Because of this we ultimately split the Great Glacite Lake update into a number of parts, many of which you've already seen released (Frozen Corpse RNG Meter & Milestones, Littlefoot's Den, Fossil Essence Shop). We still plan to release the Great Glacite Lake part of the update, which contains the next **TWO** pieces of BiS Mining Equipment, Glacite Sea Creatures, new Mineshafts, and an Offering system for the lake that makes it easier to target certain mineshafts, in the future. We apologize for the delay.
