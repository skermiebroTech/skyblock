# [October 10] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
  
After yesterday's patch, we received feedback from the community about Flower of Truth / Bouquet of Lies working on Ghosts being too powerful. We internally reviewed the change and decided to revert it.  


  * Reverted a change that allowed Flower of Truth and Bouquet of Lies to damage Ghosts.

Unlike updates with more changes, this one will slowly roll out to SkyBlock throughout the day, so please be patient. Thanks!
