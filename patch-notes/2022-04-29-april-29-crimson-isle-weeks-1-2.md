# [April 29] Crimson Isle Weeks 1 & 2

The Crimson Isle update has now been out for just over a week so here is a collection of all the fixes we've made since then!  
  
**Bug Fixes**  


  * Fixed Wheat Minions not producing Wheat.
  * Fixed Jump Pads not working in The Park.
  * Fixed being able to use teleport abilities during the mage council meeting.
  * Fixed Odger saying some quest dialogue out of order.
  * Fixed an issue with the Quiver not detecting arrows correctly.
  * Added caps to the Snail Pet perks.
  * Fixed Final Destination not working when many players were around.
  * Fixed certain Rune trails not appearing.
  * Fixed Minion spawned mobs appearing outside the Minion spawn area.
  * Fixed many issues with the Kuudra fight and Cannons.
  * Mushroom Cow Pet perk "Mushroom Eater" no longer works on player-placed blocks.
  * Fixed the Kuudra fight reputation requirement requiring more than the set amount of reputation.
  * Fixed another bug with the Master Enchanter achievement so now it actually works!
  * Disabled Loot Share on private islands and dungeons.
  * Glass is once again no longer capped in the Builder shop.
  * Fixed Wither Spectres having multiple health bars.
  * Fixed being able to corrupt the Quazzi and Typoeus slayer bosses.
  * Fixed Kat not calculating pet upgrades correctly.
  * Fixed purchasing the new minions not counting towards minion progress.
  * Fixed items displaying the incorrect mana cost when enchanted with Ultimate Wise
  * Fixed a rare pet stack caused by rescue missions.
  * Fixed the Rabbit Hat not working everywhere on private islands.
  * Fixed Sulphur Ore not sending a message when using too weak of a tool to mine it.
  * Fixed townboard quests being unable to pick quests under certain circumstances.
  * Fixed mana enchantments giving bonuses when they shouldn't.
  * Fixed Trophy Fishing using the wrong pet perk boost internally.
  * Fixed some instances where mobs would revive themselves in a "half dead" state.
  * Fixed certain non-dungeon items being able to be converted into dungeon items.
  * Fixed a lot of broken Fairy Soul locations.
  * Fixed not being able to donate starred armor to the museum.
  * Fixed the Compact enchantment giving regular Enchanted Sand when breaking Red Sand.
  * The Mages Guild Dean will now only consume the Batphone given from the Crimson Isle main quest.
  * Fixed the Withers from Witherborn armor being seen as hostile enemies when escorting Elle and Ugo.
  * Added fishing XP for some missing lava fishing items.
  * All Trophy Fish now reward fishing XP.
  * Fixed certain slayer quests not loot sharing correctly.
  * Fixed being unable to complete some townboard quests.
  * Fixed mana bombs staying active after killing Kuudra.
  * Fixed dead team members being unable to open loot chests at the end of the Kuudra fight.
  * Fixed two empty lines in a row showing on the scoreboard in the Kuudra fight.
  * Fixed Elle talking about orbs when everyone is dead in the Kuudra fight.
  * Fixed Dungeon mob drops not applying stat boosts and enchantments correctly.
  * Made the Matriarch minigame only work with melee attacks.
  * Fixed Dungeon Secrets not counting when you were using a Personal Deletor.
  * Fixed races kicking players once they reached and completed a certain stage.
  * Fixed Matriarch knockback slowly drowning players whilst in the zone.
  * Fixed a bug where party members could bypass the Crimson Isle requirement level.
  * Fixed Hot Armor not getting set bonus from previous types.
  * Added Salvages to Flaming Fist.
  * Fixed a variety of Dojo stat issues.
  * Fixed a variety of crafting recipes.
  * Fixed the Common Dolphin Pet having the Legendary abilities.
  * Fixed Terror Armor Arrows not being affected by certain buffs.
  * Fixed even more typos!
  * A lot of performance improvements!
  * And many more that didn't make the list...

**QOL**  


  * All items are now fireproof on the Crimson Isle.
  * Added baits to Bazaar.
  * Re-added Relenter's NPC into the Village ![:\)](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/joypixels/assets/8.0/png/unicode/64/1f642.png)
  * Auto Smelt now produces coal from more logs.
  * Added Maxwell and Jacobus to the Stranded mode.
  * Certain Trophy Fish items now show they can be used as bait.
  * Guards in the Crimson Isle now give warning messages before attacking.
  * The townboard daily quests will no longer give quests for disabled features/tasks.
  * The Dojo Mastery challenge now spawns target time holograms.
  * A 2nd Batphone can now be taken from Elle during the Crimson Isle main quest.
  * Swapped the response order from the Queen's Guards to make it less likely to miss click on the option that kills you.
  * Mycelium and Red Sand minions now only require 1 player in a co-op to have the required reputation.
  * Updated the Guildford race rewards to be more clear.

That's it for right now folks, thanks for reporting them and glhf!  
_Remember that this list is not exhaustive and there are many fixes not documented._
