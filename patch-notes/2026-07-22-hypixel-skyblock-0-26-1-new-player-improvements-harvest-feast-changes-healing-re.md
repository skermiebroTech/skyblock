## **► New Player Improvements** ​

One of the goals we've been working towards this year is improving SkyBlock's new player experience. This is just the first of many, _many_ , steps we'll be taking to smooth out the game's early progression and make it less intimidating.  
  


### **➥ Beginner Sacks** ​

![beginner_husbandry_sack.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/beginner_husbandry_sack-png.3594723/)

Sacks are one of the best QOL items in the game, so we wanted to make them more accessible to earlier-game players. This is where Beginner Sacks come in, which can only hold up to 640 of each item.  
  
To obtain them you can either craft them (recipes unlocked at Tier II of various collections), or you can buy them for 250 Coins from their respective skill merchant.

## ▶ Healing Revamp​

Healing Revamp changes how Healing works in SkyBlock, where Vitality is reworked to be a resource stat akin to mana. Similar to mana costs for abilities in general, healing abilities have been reworked to cost Vitality.  
  
Here’s a general summary of why we’re implementing these changes.  


Spoiler: The Healing Conundrum

  * Healing is very powerful in SkyBlock.
    * There’s a lot of healing available in the game on account of:
      * Vitality as a healing multiplier.
      * Items that double healing.
      * No limit on the number of healing abilities used.
      * Healing costs only mana and an inventory/armor/equipment slot.
  * The abundance of healing leads to damage not “sticking” to players in SkyBlock.
    * Damage dealt to players can be healed back very quickly, as if the damage never happened.
  * Due to this, large amounts of damage are required to compensate for the availability of healing;
    * To threaten consequences (such as death) to the player, enemies need to do more damage.
    * This leads to mechanics that result in 1-shots, because even if you’re at 1% health, you can heal back to full quickly enough before the next attack lands, rendering even such powerful attacks useless.
    * This also creates big jumps within content difficulty, where a slight increase in combat stats can change your fight experience from “totally impossible” to “laughably easy”.
    * It also creates arbitrarily high barriers to attempt certain content, despite that not being necessary.
      * You can read more about the effects of the last two on this design thread.
  * Another consequence is that there is no design space for new healing effects in the game.
    * In a world where healing costs a pitiful amount of spare mana, every new healing item added is free healing. Adding ‘free’ healing is not sustainable.



As part of this update, we have reduced the general amount of healing available. After all, there was previously no restriction to how often you could activate a healing ability as long as you had mana, and mana is not a resource that’s particularly restrictive in SkyBlock. You can read more about why we want to reduce healing in the game [here](https://hypixel.net/threads/6089643/), and [here](https://hypixel.net/threads/design-thread-healing-revamp.6124663).  
  
Rest assured, the goal of these changes is not to make the game harder. With the reduction in healing available, we’ve also reduced the damage of mobs and mechanics all throughout SkyBlock.  
  
We have made quite a lot of adjustments throughout the alpha session, but we’re still open to making damage changes and other adjustments after release — if there are particular mobs or content that you’re struggling with (that you weren’t previously), please let us know in the comments, along with some relevant information to help us understand where you’re coming from.  
  


### ➥ Functionality of Vitality​

  * Vitality no longer boosts healing as a multiplier.
    * Having more Vitality still increases the amount of Healing you have, just in a different way.
    * The more Vitality you have, the more frequently you can activate healing items.
  * Players have a base Vitality stat of 100.
    * The more Vitality you have from various sources, the more Vitality you will have available.
    * In other words, Vitality is a still stat you want to build if you want to heal more — having more Vitality means you can activate more healing abilities!
  * Vitality is now a resource akin to Mana.
    * Your Vitality stat defines the maximum amount of Vitality (the resource) you have.
    * Activating an ability that costs Vitality depletes the resource.
    * The depleted Vitality will regenerate every second.
  * Vitality regenerates at a rate of 5% per second, based on your Vitality stat.
    * If I have 100 Vitality, I gain +5 Vitality per second.
    * If I have 120 Vitality, I gain +6 Vitality per second.
    * If I have 200 Vitality, I gain 10 Vitality per second.
  * When you first use an ability that costs Vitality, a stat message unlock will appear, explaining how Vitality works.

  * After unlocking the Vitality stat, it will always be displayed in your status bar.

Spoiler: Power Orbs

#### Power Orbs​

Power Orbs retain the 50% of max mana cost to cast and gain an additional cost of 50 Vitality to cast.  
  
The original ‘percentage’ heal for x% HP per second has been separated into two parts, Health Regen (stat) and Flat Healing, as follows:  


  * Radiant Power Orb:⠀⠀⠀⠀25 Health Regen
  * Mana Flux Power Orb:⠀⠀50 Health Regen
  * Overflux Power Orb:⠀⠀⠀100 Health Regen
  * Plasmaflux Power Orb:⠀125 Health Regen

Each Power Orb now gives ‘flat’ HP/s as follows:  


  * Radiant Power Orb: 25 HP/s
  * Mana Flux Power Orb: 30 HP/s
  * Overflux Power Orb: 40 HP/s
  * Plasmaflux Power Orb: 50 HP/s

There are two more changes to power orbs:  


  * The HP/s healing provided to teammates is halved.
  * Power orbs no longer give Vitality and Mending.



Spoiler: Justification

Separating Power Orb’s healing to Health Regen and Flat Healing allows us to retain limited percentage healing from Power Orbs, while controlling the majority of healing generated by Power Orbs with flat values.  
  
Halving the healing of Power Orbs provided to teammates is necessary, as Power Orbs provide too much healing for the measly cost of 50 Vitality from 1 person. If someone wants to do a healer build, they can stack mending to increase the healing from Power Orbs to their teammates.  
  
The alternative to this is to have power orbs no longer heal teammates outright, else healing in multiplayer content becomes trivial.  
  
The removal of Vitality and Mending off of Power Orbs allows us to design future Power Orbs that center more around those stats (especially Mending).  
  
Power Orbs are currently strong enough for Mana Regeneration alone as is.

Spoiler: Wither Shield

#### ️Wither Impact — Wither Shield​

  * Wither Shield’s absorption is now determined by your catacombs level instead of Crit Damage.
    * The shield’s absorption is calculated using (12 + cata_lvl × 0.32) × 50 = 600 + 16 × cata_lvl
    * (12 + cata_lvl × 0.32) is the “Conversion Rate”.
      * The higher your conversion rate, the more healing you get per Vitality consumed.
      * In this case, the higher your Catacombs level, the more absorption you get out of the shield.



Catacombs Level| Conversion Rate| Total Absorption  
---|---|---  
25| 20| 1000  
30| 21.6| 1080  
35| 23.2| 1160  
40| 24.8| 1240  
45| 26.4| 1320  
50| 28| 1400  
  
  * Activating Wither Shield costs 50 Vitality
    * Not having enough Vitality does not prevent you from casting Wither Impact - it still deals damage, it just doesn’t put up the shield.
  * Wither Shield retains 10% damage reduction while the ability is active.
  * Wither Shield will no longer heal the player when it expires.
    * Instead of the healing, a Vitality refund is given based on how much of the shield is used - wasting no Vitality.

## ▶ Damage Changes​

A big part of Healing Revamp is adjusting the amount of damage dealt throughout the whole of SkyBlock to ensure that content is at a good state despite reduced healing availability.  


### ➥ Slayers​

After continuous testing and feedback on Alpha , we have decided to grant some healing after you kill a slayer boss. Slayer bosses are currently balanced around you having access to full HP and healing resources.  
  
While this healing effect isn’t a full heal, this should let players heal to full before their next fight much easier, and also help dealing with coccooned slayer bosses.  


  * Healing per second is determined by your Slayer Tier
    * T1: 40
    * T2: 80
    * T3: 120
    * T4: 160
    * T5: 200
  * Duration of healing scales with your Slayer Level
    * Base: 6s
    * Slayer Level 2: 7s
    * Slayer Level 4: 8s
    * Slayer Level 6: 9s
    * Slayer Level 8: 10s

Here’s some examples to help grasp the concept:  


  * If I have not done a slayer before, and I did T1 Zombie, I’ll regenerate 40 health for 6 seconds.
  * If I’m Slayer Level 5 and doing T3 Spider, I’ll regenerate 120 health per second for 8 seconds.
  * If I'm Slayer Level 7 and doing T4 Enderman, I'll regenerate 160 health per second for 9 seconds.

Note that this healing does not happen while you’re in The Rift.
