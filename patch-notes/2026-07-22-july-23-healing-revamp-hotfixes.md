# [July 23] Healing Revamp Hotfixes

Hello!   
  
We have a couple hotfixes for Healing Revamp:   


  * Reverted the "Heal on kill' on Berserk to be "Heal on hit".
  * Adjusted Berserk's Melee Damage buff to be multiplicative instead of additive.
  * Fixed Dropships dealing less damage than intended.
  * Fixed Goldor dealing too much damage in both his Sword Throw and TNT.
  * Fixed Pooch Sword not applying a multiplicative boost when it's the standard for Slayer weapons.
  * Fixed Tarantula Chestplate and Primordial Chestplate not having the Anti-Toxin Ability.
  * Adjusted the "average damage" of Tarantula Slayer from 3,900 to 2,200 (T5).
  * Nerfed Backstab damage per tier to 20/30%/40%/60%/80%.
  * Reduced the damage of bats to 0.1 (T4)/0.2%(T5) of your max HP.

If you have a bug to report to us following these changes, please do so [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/)
