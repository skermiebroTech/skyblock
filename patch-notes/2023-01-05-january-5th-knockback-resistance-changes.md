# [January 5th] Knockback Resistance changes

Hi all,  
  
Today we have rolled a small change to how the knockback immunity effect works in SkyBlock. This changes:  


  1. Some knockback immunity items not always cancelling knockback,
  2. Knockback immunity overriding other knockback reduction/immunity effects.

This aims to fix situations where, for example, if you unequipped a Slime Hat in Dungeons as a Tank, you would lose your knockback resistance, or if you were hit multiple times at once, you would take knockback through your resistance.  
  
This change affects the following:  


  * Slime Hat
  * Dwarf Turtle Shelmet
  * Unflippable Turtle Pet Perk
  * Dungeons Tank KB Resistance

This change also fixes Slime knockback not being cancelled by these items/effects.  
  
**We will be monitoring what effect this change has on the game and will make follow up adjustments if we feel necessary.**
