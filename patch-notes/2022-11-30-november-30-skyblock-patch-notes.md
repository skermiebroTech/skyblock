# [November 30] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  
Please note that this is a pre-update patch. New content has been added that is not yet accessible. This content will be announced with a news post when it is activated on December 1st.  
  


  * Some zone messages have been altered
  * Altered Snow Blaster rate of fire
  * New Sea Creatures have been added
  * Gift loot tables have been altered
  * The Advent Calendar may appear in the SB menu before it is activated on December 1st, but it will not be accessible yet.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
