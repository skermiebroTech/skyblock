# SkyBlock - Year 500 Century Celebration

![sb-year500.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-year500-png.3587235/)

  
  
Hello! The Year 500 Century Celebration has just kicked off, bringing with it a series of Raffles that will take place over the next week. You have to be in it to win it - so log on now! But, before you do that, read on to see what's new this time around!   
  
  
► **Crazy**Stat Buffs****  
No matter what you're doing during the Century Celebration, do it in style! All players have been given the following stat buffs while the event lasts:  


  * +50% Skill XP
  * +50% Dungeon XP and Dungeon Class XP
  * +50% Slayer XP
  * +50 Magic Find
  * +50 Tracking

Get on now to make the most of them! Stat buffs expire when the Century Celebration finishes at 12PM EST on Wednesday 8th July.  
  
  
► **Win BIG in Raffles!**  
  


![herebedragons.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/herebedragons-png.3586880/)

  
Throughout the event, the following Raffles will take place:  


  * The **Speed Raffle** is drawn every 2 hours, real-life time. All tickets players collected within the 2h will be entered.
  * The **Daily Raffle** is drawn every 24 hours. All tickets players collected within the last 24h will be entered.
  * **The Big One** is drawn once, at the end of the Century Celebration. All tickets players gather are automatically entered.

  
So, whenever you get a Raffle Ticket, it will be entered into the next Speed Raffle, Daily Raffle, and into The Big One! **Simply being online during the event will earn you Raffle Tickets** , though there are ways to earn more! Raffles have already begun, so get online now!  
  
  
**➥** **Raffle Tasks**  
Upon being assigned a team, all players receive an identical list of 21 Raffle Tasks to complete. Tasks reset every 2 hours, once the Speed Raffle is drawn, and grant +1 Raffle Ticket and a Slice of Century Cake when completed, which can be fed to another player to give both of you more tickets and rare rewards.  
  
Complete as many tasks as you can to get more tickets and increase your chance of winning items worth billions of coins!
