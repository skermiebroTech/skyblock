# [August 24] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed a couple more cases where Bestiary progress and Combat / Fishing EXP was not being awarded correctly.
  * Fixed multiple bugs where Mana cost in item descriptions did not take into account Ultimate Wise enchantment.
  * Fixed multiple bugs where Mana cost was not being reduced correctly by Ultimate Wise enchantment.
  * Fixed barriers from Wither Cloak Sword not respecting Ultimate Wise enchantment when getting hit while sword is not held.
  * Fixed Werewolf armor not applying the cap correctly.
  * Fixed a bunch of cases where players are tagged as being in combat when they shouldn't.
  * Fixed Automatons being able to deal damage for a short period of time after dying.
  * Fixed Fishing Hook not despawning when trying to fish in water with lava rods.
  * Fixed a few issues with being unable to use an Anvil UI (e.g. renaming Garden plots) when having no levels and/or playing on newer versions of Minecraft.
  * Fixed Champion enchantment increasing Fishing EXP gain.
  * Fixed a way of rod swapping.
  * Fixed some typos.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
