# [April 11] Small Glacite Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about a couple small changes that we've rolled recently:  


  * Fixed an issue where you could reset your cold by throwing an enderpearl and warping to the Dwarven Base Camp.
  * Fixed an issue where the Corpses Widget would display 'LOOTED' if it was looted by anyone, instead of when it was looted by you.
  * Significantly buffed the base Mineshaft weights (these are still affected by perks that increase the chance for you to find a Mineshaft).
    * Mineshafts can spawn by breaking any of the below blocks in the Glacite Tunnels:
    * Hard Stone: (old) 1 in 100,000 blocks broken -> (new) 1 in 20,000 blocks broken
    * Any Gemstone: 1 in 2,500 -> 1 in 1,000
    * Glacite: 1 in 1,750 -> 1 in 1,000
    * Umber: 1 in 2,500 -> 1 in 1,000
    * Tungsten: 1 in 2,500 -> 1 in 1,000
    * Mithril: 1 in 5,000 -> 1 in 2,000
    * Titanium: 1 in 1,000 -> 1 in 500
  * When a Mineshaft Portal is spawned from breaking any of the above block types, that block's Mineshaft types are more likely to generate. For example, when breaking a Ruby Gemstone, you are more likely (but not guaranteed!) to get either of the Ruby Mineshafts. **This was the behavior pre-patch.**

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
