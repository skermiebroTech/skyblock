To put it simply - we are releasing this update not only to create engaging gameplay into Farming, but also promote active play - while we do not want to completely stop the practice of playing The Garden while, for example, watching shows or talking with friends, we are hoping this update reduces the strength of botting and macroing within the Garden while improving the experience for everyone else.

### **►** Pests​

![Untitled \(4\).png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/image-45-png.3264828/)

  
  
Starting today, players who have reached Garden Level V will encounter a variety of pests spawning around their Garden. From Rats to Mosquitos, there are ten types of pest! Different pests have different behaviors, and drop different items!  
  
As you farm, Pests will spawn slowly over time, and if there are 4 pests or more in your Garden at once, you will lose some Farming Fortune!  
  
But pests are not all that bad! The more you defeat them, the bigger the buff you can claim - so make sure you vacuum all your pests! There are also tools you can use to reduce pests spawning, or spawn way more pests, your choice!

### **►** Pest Drops and a New Drop System​

![javaw_OvCbHPGwg4.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/javaw_ovcbhpgwg4-png.3264857/)

Each Pest has its own unique drop table. For example, the Rat drops:  


  * Enchanted Pumpkin (100%)
  * Compost (4%)
  * Honey Jar (4%)
  * Dung (4%)
  * Plant Matter (4%)
  * Tasty Cheese (4%)
  * Rodent Revolution Vinyl (2%)
  * Polished Pumpkin (1%)
  * LEGENDARY Rat Pet (0.2%)

Farming Fortune works on crop drops with a 5% drop chance or more, multiplying the amount of crops you receive. For example, the Rat Pest has a 100% chance to drop at least 1 Enchanted Pumpkin, so if you have a total of 1000 Farming and Pumpkin fortune, you will drop 11 Enchanted Pumpkins! How does a rat carry 11 pumpkins? You shouldn't be asking us those kinds of questions.  
  
For Pest drops with a 5% or lower chance, a new formula has been devised that utilizes Farming Fortune in place of Magic Find. Simply put, Farming Fortune **increases** your chances of getting rare drops. The higher your Farming Fortune stat, the higher your chances of getting rarer drops.

### **►** Changes to Jacob’s Medals​

![2023-11-13_22.37.06.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/2023-11-13_22-37-06-png.3264813/)

  
As part of this update, we have adjusted the brackets within Farming Contests in an attempt to create more winners! Everyone loves winners. We don’t talk about losers.  


  * The **GOLD** Medal bracket for Jacob’s Contests has been increased from top 5% to 10%.
  * The **SILVER** Medal bracket for Jacob’s Contests has been increased from top 25% to 30%.

In addition to these changes, we have added two new brackets for the farming elite to compete for:  
  
**DIAMOND** : Top 2% of participants.  


  * Awards 1 Gold Medal, 1 Silver, and 35 Jacob’s Tickets.

**PLATINUM** : Top 5% of participants.  


  * Awards 1 Gold Medal, 1 Bronze, and 30 Jacob’s Tickets.

In addition to this, collecting all unique platinum medals will award players with a gray emblem, while completing a diamond set will award a golden one.  


> As more and more players get more Farming Fortune and gear up, getting Gold Medal has become harder, especially for newer players. Due to this, we are adjusting the existing medal brackets to allow for more players to obtain the necessary progression rewards from Jacob, while adding 2 new brackets to recognize and reward the true top players accordingly.

### **►** Other Changes​

With this update comes several changes that look to improve the overall SkyBlock experience, with a particular emphasis placed upon The Garden.  
  


### Garden Changes​

  * Improved accuracy of Jacob’s Contest counter to prevent players from inflating numbers by having co-op members farm crops with a full inventory and letting them pick the crops up.
  * Text messages output when switching modes with the Fungi Cutter are now clearer.
  * Increased the base rarity of the Fungi Cutter from rare to epic.
  * When in the Garden, crop-specific Farming Fortune is now shown beneath the general Farming Fortune line in the tab list.
  * Surpassing your previous personal best in a Jacob’s Contest will now inform you via a chat message. An extra message will also play once the contest concludes.
  * Compost now has a “Right-click to view recipes” feature so that crafting a Compost Bundle is more intuitive.
  * Added 10 new Fortune stats - one for each crop. A player’s crop-specific fortune is now added to their base Farming Fortune to determine their final fortune for the crop they’re farming.
    * Turbo Crop Enchantments, Garden Crop Upgrades, Mathematical Hoes, Carrolyn NPC, Cocoa Chopper, and Garden Talisman line all now grant crop-specific fortune.
  * Disabled using Beds to setspawn in The Garden.
