### ▶ Event Details​

A Honk and Seek Adventure adds about a dozen new items that can be found around SkyBlock while the event is active. The event will be active for 7 days, from April 1st (7:55am EDT) to April 8th.  
  
Most of these items should be fairly simple finds, with varying ways to obtain them throughout SkyBlock. Aside from your initial red gift, acquiring these items does not involve interacting with Chuckleton.  
  
You can keep track of the items to be obtained by talking to Chuckleton.
