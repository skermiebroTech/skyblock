# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.21 - Rift Mountaintop

![Mountaintop.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/mountaintop-png.3376848/)

  
Today we are releasing Hypixel SkyBlock 0.21, which features The Mountaintop, a new content-pack zone at the end of The Rift.  
  
**We added spoilers in this thread for the lore enthusiasts in our community, but if you want some glimpses as to what is in store keep reading on!**  
  


> Warning: Some of the NPCs in this update are invisible with a few popular SkyBlock Mods!

Spoiler: Oruo and Eleanor

**➥ Oruo**  
Upon reaching the mountaintop, you’ll encounter Oruo, now in possession of the Blessing of Time. He grants you entry to his **Time Chamber** , where time flows at double speed. Inside, you must face Sun Gecko.  
If you emerge victorious you will unlock his Essence Shop for valuable upgrades.  
  


![Sun Gecko.jpg](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sun-gecko-jpg.3377113/)

  
  
**➥ Eleanors**  
Multiple versions of Eleanor roam the mountaintop, each following a different Shen. They search tirelessly for a certain individual. Could that person be you?

Spoiler: Ubik and Timite

**➥ Ubik Von Neumann**  
Ubik, celebrated as the smartest mind in any reality, has made his way into the Rift in pursuit of intellectual equals. He will challenge you on 3 different occasions, each rewarding different items.  


![Capture d’écran 2024-12-10 à 00.25.10.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/capture-d%E2%80%99e%CC%81cran-2024-12-10-a%CC%80-00-25-10-png.3376992/)

  
  
**➥ Timite**  
Inside the Mountaintop there is a new Timite Ore, which can age using a Time Gun. Each variant you mine contributes to the Timite Collection, unlocking crafts and rewards, such as:  
  
**Time Pocket Bag and Discrite**  
This new bag will allow you to store up to 5 Aging Items.  
Those will progress twice as fast in the bag, compared to your inventory.  
And while we are at it, Discrite is an Aging Item too! This one grants you a buff in the Rift but also outside of the Rift.  
  
**Highlite**  
This new crafted item is a component to a new recipe in the Gold Collection: The Enchanted Clock

**Puffs**  
At such dizzying heights, even your breath can spark life. Defeat these peculiar, non-hostile Puffs to earn cruxes, but do it swiftly, as consecutive takedowns are key to maximizing rewards.  
  


![Puff.jpg](https://hypixel.net/attachments/puff-jpg.3377020/)

**Reverse Murder**  
Detective Amog returns with another perplexing “reverse murder” mystery. Whether it’s new or something strangely familiar, brace yourself, your investigative skills will be tested once again.

Spoiler: The Breach

**The Breach**  
At the pinnacle of the mountain lies the breach. With sufficient Rift Time on your side, you can repair and enter it. Completing this final challenge concludes your journey through the Rift.  
Completing the Rift will grant you free access to the Wizard functionalities in a future update.

  
Completing the Rift rewards a **Memento** item! Have fun!  
  


### **► Looking Back On 2024**​

As is tradition, we have written a thread to reflect on what's changed in SkyBlock over the past year and what we are looking forward too in the next year. If you are interested in reading about the future of SkyBlock, the thread will be available in a few days in the SkyBlock section of the forums.  


### **► Other Changes**​

  * Glacite Maniacs will now always drop 1 Enchanted Glacite on kill.
  * Increased the amount of Crystal Fragments required to craft a Medium Dragon Sack to 16.
  * Changed the texture of the Harmonios Surgery Belt.
  * End Nodes have been changed to purple clay for more visibility.
  * Necromancy Menu will now show where a soul was obtained (for new souls only).
  * Glacite, Tungsten, and Umber ore now drop 5-11 experience orbs.
  * Voidgloom Seraph's vitality decrease no longer persists after the boss is slain or despawns.
  * Riding a flying pig pet now fails an active island parkour.



### **► Bug Fixes**​

  * Fixed sometimes being kicked when entering the Crystal Nucleus while having a free Crystal Hollows pass.
  * Fixed Fatal Tempo being applied by bows without the enchant by switching weapons.
  * Fixed Spirit Leap Pearls opening an empty menu when Soloing Dungeons.
  * Fixed Mobs spawned by Vampire Minions disappearing when below Y level 2.
  * Fixed being able to sell Midas' Sword to NPCs.
  * Fixed Umber Key and Tungsten Key forge times being longer than intended.
  * Fixed being able to sell Necron's Blade items and salvage them.
  * Fixed quick crafting and supercrafting not increasing collections correctly.
  * Fixed an issue where the Hex would incorrectly say that "A Beginners Guide to Pesthunting" was applied.
  * Fixed being able to put Launch Pads and Plumber's Sponges inside Builder's Ruler.
  * Fixed quick crafting being accessible below Carpentry 3.
  * Fixed Daily Powder showing an inaccurate amount received during the 2x Powder event.
  * Fixed grammar in forge notification messages.
  * Fixed grammar in Dwarven O's error message.
  * Fixed Booster Cookie confirmation menu displaying an incorrect amount of bits you can gain.
  * Fixed Detective Amog misspelling Ramero as Romero.
  * Fixed Titanium Equipment bonus working in zones it shouldn't.
  * Fixed certain mushroom blocks being unbreakable in the Glowing Mushroom Cave.
  * Fixed the zone color in Power Crystal's description.
  * Fixed the Hex showing the incorrect item rarity and stats on the preview item before reforging.
