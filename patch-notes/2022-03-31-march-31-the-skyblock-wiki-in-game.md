# [March 31] The SkyBlock Wiki In-Game

Hey all,  
  
Recently we've added a new NPC to the hub called Biblio, he is here to help promote the new [SkyBlock Wiki](https://wiki.hypixel.net/) along with its new in-game commands!  
He can now be located on the porch of the Community Center and he's eager to introduce you all to the new Wiki.  


![1648765706770.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1648765706770-png.2946679/)

  
  
When clicking on him you will be presented with a short menu explaining some brief details about the Wiki and its commands that you can use to make your life easier when finding out what the many items, mobs, and areas do in SkyBlock.  


![1648765701101.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1648765701101-png.2946678/)

  
  
**Available Commands:**  


  * /wiki - Receive a direct link to the SkyBlock Wiki
  * /wiki <id> \- Will attempt to find a page with the corresponding item ID.
  * /wikithis - Will attempt to find a wiki page for the current item you are holding.
