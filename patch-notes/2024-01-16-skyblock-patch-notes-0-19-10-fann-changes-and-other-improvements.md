# SkyBlock Patch Notes 0.19.10 - Fann Changes and Other Improvements

![sb-19-10.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-19-10-png.3283611/)

  
Hello!  
  
Welcome to **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.10**. We’re releasing a bunch of small pets changes today mostly to improve Fann’s services introduced last update and a bunch of bug fixes. We’ve been working on new content that will hopefully be ready in the near future. Read on to find out more about this patch!  
  
  


### **►** Fann Changes​

The introduction of Fann’s Pet Training services had a bunch of issues. We based Fann’s training rates off of the cost and XP rates of Carrot Candies. What we didn’t realise is how bad the XP rates of Carrot Candies were. In this patch, we’ll be increasing the amount of XP given by Carrot Candies and significantly lower Fann’s training costs again. Here are the changes as follows:  


### Taming 60 Pet Submissions​

Instead of having to give pets to George in a specific order, you can now hand in the pets in any order. You still have to submit one of each of those pets, but you can put off the ones that are hardest to obtain for later.  
  


![george-taming-cap.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/george-taming-cap-png.3283583/)

### **Carrot Candy Buffs** ​

Carrot Candies were released over three years ago in the 0.7.8 update. Throughout the last three years, skill XP has become a lot easier to come by due to increases in skill wisdom and overall better sources of skill XP. In contrast, the Pet XP rewarded by Carrot Candies has not changed throughout this period. Here are the values changes as follows:  
  


![carrot-candy-changes.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/carrot-candy-changes-png.3283601/)

### **Fann’s Training Buffs** ​

With Carrot Candies being buffed, we now have plenty of leeway to buff Fann’s Training Sessions. The tiers are now much cheaper and generally award much more XP. These changes should now make Fann’s Training an enticing alternative for levelling up some pets.  


  


![fann-rates-old.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fann-rates-old-png.3283639/)

  


![fann-rates-new.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fann-rates-new-png.3283603/)

  * The free tier scales from 20,000 EXP/day at Taming 10 to 40,000 EXP/day at Taming 50, then reaching 50,000 EXP/day at Taming 60.
  * The cost of bits is assumed to be 1,000 Coins/bit.



### **►** **Other Changes** ​

### Detailed Playtime Breakdown​

The /playtime command has been enhanced to show where you’ve spent most of your playtime! Click on your playtime in chat to open up this detailed playtime breakdown. Hover over each island category to see your playtime in each island!

### Kat’s Bouquet​

Kat’s Bouquet is five Kat Flowers bundled together. Hopefully it makes buying and using Kat Flowers slightly less tedious for pets with a long pet-sitting time.  
  


![kat-bouquet.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/kat-bouquet-png.3283606/)

### **Items and Equipment Changes** ​

#### **Fishing Items** ​

  * Added the Fisherman Equipment Set, consisting of 4 pieces across the Prismarine, Sponge, Clay and Clownfish collections.



![fishing-equipment.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/fishing-equipment-png.3283607/)

  * **Thunderbolt Necklace and Magma Lord Gauntlet can now roll for Blazing Fortune and Fishing Experience attributes.**



#### **Misc. Items** ​

  * Buffed Warped Stone's reforge with more intelligence (Rare: +65, Epic: +100, Legendary: +150).
  * Dwarf Turtle Shelmet / Slime Hat will now work against the magma cubes from the Season of Jerry's Mount Jerry Eruption event.
  * Reworded the Griffin Feather item.
  * The Coin Talisman now also works on private and garden islands.
  * Updated Rock The Fish description.



### Achievement Changes​

  * “Always Sunny” achievement will now require you to unlock the Island Time menu in the Garden.
  * Fixed the "I am Groot" achievement not actually requiring 100 HP on the Growth Armor.



### Other Changes​

#### Arachne Changes​

  * Luxurious Spool may now be salvaged for 1 Spider Essence.
  * Your Spider Essence will now be shown in the sidebar while in Arachne's Sanctuary.
  * The "ARACHNE DOWN" boss breakdown will now only be shown to damagers and players in the Sanctuary zone.



#### Mining Changes​

  * Added a new NPC to the Dwarven Mines called Silnar in the Cliffside Veins who will introduce players to the Cult of the Fallen Star and gift players with the Fallen Star Lozenge to help them track Fallen Stars.
  * Adding a new uncommon commission to kill 10 Treasure Hoarders.
  * Doubled the Precious Pearl drop rate from Nucleus Runs.
  * Messages for Essence drops in Crystal Hollows Loot Chests are now condensed.
  * Significantly increased the drop rate of Corleonite.



#### Misc. Changes​

  * Added "/skill [skill name]" shortcut command to open the skill's menu.
  * Added a NPC Price of 2050 to the Agarimoo Tongue.
  * Added a tooltip in the collection menu showing the total collected amount once maxed out (for solo players, as co-op already shows contributions).
  * Added a way to remove invalid portal blocks from your island by right-clicking on them.
  * Added Sleepy Hollow to the minion upgrades section of the bazaar.
  * Arguments given to the /show command are no longer case sensitive.
  * "Bleeds" hologram for Minotaur will now always go away.
  * Guardian Lucky Block and Magma Core have been added to the Bazaar
  * Pets will now spawn after exiting the Dojo challenge.
  * Pigman has been removed from the Bestiary as it has no constituents.
  * Player nicks will no longer be sent to the Bazaar, now only real player names will be shown in it.
  * Previewing a Fire Sale through Elizabeth's Abiphone contact will not be available unless you're in the Hub.
  * Sea Witch's splash potions will no longer damage other sea creatures.
  * The bestiary icon will now no longer disappear when clicking Combat skill after viewing rankings.
  * Updated garden milestone tooltip in sidebar.



### **User Interface and Messaging changes** ​

  * Added an extra notification on login when you have coins refunded from leaving a Dark Auction.
  * Added feedback to Soul's Rebound when attempting to use it on a Training Dummy.
  * Fire Sale notifications will not be shown after disabling the chat feedback setting.
