# [August 29] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  


  * Gave Mithril & Gemstone powder buffs to various picks.
    * Mithril Picks & Drills:
      * 5% for Mithril Pickaxe (forged)
      * 10% for Refined Mithril Pickaxe (forged)
      * 15% for Mithril Drill SX-R226
      * 20% for Mithril Drill SX-R326
    * Gemstone Drills:
      * 5% for Ruby Drill TX-15
      * 10% for Gemstone Drill LT-522
      * 15% for Topaz Drill KGR-12
      * 20% for Jasper Drill X
  * Removed unnecessary decimals from the Leaping and Silk-Edge Sword.
  * Fixed a missing percent in Archer's Bouncy Arrows class passive.
  * Fixed stats being formatted incorrectly on the Goblin Armor's lore.
  * Standardized the word "Exp" in several Pet Candy messages.
  * Fixed capitalizations in Trophy Fishing Sacks.
  * Fixed a missed comma in Odger's menu.
  * Fixed Jerry's name not being capitalized when picking up deliveries before interacting with him.
  * Removed an extra space in the Tiered Bonus lore on armors.
  * Fixed other various typos.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
