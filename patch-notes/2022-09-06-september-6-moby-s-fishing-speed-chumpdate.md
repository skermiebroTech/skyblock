# [September 6] Moby's Fishing Speed Chumpdate

Hey all!  
  
Since 0.14 changed Fishing Speed to a player stat, fishing has been a slower experience.  
We've added items and content to bring back players' fishing speed back up to the cap!  
  
**Moby**  
  


![1662493184702.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662493184702-png.3085664/)

  
Moby, found in the Mushroom Desert island  
​

Moby is a new NPC in the Mushroom Desert, hidden away in the Glowing Mushroom Cave around 206, 43, -500.  
They offer a small questline which introduces a new item for fishing: the Chum Bucket.  
  


![1662493164400.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662493164400-png.3085662/)

  
Chum Bucket in action​

  
Players can now collaborate to gather Chums together and fill buckets to trade with Moby for one of 6 new items.  
  
**New Enchants**  
  
You can now find the Bobbin's Scriptures very rarely from Yetis, Grim Reapers, Sea Emperors and Jawbus sea creatures.  
The scriptures can be crafted with _tons_ of fishing materials into the new Bobbin' Time ultimate enchant:  
  


![1662493709286.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662493709286-png.3085676/)

  
It's Bobbin' Time! _(not Legion)_ ​

  
There is also now Piscary for Fishing Rods unlocked at enchanting level 8.  
Piscary VI is available for grabs rarely in the experimentation table.  
  


![1662493849409.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662493849409-png.3085677/)

  
Piscary enchant​

  
**Ammonite Pet Rework**  
The Ammonite pet has received 2 changes to its perk.  
First, "Not a Snail" speed and defense have been moved to Gift of the Ammonite.  
Second, "Not a Snail" has been replaced with "Expert Cave Fisher", which makes the Ammonite useful for underground fishing situations.  
  


![1662494770486.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662494770486-png.3085692/)

  
What even is an ammonite really?​

  
**Other Content**  


  * Lava Leeches now rarely drop the Pitchin' Koi for the Pitchin' reforge stone.
  * Kuudra Mandibles, _wherever they could be_ , will now also have +10 Fishing Speed.
  * Added new equipment pieces using shark and crimson fishing ingredients:



![1662494641487.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662494641487-png.3085684/)

  
One of 3 new equipment pieces for fishing (3 upgrade tiers each)​

  
**Balance**  


  * Buffed the 'Submerged' reforge to get +5 Fishing Speed.
  * Pod Tactics from Dolphin pet 9->10 Fishing Speed and 10->30 blocks range.
  * Mining Fiesta now grants +75 Mining Wisdom instead of +1.5x Mining XP.
  * Cole's Mining XP Buff now grants +50 Mining Wisdom instead of _multiplying the player's Mining Wisdom by 1.5x_.
  * Buffed Enchanting Skill's Conjurer perk 4->5% extra XP orbs per level.
    * This way, you can get 350 levels from a titanic when maxed enchanting.
  * Added +20 Vitality to Mosquito Bow.
  * Changed Holy Dragon Armor set bonus to use Health Regen:



![1662495167213.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1662495167213-png.3085696/)

  
Holy Dragon Armor!​

  * Moved the Health Regen from the Jellyfish Pet's Radiant Regeneration to a base stat on the pet and renamed the perk to Radiant Scyphozoa.

Happy Fishing!
