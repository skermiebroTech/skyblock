# [May 5] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Today we have a small patch containing fixes and changes related to the recent Harvest Feast update (0.24.4).  


  * Book of Stats can now be applied to Farming Tools to track Crops Harvested
  * Lucky Clover and Poignant Lucky Clover now also grant +3 and +7 Overbloom respectively
  * Freshly Baked accessories now double their own Overbloom during Feast events
  * The Feast enchantment now grants quadruple the amount of Overbloom per level (+0.5 → +2 per level)
  * Fixed Art of War granting Strength on Farming Tools
  * The Grand Bakery menu is now correctly named

We also rolled a few fixes yesterday:  


  * Fixed Dedication sometimes giving the wrong type of Crop Fortune
  * Fixed a Harvest Feast event also starting during a Grand Feast
  * Fixed Rarefinder Chips not dropping properly
  * Fixed the effect for Finn's Focaccia expiring before it should occasionally
  * Fixed SB XP on Seasonings not being capped

We are still monitoring feedback regarding Feast events. In a future patch we plan on also having Pests drop their respective **RARE CROP** from the Harvest Feast to maintain more active farming during the event. Please continue to give feedback, especially in regards to Seasonings, Kernels, and any of the item rewards from the event.  
  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
