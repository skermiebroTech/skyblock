# Changes to Cake Souls and Spooky Pies

Hey Guys  
  
We've just rolled an update to SkyBlock that makes the following changes:  
  


  * Cake Souls will no longer show any names other than the captured soul, the time of capture and the name of the player who captured it.
  * Once a soul is captured, the item will become co-op soulbound.
  * Spooky Pies are now co-op soulbound.

We understand that this change can be sudden and weird to most players but it is necessary for the health of the game at this time. This change exists to combat some of the more obscure parts of boosting and coin selling. Ultimately, this is something we should have done earlier, and have decided to rip the band-aid off and do now.
