# [0.9.8] City Project: Blacksmith Workspace + New Bits Shop items

Hello!  
  
The community completed the second city project, the Blacksmith Workspace.  
Along with this update, we are releasing a few more features...  
  
**Blacksmith Workspace**  
  


![blacksmith_building.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/blacksmith_building-png.2056782/)

  
Upgraded Blacksmith area, now its own zone with two floors​

  
On top of its upgraded looks, the Blacksmith now houses the Smithmonger NPC.  
The Smithmonger is a shop offering 5 reforge stones:  


  * _Moil_ , 50k axe reforge, req Mining lvl 5, grants up to +3% foraging exp,
  * _Dirty_ , 100k sword/rods reforge, req Mining lvl 10, grants up to 15 STR, 20 AS & 15 Ferocity
  * _Toil_ , 1m axe reforge, req Mining lvl 25, grants up to 20 STR, 20 CD & +6% foraging exp
  * _Refined_ , 1m pickaxe reforge, req Mining lvl 25, grants up to 20 defense, +6% mining exp & +0.1% chance to drop the enchanted mined ores
  * _Blessed_ , 1m hoe reforge, req Mining lvl 25, grants up to 20 speed, 6% farming exp & +20% chance for double crops

The smithmonger also offers a 2m Reforge Anvil for convenience, or you can use the anvil in front of him to apply the reforge stones.  
  
**New Bits Shop Items**  
  
We added 3 new items to the bits shop:  
  


![builders_wand.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/builders_wand-png.2056823/)

  
The Builder's Wand lets you build your dream farm (or anything) way faster  
  


![compact_enchant.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/compact_enchant-png.2056824/)

  
Functions like Expertise, but for Mining  
  


![bits_talisman.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/bits_talisman-png.2056825/)

  
Accelerate your Bits acquisition (still capped by your Bits Available)​

  
**New Stat: Ferocity**  
  


![ferocity_stat.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/ferocity_stat-png.2056832/)

  
As seen in the player menu when you have at least 1 ferocity​

  
For the longest time, Attack Speed wasn't a functional stat in SkyBlock. During that period, one of our internal proposed solution was for AS to increase proc multiple strikes. We then added this behavior with a cool visual FX on the Tiger pet and took a different path for Attack Speed.  
  
But the idea is making a comeback in the form of a new damage stat!  
Essentially, Merciless Swipe from Tiger is now a player stat which can appear on any item, pet, enchant, etc..  
  


![tiger.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/tiger-png.2056841/)

  
Updated Tiger Pet, with +20 total Ferocity​

  
We retconned +5 Ferocity on Pigman, Ghoul, Hound & Megalodon and +10 on Ocelot pet.  
The Shredder also got a +50 Ferocity buff.  
The new Dirty reforge has +15 Ferocity on Mythic swords.
