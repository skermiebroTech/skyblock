# [May 13] Fast Travel

Hello!  
  
We've introduced a new feature to SkyBlock: Fast Travel.  
  


![eforOs7.png](https://i.imgur.com/eforOs7.png)

  
Menu accessible from the previous "Warp to: Hub" button  
​

Your private island is always accessible and you may now **warp directly to the hub** from any island.  
_(This feature was initially introduced to increase server performance, but we did more...)_  
  
You may now warp to all of the different islands in SkyBlock directly from the Fast Travel menu.  
Each warp is unlockable using **travel scrolls** :  
  


![bucb23d.png](https://i.imgur.com/bucb23d.png)

  
Scroll crafted from a recipe unlocked in the End Stone collection  
​

But wait, there's more!  
[If you have MVP+](http://store.hypixel.net), you gain access to Epic scrolls which lead to extra convenient locations across SkyBlock.  
  


![hVOaVCT.png](https://i.imgur.com/hVOaVCT.png)

  
Fast Travel: Advanced Mode  
  


![gSawfZw.png](https://i.imgur.com/gSawfZw.png)

  
Extra convenient warp​

  
Other Changes:  


  * Added an extra round to the Dark Auction (now 4 items per)​

  * All dragons are now a little bit tankier​

  * Fixed too many Tuxedo particles on private islands​

  * Fixed Pet Rock defense stacking​

  * Mobs no longer trample crops on your island​


Enjoy.
