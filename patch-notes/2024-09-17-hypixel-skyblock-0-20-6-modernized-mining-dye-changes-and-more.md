# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.20.6 - Modernized Mining, Dye Changes, and more!

![sb-20-6.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-20-6-png.3357151/)

  
Hello!  
  
Today we are bringing you Hypixel SkyBlock update 0.20.6, with massive changes to the Mining Skill, some changes to Dyes, and more! Here is the TLDR:  


  * Crystal Nucleus RNG Meter​

  * Revamped Heart of the Mountain​

  * New Forge Slots and Pure Ores​

  * Mining Balance Overhaul​

  * Dye Drop Chance Changes​

  * Various Quality of Life Changes​

  * Bug Fixes​


Keep reading if you want to know all of the details!  
  
  
  
  


### **►** Crystal Nucleus RNG Meter​

![Crystal Nucleus RNG Meter.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/crystal-nucleus-rng-meter-png.3357453/)

  
1,000 xp is earned per run!  
​

We've introduced an RNG Meter to the Crystal Nucleus, allowing you to guarantee a drop of your choice after completing a certain number of runs. You will retroactively receive Nucleus XP for runs you've completed at a 25% rate.  
  
We've also changed how Divan's Alloy drops from the Nucleus: rather than having a dynamic drop rate, the drop rate will always be ~1/1,000 runs.  
  


### **►** Revamped Heart of the Mountain​

  


![hotm-tree.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/hotm-tree-png.3357476/)

  
  
The Heart of the Mountain has been almost entirely overhauled. Most perks were either moved, changed, or deleted, and there are also quite a few new ones! Our goal was to get rid of all of the less useful perks and replace them with more useful ones. We've refunded everyone's Powder and reset their trees for free so that everyone can try the new Heart of the Mountain out!  
  
We've also made some additional changes to the Heart of the Mountain:  


  * Added a warning for when you mine with unspent HotM tokens
  * Disabled HotM perks now appear as Redstone Dust
  * You can no longer switch to a different Pickaxe Ability while you have one currently active or on cooldown

### **➥ New Stats**​

**Mining Fortune Variants**  
Similar to individual Crop Fortunes, Mining Fortune is getting 4 sub types that stack on top of your base Mining Fortune. There are _Ores_ , _Blocks_ , _Dwarven Metals_ , and _Gemstones_. **Mining Spread**  
This stat is your chance to break an additional block when mining, and it stacks above 100 similar to Fortune. If you have 350 Mining Spread, you are guaranteed to mine 3 additional blocks and then have a 50% chance to mine a fourth. Mining Spread is compatible with Ores, Blocks, and Dwarven Metals.  
  
**Gemstone Spread**  
This stat works just like Mining Spread--but for Gemstones! The key difference here compared to how it worked previously is that Gemstone Spread is compatible with Pristine, making it super powerful!  
  
**Heat Resistance**  
Just like Cold Resistance, Heat Resistance improves your survivability when exposed to Heat. We've also taken the time to revamp how Heat builds up so that it's more lenient, building up slower and dispersing faster.  


Spoiler: Heat Buildup Changes

**Old System**  
\+ While in the Magma Fields: +1 Heat/s  
\+ While in lava: +5 Heat/s  
\- While not in the Magma Fields: -0.5 Heat/s  
\- While in water: -1 Heat/s  
\- Drinking a water bottle: -40 Heat  
  
**New System**  
\+ While in the Magma Fields: +0.1 Heat/s  
\+ While in Khazad-Dum: +0.2 Heat/s _(takes priority over Magma Fields)_  
\+ While on fire: +1 Heat/s  
\+ While in lava: +3 Heat/s _(takes priority over fire)_  
\- While not in the Magma Fields: -1 Heat/s  
\- While in water: -5 Heat/s  
\- Drinking any potion: -10 Heat

Spoiler: Gemstone Gauntlet Revamp

We are significantly changing how the Gemstone Gauntlet to further encourage players to use Drills. To compensate for this, we are adding multiple new abilities that provides interesting new utility to the item.  


  * Breaking Power: 9 > 8
  * Damage: 200 > +300
  * Strength: 0 > +50
  * Mining Speed: 1600 > +800
  * New Passive: Reduced To Atoms  
Mobs killed on Mining Islands now drop Gemstones when killed with Gemstone Gauntlets. What Gemstones they drop correlate to the Gemstones that you have on your Gauntlet. Killing these mobs also charges your Gauntlet Meter by 1.
  * New Passive: Kinetic  
Mobs killed on Mining Islands will now reduce the timers on all your Forge Slots by a certain amount of time when killed with Gemstone Gauntlets. The amount of time scales by 0.5s for each Perfect Gemstone you have slotted within your Gauntlet.
  * New Ability: Gemstone Snap  
The Gauntlet Meter stores up to 150 charges (150 mob kills). Once fully charged, you may Shift Right Click with the item to drain your meter, giving you +50 Mining Fortune for 60m. This effect does not stack.

Below is a list of some of the content we're currently working on and would like to share with you. ### **➥ Chocolate Factory**​

We're pleased with the progress of the Chocolate Factory and have a few small updates on the way. These will address current issues and include new features like seasonal automatic egg collection, guaranteed methods to acquire the rarest rabbits, and more!  
  


### **➥ Museum Progression Revamp**​

An upcoming update will overhaul the Museum's reward system. Instead of gaining SkyBlock XP based on the total number of items displayed, you'll now earn XP based on each item's individual value. For example, late-game items will provide more XP. While the total XP might be slightly reduced at launch, this change allows for greater XP growth in the long term. Plus, expect enhanced Museum rewards alongside this update.  
  


### **➥ Pesthunter's Wares**​

Now in the early stages of development, the Pesthunter's Wares is a minor Garden update that will add some more farming content to the game, mostly pest-related. This update will expand upon Pesthunter Phillip's store, which currently only has a single talisman line, adding a variety of new items to aid you in your pest-hunting adventures while also making some wider changes to address balancing inequalities between crops.  
  


### **➥ Foraging**​

As mentioned in our recent [Dev Blog](https://hypixel.net/threads/dev-blog-12-navigating-the-multivers-ions-of-the-minecraft-world.5708006/), we've made excellent progress on Foraging, with the new Hunting skill nearing completion. While we're not yet ready to implement features from the latest Minecraft version, the Foraging update is nearly polished. We're just waiting for the right tech to catch up.
