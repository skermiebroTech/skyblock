# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.23.4 - Primordial Broodfather Changes, Abiphone Keynote Event, and more!

![sb-23-4.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-23-4-png.3485369/)

  
Hello!  
  
Today we're rolling out update 0.23.4 for Hypixel SkyBlock, which includes improvements to T5 Spider Slayer, the latest Abiphone Keynote Event, improvements to the stats menu, and much more! Keep reading to learn all the details!  
  
  
  
**► Primordial Broodfather Changes**  
We've made a variety of adjustments in response to community feedback from the Primordial Broodfather update.  
  


### **➥ Boss Changes**​

  * Enabled the Spider Slayer RNG Meter
  * Enabled Ananke Feather usage on the Spider Slayer RNG Meter

  * Added Spider Catalyst to the Tier II loot table (125 weight)
  * Increased the weight of the Shriveled Wasp from 10 → 20
  * Tarantula Catalysts can now drop upon reaching Spider Slayer 4 (was 5)



### **➥ Item Changes**​

  * Shriveled Bracelet's ability now activates on all Ж Arthropod mobs instead of just in the Spider's Den

    * Replaced x64 Enchanted Gold with x1 Digested Mosquito
  * Recluse Fang Changes

  * Tarantula Fang Changes

    * Crit Damage: 30% → 20%

    * Damage: 80 → 120

    * Crit Damage: 0% → 30%

    * Strength: 60 → 75

**► Stat Categories**  
We've overhauled the stats menu and sorted all existing stats into different categories: Combat, Mining, Farming, Foraging, Fishing, Miscellaneous, Hunting, and Wisdom Stats. Several items from across the game have been rebalanced to buff specific categories now instead of "all" or "most" stats.  


  * Item Changes
    * _Superior Dragon Armor_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Magic Find
      * **Designer Note: **While we understand Pet Luck not being increased by things such as Superior Dragon Armor theoretically makes Ender Dragon pets rarer, we have listed some weight changes to Dragons further down the thread that should remedy things.
    * _Blaze Pet_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Miscellaneous Stats on the Crimson Isle
    * _Ender Dragon Pet_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Magic Find
    * _Mithril Golem Pet_ : Only buffs Combat Stats on Mining Islands
    * _Dragon Horn_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Magic Find
    * _Legion Enchantment_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Magic Find
    * _Bobbin' Time Enchantment_ : Only buffs Fishing Stats and Magic Find
      * As a result of it now increasing Treasure Chance and Double Hook Chance, Bobbin' Time's values have been reduced from 0.48%/0.64%/0.8% to 0.3%/0.4%/0.5% per bobber.
    * _Giant Fishing Rod:_ Reduced Double Hook Chance from 20 -> 10
      * **Designer Note: **We feel like the Double Hook Chance given by the Giant Fishing Rod is excessive, especially now that Bobbin' Time also increases your DHC. It was unfair for a single, incredibly expensive, limited-quantity item to be so integral to fishing progression so we're nerfing it and looking to reintroduce the DHC elsewhere in future fishing content.
    * _Mayor Jerry_ : Only buffs Combat Stats and Miscellaneous Stats
  * Base stats on items are now colored based on which category they fall into.
  * When you unlock a new stat in SkyBlock a chat message will be sent about it explaining what it does.
  * Simplified the item description for the stats menu button to make it less overwhelming.
  * True Defense now shows Damage Reduction % in the stat menu similar to regular Defense

  


### **► Other Changes**​

### **➥ Item Reclaim System**​

Over the 6 years since SkyBlock has released certain items have seen changes that make them either unusable or outdated. In the past we would handle reclaims on a case-by-case basis, but our goal now is for all reclaims to happen through the new `/reclaim` command. If you have an item that has available reclaim there will be a notice for it on the item's description.  
  
To coincide with this change, the following items are no longer dungeonizable:  


  * The Primordial
  * Halberd of the Shredded
  * Reaper Scythe
  * Reaper Falchion
  * Revenant Falchion
  * Undead Sword

Master Stars and Dungeonization costs can be reclaimed from these items using `/reclaim`. As mentioned earlier in the post, Wood Singularities are now applied to Axes instead of Wooden Swords. Any Wood Singularities you have that are on Wooden Swords can also be refunded through this system.  
  


### **➥ Axe Runes**​

Axe Runes are a new cosmetic type which change the particle trail of Axes when they are thrown. The Fading White and Fading Green Axe Runes can be found rarely in Fig and Mangrove Tree Gifts respectively. In the near future we plan on adding more Axe Runes, stay tuned!
