# [November 23] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock! Those were rolled to live last night, actually, but we still want to ensure you guys are aware of the change, and why.  
  


  * The Legion Ultimate Enchantment will no longer gain a buff from AFK players.
  * When doing a Slayer Quest, each mob killed will now give maximum amount of 50% of the XP required to spawn the boss.
  * Thunderlord/Thunderbolt has finally been redesigned and made usable again. Sorry for the wait!

We had to make this change to ensure Slayer bosses weren't being exploited (and very quickly chain killed) without going back to the normal game loop of grinding their spawn. This should take care of ensuring this is no longer doable.
