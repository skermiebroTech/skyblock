# [October 9] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Combat XP not being affected by multiplier (e.g. Champion enchantment) for some specific mobs.
  * Fixed not being able to damage Ghosts with Flower of Truth
  * Fixed Spirit Bow not dropping correctly in Dungeons.
  * Fixed Gemstone Guide menu not being sorted.
  * Fixed some sign parsing issues in Dwarven Mines.
  * Buffed the drops from Dwarven Gold in the Mines of Divan (the Gold Blocks!!)

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
