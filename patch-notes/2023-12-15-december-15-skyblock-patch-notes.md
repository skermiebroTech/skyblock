# [December 15] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about some small changes that we have rolled or are currently rolling.  


  * Reverted the `/equipment` and `/eq` commands being affected by the Combat Tag timer.
  * Reduced the price of giving a Rift Ferret to George from 500k to 50k.
  * **Magic Find no longer affects Loot Share drops that the mob owner does not roll Magic Find for.**
    * For example, a Great White Shark Tooth has a 10% chance to drop from a Great White Shark. Magic Find will only affect drops with less than a 5% drop rate. Before this update, Magic Find would buff the chance to get a loot-shared Great White Shark Tooth (2% chance to drop after Loot Share) when the mob spawner's loot roll would not affected by Magic Find (10%). This will no longer happen.
    * Another example, the Epic Baby Yeti Pet has a 3% chance to drop from a Yeti. Because this loot drop is affected by Magic Find for the mob spawner (3%), Magic Find will also affect the Loot Share chance of 0.6%.
  * **Fixed Magic Find displaying the killer's Magic Find in chat instead of your own Magic Find.**
    * Contrary to popular belief, _the system always took your own Magic Find_.
    * This Magic Find value is affected by your individual Bestiary mob levels for the Magic Find bonus.

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
