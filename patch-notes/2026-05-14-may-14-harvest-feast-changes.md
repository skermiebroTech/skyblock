# [May 14] Harvest Feast Changes

Hello! We rolled some changes to the Harvest/Grand Feast event today.  
  


  * Pests now have a 15% chance to drop their crop's Feast **RARE CROP** if it is in-season
    * Field Mice have a 30% chance to drop one of the in-season crop's **RARE CROP** at random
  * Farming Fortune no longer scales the drop rates of **RARE CROPS** from Pests, instead Overbloom does
    * This applies to _all_ non-guaranteed drops, not just drops beneath a 5% drop rate
    * To compensate for this change, we've doubled the drop rate of every item from Pests
    * The drop rate of Sunset I from Lunar Moth's has been reduced to 35% as it's drop rate can now scale when it couldn't before
    * Changed the stat description of Overbloom to reflect this new behavior
  * The Crop Bug attribute now gives a flat +10 Overbloom
  * The Pest Luck attribute now gives +20 Overbloom towards Pests
  * Hedgehog Pets now give +35 Overbloom towards Pests
  * Commemorative Mason Jars are now Co-Op Soulbound rather than Soulbound
  * Reduced the Health of Zombuddies from 20 to 5
  * Fixed Crimson Armor's Swipe attack sometimes dealing the incorrect amount of damage to the Inferno Demonlord.
