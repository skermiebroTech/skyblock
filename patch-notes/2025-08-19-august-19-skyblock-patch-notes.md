# [August 19] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Crux mobs in the Rift levitating into the atmosphere and never respawning if they touch water.
  * Added Abyssal armor as an eligible set for completing Eleanor's quest.
  * Fixed Cobblestone Collection 6 giving Farming Experience instead of Mining Experience.
  * Fixed Stronghold & Plhlegblast Pool not showing in the Crimson Isle.
  * Prevented the Shellwise Attribute from working in the Dojo challenges.
  * Fixed Scarecrows dealing way more damage than they should.
  * Fixed Spooky Event mobs not spawning if a server restarts in the middle of an event.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
