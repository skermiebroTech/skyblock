# [February 2] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Removed Mushroom Armor's 3x stats ability
  * Fixed being unable to give Bachelor's Rose to Queen Nyx, even with the correct reputation
  * Fixed a softlock with Sam's Greenhouse tutorial quest
    * Now, if the player is at the Spread a mutation objective, breaking a Dustgrain will progress them in the quest
  * Fixed 'Broodfater' typo in the Darkness Within Rune
  * Rebalanced multiple lower tier mutations to require less surrounding crops to spread
  * Added hints to the Greenhouse Tutorial for Crop placement
  * Reduced the drops from Shellfruit by 5 times
  * Decreased the amount of time required to complete the Stoplight Petal harvest minigame from 30 to 20 seconds
  * Reduced the amount of Timestalk Clones needed to complete its bestiary
  * Increased Noctilume's decay period from 5 to 6 days
  * Fixed surrounding dirt not being able to be broken on some Thunderling and Chorus Fruit growth stages
  * Changed Coalroot to not be automatically taken as fuel when using inventory insert buttons
  * Fixed guests taking damage from some Greenhouse Crops
  * Buffed Lonelily spawn likelihood
  * Fixed Hard Stone minions not saying where to get the tier 12 minion

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
