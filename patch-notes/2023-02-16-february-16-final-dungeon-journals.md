# [February 16] Final Dungeon Journals

Hello,  
  
Today we are releasing the last 7 Dungeon Journals, which have been long awaited.  
Below are the details of the changes we made:  


  * The last 7 journals have been released, they can be found on floor 6 and 7.
  * “Journal Entries” do not drop as pages anymore, instead the whole Dungeon Journal will appear when you find one.
  * We've added the name of the author to each journal, which you can unlock by delving further into the story.
  * We've included descriptions to clarify that the dates in the journals refer to an ancient calendar that predates the current time.
  * A small room on the End Island has been modified on the map.

As we approach version 1.0, the game's lore will continue to expand, and we hope you'll enjoy it!
