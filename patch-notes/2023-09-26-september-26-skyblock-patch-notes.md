# [September 26] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed crit damage modifiers not affecting Soul Eater enchantment.
  * Mage Beam can now deal damage to Fairies.
  * Replaced Fetchur's Nether Quartz Ore quest with Emerald.
  * Fixed being able to join Dungeons Master Mode although not meeting the requirements.
  * Fixed fire ticks not being removed when teleporting to a Dungeon boss room.
  * Dungeon Chests now list Dungeon Chest Key in their cost when applicable.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
