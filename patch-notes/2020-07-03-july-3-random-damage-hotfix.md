# [July 3] Random Damage Hotfix

Hello!  
  
Since 0.7.11 we fixed 3 things impacting player damage:  


  1. Fixed random damage (it was caused by all weapons applying the "Fabled" modifier on crit, even if they don't have it)
  2. Fixed Dark Goggles applying its damage bonus against all mobs
  3. Fixed Ability Damage Scaling applying differently for weapons with built-in intelligence bonuses (like Yeti Sword) and modifiers (like Heroic)

We also made a few bugfixes in the background which shouldn't impact gameplay.  
  
Enjoy!
