**Small Bugfixes**  


  * Fixed /coopadd not checking for co-op invitation settings
  * Prevent abilities being used on launch pads when traveling between islands to avoid beginners from terminating their own lives by misclicking
  * Prevent players from creating a pet rule too similar to one they already have so they don't turbo-spam the chat when farming wheat and stuff
  * Update the Quiver Arrow for more conditions like dropping a bow, picking up a bow, selling a bow...
  * Added the Personal Vault button to the solo Bank menu when accessed from the Personal Bank
  * Music Pants now properly auto-play when joining a server, but you have to move out of spawn a little bit
  * Stop grinches from dropping infinite gifts by healing them
  * Prevent guest vehicles (pig pet, etc..) from interacting on islands
  * Added a 10 stacks limit to Werewolf armor to avoid abusive cases
  * Fixed the Deep Storage achievement
  * Fixed the bazaar misreading your inventory being full when claiming a buy order. Another solution is underway regarding stash being full
  * Stopped Kalhuiki Door Guardian from scamming keys when the door is already open
  * Fixed Obsidian Sanctuary diamond blocks not increasing diamond collection
  * Updated the Farming Talisman to make it work in the updated mushroom desert
  * Prevent fished coins from being grabbed by hoppers to create null skulls

**Smaller Bugfixes**  


  * Renamed an NPC who was named Terry to Tony
  * Changed delimitations of the Pigmen's Den and Lapis Quarry to avoid the wrong blocks being generated in the wrong zone
  * Now clearing the personal compactor filters when auctioning to avoid nasty fish surprises when buying one
  * Remove the enchant glint when removing all enchants from an item
  * Fixed Aspiring Leap cooldown
  * Fixed bone meal not dying default leather armor
  * Disabled Mooshrooms exploding from shears and producing vanilla mushroom stew

That's it for today, more to come!  
_Note: The update is currently being rolled out and not all fixes might be live yet._
