# [January 20] SkyBlock 0.24.1 Release Candidate

Hello everyone!  
  
Today we are opening alpha with the Release Candidate for **SkyBlock 0.24.1**. This patch mostly consists of bugfixes, with one new feature - Swappable Pet Items.  
  


Spoiler: 1/26 Garden Changes

  * Changed the Rosewater Flask to cap at 4 Growth Stages and work on the Greenhouse Plot the player is standing in
  * Added Decay Time to Diagnostic Tool
  * Added Water Lasting Time to Diagnostic Tool
  * Added message in Diagnostic Tool Menu to show if a crop has enough water to fully grow
  * Improved time displays in Diagnostic Tool Menu
  * Added the item amount to the message given when dropping rare items from pests
  * Added a message to the Plot Limit upgrade icon, clarifying that new plots come with the slots already unlocked
  * Added Sunflower Oil and Bachelor's Rose to the Bazaar
  * Added lore to the Voter's Badge accessories to show progress towards the next tier
  * Fixed Sunflowers and Wild Roses planted in the Barn plot not growing and popping off as melon stems
  * Fixed Jake's Plushie saying "Invalid reward item" in chat when trying to open the claim menu from chat
  * Fixed Pig Pet being unable to trample Wild Rose, Sunflower, and Moonflower
  * Fixed Pig Pet being unable to trample any crops sometimes
  * Fixed Walk Speed cap being lower than expected with the Sundial and Rancher's Boots when interacting with Cactus Knives and Young Dragon Armor
  * Fixed being unable to give a Bachelor's Rose to the current mayor
  * Other mayor candidates will now say they are not eligible for a rose
  * Fixed the Warty drop rate being lower than expected
  * Fixed Sunflowers and Wild Roses not dropping Rarefinder Chips
  * Fixed visitors giving low amounts of Farming Experience for Wild Rose, Sunflower, and Moonflower
  * Fixed Voter's Badge counting all mayors served instead of unique mayors served



  


### **► Swappable Pet Items**​

Starting today, you can now freely replace your pet’s held item without deleting the old item. You will be refunded the old item once it's replaced:  
  


![SwappablePetItems.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/swappablepetitems-png.3534314/)

  
The cost of the swap process depends upon the rarity of the item being **replaced** :  


  * **COMMON** : 25,000
  * **UNCOMMON** : 50,000
  * **RARE** : 100,000
  * **EPIC** : 250,000
  * **LEGENDARY** : 500,000
  * **MYTHIC** : 1,000,000 (Futureproofing)
  * **DIVINE** : 2,500,000 (Futureproofing)
