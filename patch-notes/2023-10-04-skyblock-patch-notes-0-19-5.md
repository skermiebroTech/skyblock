# SkyBlock Patch Notes 0.19.5

![1696429854671.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1696429854671-png.3252563/)

  
Hey guys! Here are the patch notes for **SkyBlock Patch 0.19.5**. Today's update is a fairly minor patch with many bug fixes and adds many quality of life changes!  
  


### **►** Inferno Minion Economy Update​

Following our [announcement thread](https://hypixel.net/threads/inferno-minion-design-heads-up.5499078/#post-38837025), we have made the following changes to the Inferno Minion and Gabagool in order to reduce the coins cost associated with Blaze Slayer content:  


### Hypergolic Fuel Drop Rates​

Inferno Minions running Hypergolic-tier fuel will have higher drop rates:  


  * 2.8x more [Inferno Vertex](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Inferno_Vertex)
  * 20% more [Inferno Apex](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Inferno_Apex)
  * 15% more [Chili Peppers](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Chili_Pepper)



### Other Cost-Slashing Measures​

  * Hypergolic-tier fuels now take half the amount of Heavy Fuels (from 24 to 12, removing the corners of the crafting grid)
  * Gabagool distillates used to be a RARER drop than other distillates. It is now a more FREQUENT drop than other distillates.
    * Before, each distillate had 900 weight, except 500 for Gabagool
    * Now, each distillate has a 700 weight, except 1400 for Gabagool
  * Inferno Minion action speed has been multiplied by 0.92x, meaning it’s about 9% faster.

### Other Inferno/Blaze Changes​

  * Inferno Demonlord now has good chances of dropping some Enchanted Blaze Powder as an EXTRA drop.
  * The Coldfused Reforge (from [Chili Peppers Collection](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Entropy_Suppressor)) now adds +15/20/25/35/45/55 Strength and +20/30/40/50/60/75 Crit Damage to the daggers.
  * Added Very Crude Gabagool item, which can be compacted using an Super Compactor 3000.
  * Cayenne Enchant now also grants 0.1 true defense per digit, per enchant level.
  * Amalgated Crimsonite recipe will now output 2 at a time (halved cost!)
    * Amalgated Crimsonite can also be crafted en-masse with the enchanted block/cube variants of the materials!
    * Enchanted Glowstone now costs 160 Enchanted Glowstone instead of 196.

  


### **►** Island Entry Requirement and Crimson Isle Changes​

We have lowered the requirements to enter the following zones:  


  * Crimson Isle entry requirement has been lowered from Combat 24 to Combat 22.
    * All items from the Crimson Isle have had their item requirements lowered from Combat 24 to Combat 22.
  * Zealot Bruiser Hideout entry requirement has been lowered from Combat 22 to Combat 20.

While the Crimson Isle is intended to be mainly for endgame combat content, there are many other content on the island that players at earlier stages could still enjoy. In order to make the island more hospitable for newer players, we have nerfed the HP and damage of most mobs in the Crimson Isle. Combat EXP gained from these mobs have also been adjusted accordingly. Hopefully these changes will allow more players to enjoy the Crimson Isle’s expansive content!

    * Added a way to reset your burrows in case it's still happening, or if you want easier burrows after upgrading your Griffin and realizing the mobs are maybe a bit too tough:
