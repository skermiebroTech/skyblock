**► Dungeonbreaker**  


![dungeonbreaker.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/dungeonbreaker-png.3498106/)

  
_Where Dungeonbreaker?_ ​

The Dungeonbreaker is a new pickaxe that can be purchased at Ophelia for 500 Wither Essence. It allows you to mine through most blocks in the Catacombs.  
  
The Dungeonbreaker has a couple of limitations:  


  * The number of blocks you can break is limited by how many Charges you have available
    * Charges regenerate at 2 blocks per second
    * You also regenerate up to 5 Charges when collecting a Secret with the new Echoes of the Lost perk
      * This perk can be found in the Wither Essence shop
  * You cannot break blocks around room boundaries
  * Crypts can't be broken with the Dungeonbreaker. They will still require Superboom TNT or an equivalent explosion effect, as previously

  
With the release of the Dungeonbreaker, you will no longer be able to "stonk" with previous methods. This allows us to enable anticheat measures that were previously disabled due to having to allow "stonking" as a mechanic.  
  
The Dungeonbreaker is an important step in moving towards a fully latest version experience, as stonking was a vanilla exploit only found in Minecraft 1.8. While the Dungeonbreaker is designed for the latest version functionality in mind, it should be fully functional in older versions. However, we still encourage you to update your Minecraft version (and mods!) as soon as possible since a lot of future content will most definitely require the latest version.  
  
  
**► /pity**  


![pity.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/pity-png.3498107/)

  
​

RNGesus not on your side? Say hello to /pity! This new menu keeps track of various new and pre-existing pity across the game. The addition of /pity does not mean we are discontinuing RNG Meters - this system just allows us to add hard pity for smaller systems that aren't large or complex enough to warrant a dedicated meter. The Pity menu can also be accessed through the /rng menu, or by typing /pity directly.

**► Ironman W's**  
There's a handful of improvements we've made to Ironman Profiles with this update.  


### **➥ Ironman Shen's Auctions**​

Ironman profiles will no longer be bidding against Normal profiles in Shen's Auctions to help level the playing field a bit more. There will be about half the regular quantities of items available in Shen's Auction and Special. We anticipate the numbers will need tweaking, but we want to see how it plays out first.  
  
Keep in mind that purchasing any items from Shen's Auction or Special will Co-op Soulbound the item.  


### **➥ Convert To Normal Profile ("De-iron")**​

![convert_to_normal_profile.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/convert_to_normal_profile-png.3498108/)

  
Tired of the Ironman experience? Solo and Co-op Ironman profiles now have the ability to convert to a Normal profile, although the process is a bit different for both.  
  
If you're on a Solo Ironman profile, you can choose to start the conversion in the Profile Management menu. In most cases, you'll be required to wait a certain amount of time (up to 35 days) in case you change your mind. Once the timer is up, you'll be asked one last time if you are absolutely sure you want to de-iron, as the process is permanent!  
  
For Ironman Co-ops, the process is a little different. Once someone in the Co-op wishes to convert, it will start a vote that lasts 3 days. During this time, if any player in the Co-op votes "NO" or fails to vote, the conversion process will stop. If the Co-op unanimously votes "YES", then the countdown starts (up to 35 days). If during this time any player in the Co-op has second thoughts, they are able to cancel the conversion process in the Profile Management menu.  


### **➥ Cosmetic Auction House**​

![cosmetics_browser.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/cosmetics_browser-png.3498109/)

  
Ironman profiles are now able to purchase _most_ cosmetics listed on the Auction House (they are still not able to sell any). When opening the Auction House menu, they'll now see a custom browser with filters for various cosmetic types.  
  
For Normal profiles, we've replaced the Blocks tab in the Auction House with a Cosmetics tab so that players can more easily find cosmetics. There wasn't much going on in the Blocks tab previously, and most of its items can now be found in the Tools & Misc tab instead.  
  
On a side note, Ironman profiles can now purchase a custom amount of Booster Cookies from the Ironman Bazaar.  


### **➥ Ironman Guild**​

![ironman_guild.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/ironman_guild-png.3499551/)

  
Found above spawn in the Dwarven Mines, the Ironman Guild is an in-universe Guild with Ironman Dwarves. If you talk to Ian of Ironmen, he'll explain Ironman profiles, and he'll even open a menu for you to create an Ironman profile right there if you want.
