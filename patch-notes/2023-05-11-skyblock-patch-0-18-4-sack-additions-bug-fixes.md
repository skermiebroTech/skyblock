# SkyBlock Patch 0.18.4 - Sack Additions & Bug Fixes

![sb-18-4.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-18-4-png.3200929/)

  
  
Howdy gamers! SkyBlock is back at it again with a round of bug fixes and small QoL changes, including several new **Sack Items**.  
  
Read more below for the full list of changes!  
  
  
**►** **Changes**  
**General**  


  * Corruption enchant is now applicable to Fishing Weapons.
  * Items with more than 1 ingredient in their recipe can now be compacted.
    * Enchanted Golden Carrot
    * Enchanted Cookie
  * Added _/sbbingo_ alias to Bingo command.

**Sprays**  


  * Due to improving how we load sprays into the game, we've had to change the layout of the sprays in the Spray Can menu. They now show alphabetically, and we've added some fancy filters to find what you're looking for!



![1683669902744.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/1683669902744-png.3200586/)

**►** **Bug Fixes**  


  * Dungeons RNG Meter now properly resets progress when the selected drop is found randomly.
  * Fixed some Elle dialogue grammar issues.
  * Fixed Teleport Pad bulk trade options in Trades menu.
  * Fixed Ammonite Forge recipe lore sometimes being incorrect.
  * Renamed "Melon" to "Melon Block" to prevent some confusion between Melon and Melon Blocks.
  * Fixed Crop Milestone progress going negative.
  * Fixed Finnegan's GOATed perk being active even when he isn't elected mayor.
  * Fixed Block Zapper not giving a message when it does nothing sometimes.
  * Fixed Stranded Gardens trying to send players to the hub when the server restarts.
  * Fixed a bunch of minion personality skins having the wrong tier icon on their head.
  * Fixed being able to use Great White Tooth meal as bone meal to grow flowers.
  * Fixed Commission Milestones not showing yellow colored panes for the next tier.
  * Fixed the Personal Deletor not working on loot from frozen treasure.
  * Allowed muted players to complete the quest to earn Kuudra Follower Boots.
  * Fixed the colors of garden visitors to show their rarity again.
  * Cropie and squash drops now directly go into sacks.
  * Prevented banner items being put into the Builder's Wand and Builder's Ruler.
  * Fixed the Travel to the Park objective getting cut off on the scoreboard.
  * Fixed some temporary stat boosts, such as the Rogue Sword speed boost not expiring correctly.
  * Fixed gravel minions with Flint Shovel producing less flint than they should when Derpy is the active mayor.
  * Fixed being able to place Spirit Sceptres in flower pots.
  * Fixed being able to get vanilla water bottles sometimes when filling up glass bottles.
  * Fixed tree minions not recognizing the correct type of saplings if they have higher data values.
  * Changed the Gold Forger NPC to always reward 15 Copper and 2-5 Gold Essence.
  * Changed redstone key rooms in Dungeons so the key is no longer a physical item; anyone can unlock the lock once the key has been found.
  * Piggy Bank now properly displays that it works in the Accessory Bag.
  * Kuudra Teeth Plaque's lore is now less impaired by grammar.
  * The Dwarven Tankard has been added to Rusty's shop.
  * The Dungeon Sack now shows the correct icons for items.
  * Tal Ker & Trevor the Trapper speaks with more better English now.
  * Catacombs RNG meter is now more grammatically correct.
  * Fixed the incorrect Game Stages shown for Gold/Diamond Hunter armor and their matching accessories.
  * Fixed multiple SkyBlock Guide tasks not being marked as complete.
  * Fixed Horse Trapper mob showing particles to the nearest player instead of the player that is hunting it.
  * The Cocoa Chopper is now properly spelled in all places of the game.
  * Small Gemstone Sack now has the correct tier as Uncommon.
  * Fixed the Chomp Reforge overriding the Frail enchantment's health removal.
  * Cannot claim auctions across your deleted profiles anymore.
  * Fixed Venomous re-applying damage modifiers on its poison ticks.
  * Fixed anvils receiving falling damage
  * Fixed throwing Silent Pearls at illegal locations returning a normal Ender Pearl.
  * Fixed Farm Armor not working on all zones of the Mushroom Desert island.
  * Fixed Witch Mask Bats attacking Creepers generated by a Wither Cloak Sword.
  * Fixed Enchanted Firework Rockets being able to be placed.
  * Fixed Auction House accepting invalid items.
  * Fixed Witch Mask Bats attacking Bats from Bat Firework.
  * Fixed Skeleton Master Chestplate ability not working with Artisanal/Juju Shortbows and Terminator.
  * Fixed lava visually removing items from Showcase Blocks.
  * Hid some messages that reference Auction House and Bazaar while on Ironman mode.
  * Fixed Amelia selling items without the required Social Skill Level.
  * Added Goldor's and Maxor's armors sell price.

Enjoy!
