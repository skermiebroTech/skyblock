# [August 3rd] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hi all,  
  
Today we conducted some maintenance to the backend of Dungeons and Kuudra to migrate them over to a new unified system. This new system fixes a lot of the inconsistencies between the two instanced modes and contains many under-the-hood improvements, bug fixes and QOL changes:  
  
**System Changes**  


  * Dungeons and Kuudra can now be re-joined directly if you accidently leave SkyBlock by simply re-joining SkyBlock.
    * __If you leave to another island within SkyBlock, you will still need to be party warped back in.__
  * The party leader can now instantly requeue a Dungeons or Kuudra instance when it has finished by clicking on the prompt in chat displayed after the end of the instance.
    * _This should remove the need for players to re-party after each completion._
  * The queuing system will now retry up to 3 times to queue you into an instance upon error or generation failure.
    * _This change should help prevent players getting locked out of an instance due to queuing cooldowns._
  * Some menus and inventories that were previously disabled in Kuudra after the Rift update have been re-enabled.
  * Players will now respawn with 3 seconds of invulnerability after being revived in Dungeons and Kuudra.
  * Players will no longer take damage before a Dungeon or Kuudra fight has started or after they have ended.

  
**Other Changes**  


  * The Archer Ghost Stun Bow ability now actually works in Dungeons. ![:\)](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/joypixels/assets/8.0/png/unicode/64/1f642.png)
  * The Watcher mob spawning animations in the Blood Room have been sped up by about 25%.
  * Telekinesis is now no longer disabled in Dungeons.
  * Additional checks have been put in to prevent players from skipping directly to the boss fight ("blood skipping").

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: Solo Dungeons will be coming very soon. Also, we are still tweaking and making improvements to this system, and are still monitoring the forums for feedback on the changes made._
