# [December 18] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Jacob's Contests showing inaccurate rewards for contests that occurred before the Greenhouse update (either omitting contests entirely, or showing different ones)
  * Fixed a simulation issue with the Greenhouse when loading the Garden leading to unusually fast growth cycles
  * Fixed Birch trees not regrowing in The Park
  * Fixed Greenhouse upgrades giving 5x more Skyblock XP outside of the Garden
  * Increased Rarefinder Chip drop chances again
  * Added Sunflower and Wild Rose to preset icons
  * Lowered the amount of commissions that need to be completed before Beth and Carpenter appear again
  * Fixed a layout issue in the Gemstone Grinder
  * Fixed a Beth quest softlock if a player accepted her offer without talking to her on the Farming Island first
  * Fixed being unable to change your Greenhouse skin if you only have one Greenhouse
  * Allowed the new crops to give pity towards Overclocker
  * Allowed the new crops to drop the Rarefinder Chip
  * Fixed custom presets dropping new crops when they shouldn't
  * Fixed the drop rates of Cropie, Squash, and Fermento when wearing the Fermento or Helianthus armor set
  * Fixed the 4th Gemstone slot unlock costing a perfect gem for the Wild Rose and Eclipse hoes
  * Prevented the Rosewater Flask from being blessed by Oruo
  * Added a co-op wide cooldown of 2 hours on using the Rosewater Flask
  * Increased the recipe cost of the Rosewater Flask from 16 -> 32 Compacted Wild Rose
  * Reduced pest drops for Cricket, Moth and Slug to reflect the recipe changes to Enchanted Golden Carrot, Enchanted Cookie and Enchanted Mushroom Blocks



Spoiler: Additional Changes

  * Fixed Visitors asking for too many Mushrooms, Cocoa and Carrots
  * Reduced base crop drops for Greenhouse for Mushrooms, Cocoa and Carrots
  * Reduced crop drops from the Gloomgourd mutation by 65%



  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
