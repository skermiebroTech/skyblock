# [December 13] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed an issue with Mayor Foxy and the Spooky Event preventing the event from playing out properly
  * Kuudra - Reduced Fuel Cells needed to fuel the Ballista on Phase 3 from 5->4
  * Kuudra - Reduced player melee/bow damage dealt when Kuudra is stunned by ~~50%~~ 25%
  * Kuudra - Increased cannon damage to Dropships by 50%
  * Kuudra - Increased maximum damage increase of the Steady Aim Perk to ~~15%~~ 25%
  * Kuudra - Fixed Nuke Perk insta-killing all mobs instead of dealing 80% of their health
  * Dungeons - Possible fix for dragon prioritization in the Wither King fight on M7

**UPDATE 12/14/22:**  


  * Kuudra - Buffed player melee/bow damage when Kuudra is stunned by 25%
  * Kuudra - Increased maximum damage increase of the Steady Aim Perk to 25% with 2 more upgrade tiers
  * Kuudra - Increase Phase 4 health from 200m -> 300m
  * Kuudra - Increased base Ballista damage on Infernal Tier by 5%

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
