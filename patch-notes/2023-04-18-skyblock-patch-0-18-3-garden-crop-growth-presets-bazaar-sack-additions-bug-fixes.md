**►** **Crop Growth Changes**  
After releasing The Garden, it became clear that growth rates were not balanced properly, but we couldn't change them with our existing code. So, we've spent the last few weeks tweaking it to satisfaction. We will monitor the current speeds and will make changes if necessary, and hopefully after this, players should be able to properly set up farming loops on their islands.  


  * Crops now grow 10% faster per garden level.
  * No more light requirements for any crops.
  * Mushrooms will now only spread on podzol and mycelium.
  * Mushrooms will only grow on the same y-axis, on the 8 blocks immediately surrounding another mushroom.
  * Soil mechanics (for wheat, potatoes, carrots, melon stems, and pumpkin stems):
    * Crops will not grow on completely dry soil.
    * Crops will grow with a 40% speed decrease on soil that is not at full moisture.
    * Crops will have no debuff on fully moist soil.
  * Mycelium no longer spreads.

  
**►** **Garden Preset Changes**  
Some of our pre-included presets were not up to standards. We've improved them for your convenience, and added a preset to allow you to reset a plot completely.  


  * Added a super flat default preset.
  * Improved Melon and Pumpkin presets.
  * The default mushroom preset now has more space for mushrooms to grow.
  * The default cactus preset now has more rows, with less cactus in each row.

**►** **Trophy Fish Pity System**  
A new pity system has been introduced to help you when you get unlucky during Trophy Fishing:  


  * When you fish 100th of the same fish, you are guaranteed to get a Gold Tier fish if it wasn't obtained before
  * When you fish 1,000th of the same fish, you are guaranteed to get a Diamond Tier if it wasn't obtained before
