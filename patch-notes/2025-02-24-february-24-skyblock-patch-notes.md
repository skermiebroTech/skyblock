# [February 24] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Howdy folks!  
  
We've got a few quick bug fixes for you, including the following:  


  * Disabled Berberis Blowgun in the Time Chamber zone.
  * Disabled Player kill credit from Eleanor the 10th mirror mobs.
  * Disabled mob drops from Eleanor the 10th mirror mobs.
  * Disabled Skeleton Hat in Rift mode.
  * Disabled Beach Balls in Crystal Hollows & Mineshaft modes.
  * Improved stability regarding some Trapper mob spawning logic.
  * Changed the Defense values for the Tidal enchantment to 5%, 10% and 15%.

If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
