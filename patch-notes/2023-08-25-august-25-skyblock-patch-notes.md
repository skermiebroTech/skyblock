# [August 25] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hello, here is another quick patch following the [chunky 0.19.3](https://hypixel.net/threads/skyblock-patch-0-19-3-crystal-hollows-qol-bug-fixes.5465058/) update:  


  * Added text notification telling players to right-click to open loot chests when instantly unlocked with the Great Explorer perk.
  * Fixed adding the same items to personal compactor/deletor.
  * Fixed Prehistoric Eggs always yielding COMMON armadillos.
  * Fixed the cost of shiny gear and discs in dungeon chests.
  * Fixed an issue with slayer bosses not dying... properly.
  * Fixed some instances of being unable to claim commissions.

That's it!
