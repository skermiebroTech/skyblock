# [January 13] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Greenhouse offline calculation not accounting for bonuses in time calculation
  * Fixed melon and pumpkin stems not growing in The Garden sometimes
  * Fixed being able to place blocks above the 10 height limit in Barn plots
  * Fixed Moonflowers not applying their Greenhouse effect
  * Updated Greenhouse Crop Guide with better clarification for Mushrooms and Sunflowers/Moonflowers
  * Fixed Jerry not spawning when breaking new crops during Jerrypocalypse
  * Added new crops to the Wild Strawberry Dye's description
  * Fixed Greenhouse models not appearing when visiting an offline player's Garden
  * Fixed Moonflower and Sunflower counting as separate crops for the unique crop buff
  * Balanced Farming XP from Greenhouse harvesting
  * Fixed an issue with /show and re-enabled it

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
