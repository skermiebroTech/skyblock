# [June 19] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Removed Grand Feast as perk provided by Finnegan when selected by Jerry's Perkpocolypse.
  * Fixed pearls failing to teleport you when thrown at corners.
  * Marked many bugged items as CO-OP Soulbound.  
These items were never officially intended to be obtainable, but they found their way into the game due to various bugs and exploits. We are aware many players collect them as vanity items, so we do not plan on actively removing them. However, they will not be officially supported and if they do cause issues, they will be removed. These items are often abused for boosting, so we are marking them as soulbound to prevent them from being traded between players.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
