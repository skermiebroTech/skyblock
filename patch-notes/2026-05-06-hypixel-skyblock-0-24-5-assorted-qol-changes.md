# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.24.5 - Assorted QoL Changes

![sb-24-5.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-24-5-png.3569003/)

  
  
Hello! Today we are releasing a small patch containing quality-of-life changes to several areas of the game. Keep reading for all the details!   
  
  
************► List of Changes************  


### **➥ Combat** ​

  * Added a 2x coin multiplier to Crown of Avarice upon reaching a 1B counter
  * Increased cooldown of the "Life Recovery" Attribute from 0.2s -> 0.5s
  * Moved several items from slayer "extra" tables to main:
    * Festering Maggot
    * Enchanted Blaze Powder (Weight reduced to 1500)
    * High Class Archfiend Dice
  * Added a floor-wide drop message for more rare Dungeons items obtained from end-of-floor chests
    * Also added a more exciting variant for particularly desirable drops, such as the Necron's Handle, which also shows up when opening chests at Croesus.
  * Adjusted Jerry Boxes to now go straight into the inventory instead of being left on the floor
  * Adjusted Soulflow Engine to work with Catalysts
  * Updated the quiver arrow to display the current arrow's stats in its itemlore
  * Adjusted the Horse Pet and Skeleton Horse Pets into skulls instead of ridable mobs. Also gave them new perks
  * Adjusted Tormentor:
    * Replaced 2x Sapphire Gemstone Slots -> 2 Combat Gemstone Slots
    * Added +30 Strength
    * Reduced "Angered" extra damage taken from 2x -> 50%
    * Added new ability named "Chain of Agony"
      * Each melee hit arcs with cursed energy, chaining to up to 5 nearby mobs dealing 100% damage (arcs jump from mob to mob, max range of curse jumps are 4 blocks)
      * Cost: 200 Mana
      * Cooldown: 0.5 seconds
      * Toggleable
  * Reduced Final Destination Armor's crafting requirement from Enderman Slayer 4 -> 3

  
  


### **➥ Farming** ​

  * Added Tal Ker as a Garden Visitor
  * Renamed "Garden" section of /pity to "The Garden"
  * Fixed St. Jerry and Hoppity visiting The Garden outside of their respective events
  * Fixed being able to plant crops in the Greenhouse that you haven't unlocked yet

  
  


### **➥ Fishing** ​

  * Added the functionality of your current bait being shown in slot #9 while fishing
  * Added the Personal Sea Creature Cap from the Crimson Isle to every island in the game. Caps at 10 SC/player
    * **Designer Note: **Individual players hoarding large amounts of Sea Creatures on certain islands was causing grievous amounts of lag and preventing others from fishing due to the island-wide Sea Creature cap previously being shared by all players
  * Adjusted Magma Lord Gauntlet to now be a Necklace and buffed its stats:
    * Increased SCC from 1.5% -> 2.5%
    * Increased HP from 35 -> 45
    * Increased rarity from EPIC -> LEGENDARY
    * Buffed "Lord's Blessing" tiered bonus:
      * 4/5 -> +20 Magic Find on Magmatic mobs
      * 5/5 -> +30 Magic Find on Magmatic mobs
  * Removed all unrelated Shards from Thunder & Lord Jawbus loot-pools
  * Reduced the Grinch's HP from 5 -> 3

  
  


### **➥ Hunting** ​

  * Added indicative text in the Attributes menu to show when one Attribute is being buffed by another
  * Added a visual indicator of how many more Shards are needed to max out an Attribute in /attributes
  * Improved the Attribute level-up message
  * Added "Demon" family to Hellwisp Shard
  * Swapped the rarities & IDs of the Burningsoul and Hellwisp attributes
    * Burningsoul -> Rarity swapped from LEGENDARY -> EPIC, ID changed from L27 -> E29
    * Hellwisp -> Rarity swapped from EPIC -> LEGENDARY, ID changed from E29-> L27
      * **Designer Note: **To prevent players from losing any attribute levels, levels of these attributes have been swapped. E.g if you had Burningsoul 10 and Hellwisp 7, you now have Hellwisp 10 and Burningsoul 7.
  * Renamed Burningsoul Shard -> Demonlord Shard - now drops from the Inferno Demonlord boss:
    * T1 -> N/A
    * T2 -> 250 weight
    * T3 -> 500 weight
    * T4 -> 1,000 weight
      * **Designer Note: **This series of changes should lead to players no longer feeling the need to spawn slayer minibosses to farm for shards without wanting to actually fight the boss, meaning people actually doing the slayer will be contested less and those wanting to grind shards for fusions can instead do so in the area where Hellwisps spawn.
  * Adjusted Mass Syphon button's itemlore to now list all Shards that will be syphoned if pressed
  * Renamed "Big Game Hunter" attribute -> "Mythological Tracker"
  * Renamed "Maximal Torment" attribute -> "Unlimited Torment"
  * Renamed "Paramount Fortitude" attribute -> "Unlimited Fortitude"
  * Huntraps placed in the "Matriarch's Lair" zone will now always catch a Hellwisp Shard
  * Huntraps placed in the "Smoldering Tomb" will now always catch a Cinderbat Shard

  


### **➥ Mining** ​

  * Added the ability to salvage some items for Fossil Essence:
    * Claw Fossil (25 Fossil Essence)
    * Helix Fossil (25)
    * Footprint Fossil (50)
    * Clubbed Fossil (50)
    * Webbed Fossil (50)
    * Ugly Fossil (50)
    * Spine Fossil (50)
    * Tusk Fossil (50)
  * Adjusted how Gemstones are displayed on items - there is now a specific "Gemstones:" line in itemlore
  * Adjusted "Crystal Serendipity" Attribute to work on all Crystal Hollows Treasure Chests - reduced values from 2-20% -> 1-10% as a result
  * Adjusted Jotraeline Greatforge to now ask for an Abiphone Basic as part of her Abiphone Contact quest
  * Adjusted Nest Endermites to now consider their spawner their owner for Bestiary/Lootshare purposes
  * Removed Claw Fossils and Helix Fossils from the Fossil Excavator
  * Fixed some sand not being harvestable in the Mushroom Desert

  


### **➥ Spider Slayer** ​

  * Added the ability for the "Piercing" enchantment to damage multiple Tarantula Broodfather egg sacs at once
  * Added a message for when The Primordial / Bloodshot Reforge activates
  * Added a prompt in tier 3 telling players to shoot Tarantula Broodfather's egg sacs when the phase begins
  * Adjusted the Tarantula Broodfather's "Web of Lies" animation to now be significantly faster
  * Adjusted Tarantula Broodfather's "Backstab" in accordance with the mob dealing AOE damage
  * Adjusted Cocooned mobs to now belong to the player who cocooned them
  * Adjusted Mosquito Shortbow so that the item's internal ability cooldown is now explicitly stated on the item
  * Adjusted Egg Sacs to be affected by Swing Range
  * Adjusted Tarantula Broodfather's "Noxious Paralysis" to now reduce your Vitality & Speed by a flat amount
  * Reduced HP of Primordial Bats
  * Fixed Arachne and Broods being able to exit Arachne's Sanctuary
  * Fixed a Tarantula Slayer RNG Meter exploit 
  * Related to the above, reduced Ananke Feather effectiveness on Tarantula Broodfather from 500k -> 300k

  


### **➥ Other** ​

  * Added a feature to Mayor Voting menus that will show upcoming Special Mayors ahead of time
  * Added an Area Discovery message for the Mob Types House
  * Added Smite 7, Ender Slayer 7, and Bane of Arthropods 7 support to /legacytrades
  * Prevented Dyes from appearing back-to-back in Vincent
  * Bingo Pet's “Mountain Climber” perk now applies to Heart of the Forest, and has been renamed to “Climber”
  * Adjusted Century Party Invitation and Fruit Bowl to now be consumed upon completion
  * Adjusted nametags of several NPCs to display their job/title above their name instead of below
  * Adjusted the color of the Farming for Dummies modifier from DARK GREEN -> LIGHT GREEN
  * Adjusted the color of the Foraging Booster modifiers from LIGHT BLUE -> DARK GREEN
  * Recategorized some Attributes:
    * Lapis Creeper: Combat -> Mining
    * Thyst: Combat -> Mining
    * Star Sentry: Combat -> Mining
    * Fungloom: Combat -> Mining
    * Spike: Foraging -> Combat
    * Hideoncave: Global -> Mining
    * Silentdepth: Global -> Mining
    * Shellwise: Global -> Combat
  * Unsoulbound some items:
    * Pre-digestion Fish
    * Future Calories Talisman
  * Fixed the SkyBlock guide showing Harp songs as incomplete despite achieving their 90% requirement
  * Fixed the XP gain Autopet rule not triggering when Taming XP is selected and gained
  * Adjusted Slayer RNG Meters to now only roll your selected drop upon reaching 100% - no longer rolls for other drops

  
And that's all for today!
