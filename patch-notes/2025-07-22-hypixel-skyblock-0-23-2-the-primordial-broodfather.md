# Hypixel SkyBlock 0.23.2 - The Primordial Broodfather

![sb-23-2.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/sb-23-2-png.3466205/)

  
  
Hello! Welcome to **Hypixel SkyBlock Patch 0.23.2**. Today's update brings you some long-awaited slayer content and fixes to other parts of the game.  
  
  


### **► The Primordial Broodfather**​

The **Primordial Broodfather** is the fifth and final tier Spider Slayer boss, being a step up from the Tier V Revenant Horror. It brings with it not just several exciting new drops that aim to alter the overarching slayer progression, but also several key revamps to older items.  
  


### ➥ Boss Abilities​

We didn't just add a fifth tier to Spider Slayer and call it quits - we instead took the time to rebuild the slayer from the ground up, aiming for a more active and varied gameplay experience. Each tier of the boss is now entirely different from what it was before.  
  
**T1 Tarantula Broodfather**  


Spoiler: Stats & Abilities

1,000 HP  
35 Damage  
• **Backstab** : Occasionally leaps behind you. Attacks from behind deal +50% Damage.

  
**T2 Tarantula Broodfather**  


Spoiler: Stats & Abilities

30,000  
55 Damage  


  * **Backstab** : Occasionally leaps behind you. Attacks from behind deal +75% Damage.
  * **Noxious Paralysis** : Reduces your healing by 25% and inflicts a stacking -2.5% debuff to your Speed Cap every 5th time you get damaged.



  
**T3 Tarantula Broodfather**  


Spoiler: Stats & Abilities

900,000 HP  
260 Damage  
  


  * **Backstab** : Occasionally leaps behind you. Attacks from behind deal +100% Damage.
  * **Noxious Paralysis** : Reduces your healing by 50% and inflicts a stacking -5% debuff to your Speed Cap every 5th time you get damaged.
  * **Web of Lies** : Spews webbing along the ground, disabling teleportation abilities until they are cleared. Also spawns 2 Eggs Sacs, break them before they hatch! (at 66% and 33% Health)



  
**T4 Tarantula Broodfather**  


Spoiler: Stats & Abilities

2.4M  
530 Damage  


  * **Backstab** : Occasionally leaps behind you. Attacks from behind deal +125% Damage.
  * **Noxious Paralysis** : Reduces your healing by 75% and inflicts a stacking -7.5% debuff to your Speed Cap every 5th time you get damaged.
  * **Web of Lies** : Spews webbing along the ground, disabling teleportation abilities until they are cleared. Also spawns 2 Eggs Sacs, break them before they hatch! (at 66% and 33% Health)
  * **Bat Barrage** : Becomes immune to magic damage for the rest of the fight and releases a horde of Bats. Take 1% of Max Health as Damage per second for each Bat alive. (starting at 50% Health)



  
**T5 Primordial Broodfather(NEW!)**  


Spoiler: Stats & Abilities

10,000,000 HP  
3,500 Damage  


  * **Backstab** : Occasionally leaps behind you. Attacks from behind deal +150% Damage.
  * **Noxious Paralysis** : Reduces your healing by 100% and inflicts a stacking -10% debuff to your Speed Cap every 5th time you get damaged.
  * **Web of Lies** : Spews webbing along the ground, disabling teleportation abilities until they are cleared. Also spawns 3 Eggs Sacs, break them before they hatch! (at 66% and 33% Health)
  * **Bat Barrage** : Becomes immune to magic damage for the rest of the fight and releases a horde of Bats. Take 2% of Max Health as Damage per second for each Bat alive. (starting at 66% Health)
  * **Til Death Do Us Part** : Upon initial "death", calls out to the Primordial Broodmother, who will awaken and fuse with the Broodfather, creating the Primordial Brood. The Brood has x2 Health and Damage of the Primordial Broodfather but only uses passive abilities.



  


### ➥ New Items​

Please note that we are aware of just how much Spider Slayer RNG Meter XP some players have stockpiled over the years. While we will not be removing any Meter XP from players, we will also not allow for the new chase items to be redeemed in the RNG Meter for a little while after the release of this update. We have made this choice to prevent the market from being flooded on day 1 and to give everyone an equal chance at obtaining the rarer drops.

### ➥ Other Spider Slayer Changes​

The following changes have been made to existing parts of Spider Slayer.  


Spoiler: Other Spider Slayer Changes

  * Added an ability that buffs the base stats of the Tarantula Pet by up to 50% to the Tarantula Leggings.
  * Added a "Webcutter" ability to the Recluse Fang - allows you to cut through webs left by the new Broodfather.
  * Added the ability to dungeonize the Scorpion Foil.
  * Added a free Combat Gemstone Slot to the Scorpion Foil.
  * Adjusted Spider Egg Mixin to now grant +15 Crit Damage.
  * Adjusted Scorpion Foil's "Heartstopper" ability - now has a flat 2s cooldown.
  * Adjusted Mosquito Bow - it is now a Shortbow.
  * Adjusted Slayer Sacks to now unlock at Zombie Slayer II (Small), Spider Slayer II (Medium), and Wolf Slayer II (Large).
  * Increased the amount of Combat XP required to spawn Spider Slayer bosses
    * T2: 600→650 Combat XP
    * T3: 1,000→1,500 Combat XP
    * T4: 2,000→3,000 Combat XP
  * Reduced the amount of healing reduction immunity granted by the Tarantula Chestplate.
  * Reduced the amount of Tarantula Webs required in the Tarantula Boots recipe.
  * Reduced the cost of Tarantula Boots' Double Jump ability from 40 -> 20 mana.
  * Removed Recluse Fang's "Squash 'em" ability and replaced it with a +150% Damage to Spiders passive.
  * Replaced all obtainment sources of Bane of Arthropods 7 with the new "Ensnared Snail" endcap item.



  
Please note that any further changes mentioned on the July 8th Alpha Thread that have not yet been implemented are still planned for a later date.  
  
  


### **► Kuudra Swapping Shenanigans**​

  * Added support for swapping your Kuudra Armor Type.
    * The menu can be accessed with the /kuudratransfer command, or at the faction blacksmiths Alwin and Igor at the Crimson Isle.

  
  


### **► Changes to Unclaimed Event Rewards**​

As previously announced on our [last release thread](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-23-1-hinas-chapters-pet-crafting-changes-and-more.5947293/), we have now deployed a change that will cause any Event rewards (Spooky Festival, Carnival, etc.) to expire if it has been 10 or more SkyBlock years since they were given. If you have any rewards sitting around that haven't expired already, now is the time to claim them.  
  
  


### **► Damage Reduction Fixes**​

When it came to damage reduction, some items were working in strange and mysterious ways. The most notable issue is being able to stack 100% damage resistance to get full damage immunity. Fear no more, for they have been corrected!  


  * Adjusted how damage reduction was calculated in Pets, resulting in lower reduction than stated.
  * Adjusted End Stone Sword's ability to now gain damage reduction for every 100 mana consumed (was 5) and added a cap at 20% multiplicative damage reduction.
  * Adjusted the following items from an additive damage reduction to a multiplicative reduction instead:
    * Monster Hunter’s Set Bonus
    * Magma Lord Armor and Magma Lord Gauntlet
    * Taurus Helmet, Flaming Chestplate, Moogma Leggings, Slug Boots
    * Thunder Armor and Thunderbolt Necklace
    * Wither Impact and Wither Shield
    * Soulweaver Gloves
    * Pumpkin Armor
    * Ender Relic/Artifact
    * Nether Artifact
    * Zombie Artifact/Ring/Talisman
    * Spider Artifact/Ring/Talisman
    * Wolf Ring/Talisman
    * Fire Talisman
    * Lava Talisman
    * Wither Relic/Artifact
  * Fixed an issue which allowed all variations of the Sea Creature Accessories to stack their damage reduction together.

  * Fixed some typos and cleared up some descriptions.

  * Fixed Dwarven Mines events color on scoreboard not displaying correctly.
