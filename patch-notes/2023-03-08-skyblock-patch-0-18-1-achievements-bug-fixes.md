# SkyBlock Patch 0.18.1 - Achievements & Bug Fixes

Hello everyone!  
  
Today we rolled a small patch to SkyBlock, including new Achievements and several bugfixes and small changes.  
  
  
► **New Achievements**  


  * _Cleanup Crew -_ Finish clearing a plot in The Garden
  * _Quite the Crowd -_ Have 5 visitors at once in The Garden
  * _Fancy Farming -_ Change your Barn skin in The Garden
  * _Compost Collector_ \- Retrieve 8 compost from the Composter at once
  * _Always Sunny in SkyBlock -_ Place a Day Saver in The Garden
  * _Bountiful Harvest -_ Find a Squash while farming
  * _Forbidden Fruit -_ Wear a full set of Fermento Armor
  * _Player of the People_ \- Serve 50 Unique Visitors in The Garden
  * _Humble Beginnings_ \- Catch all 18 types of Trophy Fish
  * _Wow, that’s useful!_ \- Use the /wiki, /wikithis, or /wikihand command to view the Official Hypixel Wiki
  * _Gonna Put a Spell on You_ \- Use the Hex for the first time
  * _The Perfect Heist_ \- Gather 3 Heavy Pearls from the Matriarch and escape without taking damage
  * _Memories of a Bal_ \- Wear a full set of Divan’s Armor
  * _There Can Only Be One_ \- Kill a Tier 5 Revenant Boss
  * _Climbing the Ranks_ \- Reach SkyBlock Level 40/80/120/160/200
  * _People Pleaser_ \- Serve 5/25/100/250/500 Total Visitors in The Garden
  * _Curator_ \- Donate 10/25/50/100/150 Items to the Museum
  * Added 2 Secret Achievements

  
► **Additional Changes**  


  * Fixed Wardrobe not being unlocked at the right SkyBlock level.
  * Fixed Tia the Fairy not giving a Fairy Soul after completing her Garden offer.
  * Made Sculptor's Axe work on the Garden.
  * Fixed Mycelium Dust affecting a larger area than intended.
  * Fixed Fermento Armor not showing up in the recipe when right-clicking Condensed Fermento.
  * Fixed an issue with Coco Chopper being used to clean plots.
  * Added a toggle inside of the Garden Settings that disables plot holograms.
  * Jake's Plushie is now soulbound and has been added to Rusty.
  * Fixed Wither Impact not being able to be used in the Garden.
  * Made sugar cane and cactus less effective at reducing the time it takes for the next visitor to appear by half.
  * Changed the sell price of Enchanted Red Mushroom Blocks and Enchanted Brown Mushroom Blocks from 2,300 to 20,480 coins.
  * Fixed an issue with the Spaceman asking for way too many mushrooms.
  * Fixed the copper and farming rewards from visitors being slightly wrong.
  * Fixed custom presets not pasting crops sometimes.
  * Fixed Parrot pet not repeating emotes correctly.
  * Fixed not being able to spawn the Builder NPC on Stranded.
  * Fixed losing coins when trying to add a contact to the Abiphone.
  * Added a way to preview Fire Sales through Elizabeth on Stranded.
  * Fixed rarity in Ring of Love chat message when it has been recombobulated.
  * Fixed being able to shear Sheep summoned by Conjuring in Dungeons.
  * Added Red Sand and Mycelium trades to the Trades menu in Stranded.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
