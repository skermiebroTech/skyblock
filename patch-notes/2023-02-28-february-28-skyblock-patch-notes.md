# [February 28] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Scaled Garden Experience rewards from visitors with Garden Level
  * Standardized lore formats between Farmer and Rancher Boots
  * Disabled fire ticking in The Garden
  * Fixed new Enchanted Mushroom Block recipes not working in the Personal Compactor
  * Fixed Farming Talisman not working in The Garden
  * Fixed Cultivating enchantment appearing to not give farming fortune
  * Made Hungry Hiker unlockable after completing his quest
  * Made Jacob and Anita spawn in The Garden next to Sam
  * Fixed undoing Builder's Wand operations using the wrong item name
  * Fixed another issue with Suus

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!  
  
_Please note: We are still balancing and patching this release. This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change._
