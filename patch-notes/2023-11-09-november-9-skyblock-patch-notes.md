# [November 9] SkyBlock Patch Notes

Hey all!  
We are here to inform you about the latest update that was just rolled to SkyBlock!  
  


  * Fixed Sky Mall 5x Titanium buff not working correctly.
  * Fixed incredibly, _incredibly_ low Worm spawn rates without Treasure of the Earth Gold Essence perk.
    * We received a few reports about the 30 second spawn cooldown being global and not by-player. This is not the case, but we assume the low spawn rates made it appear like that to players.
  * Fixed Shadow Fury ability not doing damage to mobs when playing Berserk in Dungeons.
  * Fixed null heads dropping in Floor 3 boss fight.
  * Fixed Endstone Protector message incorrectly appearing on Private Islands.
  * Updated Rezar Abicase and Mithril Golem pet legendary perk descriptions to be more accurate.

  
If you have a bug to report to us, please make a report [HERE!](https://www.hypixel.net/skyblock-bugs/) Thanks to everyone who has reported bugs!
