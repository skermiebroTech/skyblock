# [March 19] Backwater Bayou - Day 1 Patch

Hello there!  
  
Today we've got a few fixes and additions to the game following [SkyBlock 0.22 - The Backwater Bayou](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-22-the-backwater-bayou.5863219/).  


  * Increased the drop rate of each tier of Treasure Dye by 10x.
  * Increased Alligator rarity from RARE to LEGENDARY.
  * Increased Alligator weight from 25 -> 50.
  * Increased Titanoboa Fishing XP from 4.5k -> 10k.
  * Increased the drop rate of Octopus Tendril from 0.1% to 0.2%.
  * Increased the drop rate of Severed Pincer from 0.1% to 0.2%.
  * Adjusted Hotspot Bait's recipe to craft 16x -> 32x Hotspot Bait.
  * Removed the Backwater Bayou requirement from Water Hotspot Sea Creatures.
  * Added Can of Worms to Bazaar.
  * Added Blue Ring to Bazaar.
  * Added Moby Duck: Collector's Edition, to Bazaar.
  * Added Flake the Fish as a rare drop when fishing on Jerry Island.
  * Added the a new hook - the Treasure Hook - to Junker Joel's shop.
    * With this hook, you can only catch treasure or, if you don't roll treasure, regular drops.
