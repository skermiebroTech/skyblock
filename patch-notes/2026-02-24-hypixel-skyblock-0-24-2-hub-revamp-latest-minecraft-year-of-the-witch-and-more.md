**►** **Year of the Witch  
  
**_NOTE_ : The first Year of the Witch will be during SkyBlock Year 476, which is a little over 4 days from the posting of this thread (2/24).****  
  


![2026-02-23_20.01.27.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/2026-02-23_20-01-27-png.3546363/)

  
Joining annual celebrations for Pigs an _d_ Seals comes the Year of the Witch! Once every 12 SkyBlock Years each mob type will have a rare chance of dropping a special stew ingredient. Gather a stack of an ingredient to brew a special stew which grants Alchemy XP and some Magic Find towards that Mob Type.  
  
You can also talk to Agnes for access to the Year of the Witch shop. There's a new talisman, two new Mixins, a sack to hold all of your stew ingredients, and a Witch Pet!

**►** **Frozen Corpse RNG Meter**  
With this update we've introduced an RNG Meter for particularly unlucky players who are looting Frozen Corpses.  
  


#### **➥ RNG Meter**​

![frozen_corpses_rng_meter.png](https://hypixel.net/attachments/frozen_corpses_rng_meter-png.3544982/)

  
Looting Frozen Corpses will give various amounts of RNG Meter XP:  


  * Lapis Corpses: 500 XP
  * Umber/Tungsten Corpses: 2,500 XP
  * Vanguard Corpses: 25,000 XP

Like other RNG Meters in the game, you can select a drop through `/rng` and you'll be guaranteed to drop it in the next Frozen Corpse you open once you hit the required amount of XP. We're granting 10% retroactive RNG Meter XP to all players with this update.

Grandpa Wolf can be found at -56, 123, 332 in the Glacite Tunnels. If you loot enough Frozen Corpses, he'll reward you for your time in the form of several useful perks and a new talisman line. **►** **QOL Changes**  
Today we have a number of high-impact quality-of-life changes to several areas of the game.  
  


#### **➥ Combat**​

  * Adjusted the unlock order of the Kuudra Pet's perks. The new order can be found below:
    * COMMON -> Wither Bait
    * UNCOMMON -> Trophy Bait
    * RARE -> Crimson
    * EPIC -> Kuudra Fortune
    * LEGENDARY -> Kuudra Specialist
      * **[!] Designer Note:** A key goal here is for the "Trophy Bait" perk to be more accessible, since +20 Trophy Chance is a massive amount to be locked behind the Epic tier of the pet.
  * Adjusted the rarity of the Wolf Talisman from COMMON -> UNCOMMON.
  * Adjusted the purchase requirements for the Blood God Crest:
    * Can now be purchased infinitely instead of once per profile.
    * Now capped at +7 Strength.
  * Rogue Swords can now be purchased at the Weaponsmith



#### **➥ Dungeoneering**​

  * Added the Spirit Mask to the Master Mode Floor 4 drop table and removed it from the Dark Auction.
    * Can only drop from the Obsidian Chest, costs 15m to open.
    * Has 4x less weight than a Spirit Sword.
  * Added a chat message that displays whenever you level up a Boss Collection.
  * Adjusted Golden Heads and Diamond Heads to now be purchased from Ophelia upon being unlocked.
    * **[!] Designer Note:** With this change, Diamond Heads now carry over upgrades from Golden Heads.
  * Added 250 Gold Essence to Boss Collection tiers where Golden Heads were previously awarded.
  * Added 1,000 Diamond Essence to Boss Collection tiers where Diamond Heads were previously awarded
  * Adjusted the default dungeons class from Archer -> Berserk.
  * Removed the ability to activate Berserk's Throwing Axe by sneaking while in The Catacombs.



#### **➥ Fishing**​

  * Added a Golden Fish Tablist Widget to the Crimson Isle.
  * Added a Seal Pet to Lukas the Aquarist's shop during the Year of the Seal!
  * Adjusted the speed of earlier-tier Fishing Minions to ensure the speed increase was more gradual between tiers.
  * Adjusted Fishing Baits to only be consumed upon successful catch.
  * Adjusted Sea Creatures to no longer attack players aside from the spawner unless provoked.
  * Removed the mechanic that would cause you to catch nothing if you didn't move for a while when fishing.



#### **➥ Miscellaneous**​

  * Adjusted the Pig Pet, giving it a new set of perks.
