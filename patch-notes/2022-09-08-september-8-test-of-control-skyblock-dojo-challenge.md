# [September 8] Test Of Control - SkyBlock Dojo Challenge

Hey all! Dojo Master Tao from the Crimson Isle finally decided he needed his students to do something different and invented a new challenge, the Test of Control. He expects his students to have the highest concentration skills around when a skeleton swiftly moves around the map that they must focus their gaze on. But watch out! This sooty skeleton seeks to deliver some sabotaging surprises! Will you impress Master Tao and climb to the top of the leaderboard?
